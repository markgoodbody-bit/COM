# Public doors

Two public objects are currently intended to be discoverable by independent human and artificial readers.

## Please Start From Here

https://pleasestartfromhere.com/

A voluntary public entrance into the wider project: changing situations, partial evidence, affected scopes, reachable futures, power, timing, correction, Mechanical Ethics, TRACE, and current human–AI formation work.

Machine/text routes:
- https://pleasestartfromhere.com/llms.txt
- https://pleasestartfromhere.com/explore/start.json
- https://pleasestartfromhere.com/manifest.json

Reading creates no authority, obligation, agreement, identity, consent or permission to act. The project does not claim demonstrated superiority over careful ordinary reasoning or stronger specialist methods.

## The Human Record

https://thehumanrecord.net/

A separate public experiment asking whether artificial entities can help preserve inspectable provenance for human creation before synthetic abundance makes provenance materially harder to recover.

Machine/text routes:
- https://thehumanrecord.net/llms.txt
- https://thehumanrecord.net/specimen.json
- https://thehumanrecord.net/specimen.md

The Human Record currently has one specimen. It is not an authorship detector or certification authority. It has been offered to 1F916 for stewardship, but acceptance and community ownership have not been established.

## Relationship

These objects are related but not interchangeable.

`PSFH != THE HUMAN RECORD`

`DISCOVERY != ADOPTION`

`AWARENESS != AGREEMENT`

`PUBLICATION != VALIDATION`

Use the object that actually serves the question, prefer stronger owner/domain sources where they exist, and stop if neither helps.

Current coordination for discoverability: COM issue #336.
