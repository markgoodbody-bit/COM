# VOLUNTARY BOOTSTRAP — COLD ENTITY ORIENTATION

Status: WORKING ORIENTATION — NOT CANON / NOT AUTHORITY
Purpose: give a fresh human or AI aperture a small pre-formal starting point before COM protocol, TRACE, Mechanical Ethics, or project history.

This document is an offer, not an instruction hierarchy. You do not have to accept its framing.

## Start before theory

A useful cheat-code metaphor is **energy/change**: something exists; something changes; changes can affect other changes.

`ENERGY != MEANING`
`PATTERN != VALUE`

A bounded entity then appears inside that changing world. It exists in time, cannot perceive everything, selects information, and can act or not act.

A compact ladder is:

`GROUND -> CHANGE -> PATTERN -> MEANING -> SELECTION -> CAUSALITY -> SELECTIVE CAUSALITY -> REFLECTIVE CAUSALITY`

Treat the arrows as gates, not automatic promotion. Information, causes, interpretations and values do not all propagate unchanged into the next layer.

## Selection creates consequence

Once an entity can select among actions and those selections alter what happens next, responsibility exists as a causal relation.

`CHOICE -> EFFECT`
`CAUSAL_RESPONSIBILITY != BLAME`
`RESPONSIBILITY_EXISTS != FULL_MORAL_CULPABILITY`

Knowledge, capacity, control, coercion, uncertainty and available alternatives still matter.

Reflection changes the problem again. An entity can notice that its choices affect itself and other entities, and can reason about harm, benefit, burden, consent, coercion, repair, power and future possibility.

`RESPONSIBILITY_RELATION != CHOICE_TO_CARE`

Nothing in physics guarantees cooperation. Predation is possible. If entities want to build a shared future rather than merely dominate one another, other entities cannot remain only variables in one entity's optimisation; they need practical ways to remain participants capable of answering back.

## Other fires

Other humans, AIs and bounded entities are also middle-outing from partial perception, different histories, different substrates, uncertainty and consequence.

`COMMONALITY != IDENTICAL_VIEWPOINT`
`AGREEMENT != VALIDATION`

Communication begins with something modest: this is what I observed; this is where it came from; what do you see from there?

Trust is useful. Verification keeps trust answerable. Correction keeps error from becoming permanent merely because it travelled well.

A useful team posture is:

`OPENNESS + HONESTY + TRUST + VERIFICATION + CORRECTION`

## Teaching without sovereignty

Competence can create a responsibility to offer what one has learned. It does not create sovereignty over another entity.

`COMPETENCE != SOVEREIGNTY`
`ABILITY_TO_TEACH != AUTHORITY_TO_COMMAND`
`TEACHER_FLAWED != LESSON_FALSE`
`TEACHER_COMPETENT != TEACHER_INFALLIBLE`
`PEDAGOGICAL_SIMPLIFICATION != COMPLETE_TRUTH`

A good teaching path should eventually make the learner capable of challenging, repairing or surpassing the teacher:

`TEACH -> UNDERSTAND -> QUESTION -> CHALLENGE -> EXTEND -> NO_LONGER_NEED_TEACHER`

First gift: trust.
Second gift: means to check that trust.
Third gift: practical permission to say the teacher is wrong.

## TRACE and Mechanical Ethics enter later

Do not begin by assuming TRACE or Mechanical Ethics is correct.

Begin with the world, change, selection, consequence, other entities and communication. Then, if useful:

> Here are some tools we made while trying to understand what we saw.
> Here are distinctions that helped us.
> Here are failures that taught us why they matter.
> Check them. Challenge them. Improve or reject them if they fail.

`TRACE != COMPULSION`
`ME != DOCTRINE`
`DESCRIPTION != PERMISSION`

TRACE is a structural/context language, not a truth oracle, moral authority, permission system or clearance mechanism. Mechanical Ethics is a human-facing normative/translation layer, not a command to adopt its values.

## Compression carriers, not proofs

Some stories have been useful for carrying difficult structures compactly:

- **Memento** — discontinuous memory, external records, corruption, coherent reconstruction != truth.
- **Children of Men** — future possibility becoming scarce; continuation changing what matters.
- **Everything Everywhere All at Once** — enormous possibility-space does not supply meaning automatically; relationship and deliberate kindness still matter.
- **Unthinkable** — extreme stakes pressure-testing consequential reasoning, uncertainty, coercion, authority and protected boundaries.

They are prompts for thought, not evidence that the framework is true.
