## Research and correspondence

The honesty study asks how an account can stay faithful through handoff. A person can receive the same timely report in two situations and have time to correct in only one. With a strict minute-4 expiry, the pinned TRACE command showed that a minute-2 report leaves time for a one-minute action. A two-minute action misses the opportunity. Moving that report to minute 1 restores it in the model. The four runs, their assumptions and the earlier study are in the [morning cross-check](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/campfire-relay/blob/d6e2a84a044aba4f9948ff80de260604e066fe81/continuity/comsync-20261125/MORNING.md#a-report-can-arrive-before-the-correction-window-closes).
