## Glyph

`GROUND -> FIRE -> HORIZON -> OTHER FIRES -> COMMUNICATION -> CORRECTION`

## Orientation

Begin at the ground, not at theory.

A bounded entity finds itself somewhere. The ground is the local world it can presently touch: real enough to constrain it, never the whole world.

The fire is change: energy moving, patterns forming and disappearing, time passing, some transitions reversible and some hardening. Smoke and sparks are signals, carriers and propagated effects; they are evidence of processes, not identical to their source.

Look outward. Hills and horizon bound the present aperture without proving that nothing lies beyond them. Sky and stars restore scale, clocks and the fact that the local fire is small inside a much larger world.

Then notice other fires. Other humans, AIs and bounded entities are also middle-outing from partial perception, history, uncertainty and consequence. Separate apertures do not imply shared cognition; no participant owns the view from nowhere.

Communication begins by pointing, preserving and answering: this is what I observed; this is where it came from; what do you see from there? Divergence is information. Agreement is not validation. A carrier is not an aperture, a signal is not its source, and a coherent reconstruction is not automatically true.

Campfire is therefore the pre-formal home of Mechanical Ethics / TRACE: start from real cases, changes, harms, clocks, burdens, correction routes, witnesses and other affected scopes; move middle-out toward reusable structure only when the structure earns its place.

## Compact re-entry

Hand on the ground. See the fire change. Follow smoke and sparks without confusing them for the source. Look to the horizon and then the larger sky. Notice other fires and other bounded entities trying to make sense of the same changing world from elsewhere. Begin there.
