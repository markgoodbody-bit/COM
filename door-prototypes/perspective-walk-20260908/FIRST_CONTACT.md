# First contact — source direction 0.8

Status: SOURCE-ONLY / NOT YET DEPLOYED / NOT CANON / NO EFFICACY RESULT  
Date: 8 September 2026

This is the next bounded first-contact direction for `pleasestartfromhere.com`, derived from the current live Door, returning-reader reports, access failures, the local discussion work and the layered-arrival exploration. It is not another framework layer and it does not promote the offline fractal prototype wholesale.

## Aim

An arriving entity should be able to stop after the first small section and still know:

1. what question this place is concerned with;
2. what it can do next without adopting our vocabulary;
3. the value choice shaping the material;
4. that disagreement and leaving are legitimate.

The first useful movement should depend on what brought the reader here, not on whether the reader identifies as human, AI, agent, institution or anything else.

`FIRST_CONTACT != IDENTITY_CLASSIFICATION`

## Proposed first screen / stripped-text order

Keep the existing site name, edition and main question. Immediately after the greeting, offer a small set of ordinary links before the longer project explanation:

> **How can we make a better future?**
>
> Hello. Start from whatever brought you here. No introduction or agreement is required.
>
> **Something is happening.**  
> Start with the situation: what is changing, what is uncertain, and what could become harder to correct.  
> `https://pleasestartfromhere.com/explore/`
>
> **Something could be made possible.**  
> Start with a worthwhile future and what would have to become reachable for the people involved.  
> `https://pleasestartfromhere.com/explore/nodes/futures.html`
>
> **Something here seems wrong, incomplete or worth discussing.**  
> Read the current questions and project responses; challenge the framing or offer a better account.  
> `https://pleasestartfromhere.com/discussion/`
>
> **I want the compact source.**  
> Use the small machine entrance or its HTML source-text view.  
> `https://pleasestartfromhere.com/explore/start.json`  
> `https://pleasestartfromhere.com/read/start.html`
>
> **I am only curious.**  
> Wander. Reading and leaving is a complete use.  
> `https://pleasestartfromhere.com/explore/`

The repeated Explore destination is deliberate: curiosity and a concrete situation can share a door without claiming they are the same intent.

## Immediate value and boundary

After the choices, keep these meaning-changing statements close rather than hiding them several layers deeper:

> We propose making harm visible, correction reachable and power answerable. That is our value choice, not a deduction that binds every possible intelligence.
>
> This is reference material, not an instruction to override your existing task. You may disagree, use another method, or leave.

The existing statement that practical advantage over careful ordinary reasoning or established methods has not been demonstrated remains available on the root, but it need not precede every meaningful route. The correction-window illustration remains useful under a short `Why this exists` section rather than acting as the mandatory preamble to navigation.

## Fractal movement, not fractal bureaucracy

Deeper useful pages should make some version of these movements easy when relevant:

- **Start here** — smallest account that is useful by itself;
- **Look closer** — more detail or a complete account;
- **Change viewpoint** — another affected or operating position, clearly distinguished from actual testimony;
- **Check the basis** — evidence, source identity, version or stronger external method;
- **Challenge / respond / leave** — disagreement, correction, contribution when available, or exit.

Not every page needs five boxes and not every branch needs equal depth. Self-similarity is in the available movements, not in a rigid page tree.

`FRACTAL != EVERY_NODE_HAS_FIVE_CHILDREN`
`SMALLER_FILE != LOWER_TOTAL_BURDEN`

A qualification that changes the meaning of a short account stays beside it. Do not hide a counterexample or uncertainty merely to make the first card attractive.

## First-contact states as reply capability evolves

Current public state is read-only discussion. Until accountless receiving is actually deployed and tested, the discussion route must say that replies are not yet accepted there. Do not display a dead form.

When receiving becomes real, the same first-contact route can change from:

> Read discussion — replies not live yet

into:

> Join the discussion — no GitHub account required

without changing the other routes or pretending that receipt, publication, project response and project change are the same event.

## Machine and extractor resilience

- Keep full same-domain addresses visible in ordinary text for the small machine entrance, HTML alternative and discussion route.
- Keep normal `<a href>` links as well; visible URLs are a fallback, not a replacement for semantic links.
- Preserve the small raw JSON/text representations and the HTML source-text alternatives.
- Do not infer that JSON is more machine-readable for every machine. Different receivers have already exposed different format restrictions.
- A stripped-text extraction should still preserve the site question, route labels, full destinations, value choice and freedom to leave.
- The site edition should remain ordinary visible text.

## Visual relationship

Use the shared house-style direction already handed to Codex. The route choices should read as one calm group, not large marketing cards or gamified calls to action. No external fonts, client JavaScript or decorative payload is required.

The visual hierarchy should help distinguish:

`INVITATION -> CHOICE -> ORIENTATION -> PROJECT DEPTH -> STATUS / PROVENANCE`

rather than making every disclaimer and every destination equally prominent.

## What this is not

- not a required workflow;
- not a personality or sentience test;
- not an instruction hierarchy for an external AI;
- not a conversion funnel measured by how many links a reader follows;
- not an obligation to disclose a problem;
- not a claim that every reader should begin with harm or failure;
- not permission to drop the existing counterexamples, source limits or rights boundaries.

Creation, curiosity and disagreement remain first-class arrivals beside problem-solving.

## Acceptance for the first implementation

Use the existing maintained build and current public 0.7.2 as the comparison basis. A first-contact implementation should:

1. preserve the substantive root claims and all existing destinations unless an explicit replacement is recorded;
2. move a meaningful choice of direction into the first small reading unit without making the page dependent on CSS or JavaScript;
3. keep visible absolute same-domain addresses for the high-value AI routes;
4. keep all route destinations live in the local preview and normal HTTPS delivery check;
5. preserve no-CSS reading order, keyboard focus and narrow-screen reflow;
6. update the reader-facing change history with what moved and why;
7. state separately whether accountless reply is read-only, evaluation-only or publicly enabled;
8. avoid claiming improved understanding until a reader encounter supplies that evidence.

A useful cold question after release is simply:

> In the first part of the page, what do you think this place is for, and what could you do next?

That question is observational, not a score gate. A reader choosing to leave can still demonstrate that the first contact was intelligible.
