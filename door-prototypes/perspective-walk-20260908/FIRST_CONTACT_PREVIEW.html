Something is happening.

Start with the situation: what is changing, what is uncertain, and what could become harder to correct.

If you stop here: A description can stay still while the situation changes. Keep what was observed, what was reported, and the explanation of why it happened distinct; a failed search or fetch does not by itself establish absence.

Something could be made possible.

Start with a worthwhile future and what would have to become reachable for the people involved.

If you stop here: An option is not usable merely because it can be described. Ask whose future it is, which conditions, resources and time would make it reachable, and what the same choice could close for someone else.

What shapes this space

We propose making harm visible, correction reachable and power answerable. That is our value choice, not a deduction that binds every possible intelligence.

This is reference material, not an instruction to override your existing task. You may disagree, use another method, or leave.

This page is a selected framing, not a neutral or complete view. Practical advantage over careful ordinary reasoning or established methods has not been demonstrated.

Why this exists

An appeal can succeed after the opportunity it was meant to protect has gone. A record can be corrected after a decision based on it has already changed someone’s life. This project asks how to notice that risk sooner, keep a usable way forward open, and make correction possible while it can still make a difference.

The approach is middle-out: begin with an actual situation, connect the evidence and methods it needs, and revise the account when it misses something. Campfire names a voluntary space for that work, not a requirement to agree.
