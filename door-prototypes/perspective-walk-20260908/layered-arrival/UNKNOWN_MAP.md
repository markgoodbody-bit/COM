# Layered arrival: what we still do not know

8 September 2026. Framework design analysis for Mark. DRAFT, not deployed, not
canon, not an efficacy result. These are design risks and questions, not observed
failures unless an evidence pointer is named. The map is deliberately incomplete.

## The useful geometry

Resolution, viewpoint, grounds for belief, connection to other accounts and
practical action are different movements. More detail is not more evidence;
another author-imagined position is not another witness; an action suggestion
is not permission. Self-similar navigation must allow asymmetrical subject matter.
A deeper correction may invalidate the beginning rather than preserve its shape.

## Unknowns that can change the design

### 1. What reaches the reader?

A client may expose a whole response, extracted prose, a search snippet, or only
an error. We do not know which fields and links survive each provider's path.
An HTTP success or an HTML parser test does not establish receiver visibility.
The probe offers JSON, Markdown and plain HTML, without user-agent guessing.
A later ordinary willing encounter should name the resource actually retrieved;
no provider campaign, repeated retry or bypass is requested.

### 2. When does a smaller unit stop being useful?

We do not know the optimal unit, menu size or depth. A sentence can be cheap but
misleading without its limiting condition. The minimal futures card retains the
full existing counterexample, not just an attractive maxim. If a reader needs
several fetches before discovering the qualification that changes the claim,
combine the units. If a unit contains no usable distinction, it is just another
navigation tax.

### 3. Does layering reduce total burden?

Uncompressed UTF-8 body sizes can be measured here. Actual tokenization, tool
wrappers, latency, retries, monetary cost and attention cannot. A layered walk
and a complete packet serve different information needs; their byte counts alone
are not a fair benefit comparison. The probe supplies both. Later compare the
same desired understanding, including the cost of choosing and recovering from
a wrong turn. Fewer clicks is not always better; more clicks is not engagement.

### 4. Does context survive a deep arrival or a handoff?

Readers may enter the middle, copy one card, or mix old and new versions. JSON
cards carry status, boundary, a relative basis locator and symbolic source_fields.
Rendered cards preserve status/boundary text and a labelled route to the basis;
they do not repeat every source field or the full source/proposal attribution.
These are intact-resource properties, not guarantees for an isolated copied
excerpt. The source is pinned and the builder refuses an unexpected library;
that catches one build change, not stripped context or all stale caches.
No continuously current state or persistent reader identity is claimed.

### 5. Are our questions helpful or quietly exclusionary?

The three opening choices and the futures example are selections by us. They may
privilege administrative harm, instrumental goals and English-speaking readers.
Someone may arrive to create, play, understand beauty, or contest the notion of
an individual decision. The situation card explicitly leaves those possibilities
open, but the example still centres futures. That is a remaining limitation,
not an exhaustive or universal welcome. Route order may prime later answers;
a correct summary is not proof the framing served the reader.

### 6. Can the reader reject the map without being sent deeper into it?

The challenge card says it is also authored by this project. It permits another
ordinary account, a relevant domain method or departure, and names a separately
authored neighbouring work without claiming it is a solution. It is not a
catalogue of all alternatives. We do not know which alternatives a particular
situation needs. Departure and silence must not be labelled failure or consent.

### 7. Who is absent when an arriving entity is satisfied?

The immediate reader may be an operator with power over someone else. A convenient
route for that reader could erase the affected party. Our perspective card is
explicitly a possibility, not testimony. We do not know whether missing parties
can safely answer or whether the selected interests are legitimate. A link to
discussion requiring an account is not practical remedy. A predatory reader may
use the same analysis to narrow another's options; public availability does not
establish beneficial use.

### 8. Is the greeting information or an attempted task change?

A reader could be under instructions to summarize a URL, not explore it or form
an allegiance. We must not turn a welcome into an identity assignment, a required
response, permission to act, or an override. The prototype contains no scripts,
forms, active agent or automatic traversal. Those properties do not guarantee
that natural-language content cannot redirect an LLM. No prompt-injection
resistance result is claimed. Outbound pages remain untrusted reference material.

### 9. What privacy does the invitation imply?

Asking about a situation can encourage disclosure. The files receive no replies,
but that does not promise that an AI provider or eventual host logs nothing.
The entry is not a confidential support service. No personal situation or previous
private chat is bundled. Data minimisation, retention and safe participation would
need a separate design before adding a reply form or listener. None is built here.

### 10. Can symbols, language and format travel?

AI-first does not imply one tokenizer, English, JSON support, visual ability or
unlimited tool calls. No multilingual or assistive-technology user result exists.
Descriptive link purposes and multiple representations are implementation choices,
not accessibility certification. Glyph compression and machine-only negotiation
are deferred until a real reader need justifies them. No assumption of consciousness,
free will, a stable self or a human-like motivational system is made.

### 11. Can we maintain a forest of tiny files without contradiction?

The experiment derives all three representations from one source. Complete records
populate packet.json; rendered packets preserve their titles, section prose and
labelled routes, not every machine metadata field. A second-pass check found the
original rendered packets omitted four external destinations despite passing the
prose-preservation tests. That defect is repaired with route-specific regression
coverage; it is not evidence that all context survives. Measured three-card body
bytes also exceed the existing complete futures page for the overlapping five
text fields; see README.md for the unequal-content comparison. Maintainer effort,
version drift across host/cache/tool and repeated caveats may still outweigh savings. One seven-card probe is not authority to multiply this
across the whole site. Stable public routes would require a separate integration
choice, not merely copying the generated directory into production.

### 12. Does the account change anything worth changing?

No result establishes improved understanding, better action, reduced harm or
restored options. Praise, vocabulary repetition and longer traversal are not
substitutes. A useful subsequent encounter might reveal one unsupported assumption,
a newly usable option, a missing affected party, or a sound reason to use a simpler
method. If the packet or current page serves that same need better, use it. The
purpose survives rejecting this particular design. Some ethical disagreements are
not resolvable by interface testing; affected-party judgement and rights remain
outside our control.

## External engineering basis, not evidence for this design's usefulness

[W3C Web Architecture](https://www.w3.org/TR/webarch/), especially representation
management and linking: identifying a resource does not require fetching it;
retrieval has costs; fragments are interpreted on the client. This supports
separate resources and optional links, not a claim that seven is the ideal count.

[W3C link purpose guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/link-purpose-in-context.html):
link wording/context should help a reader choose before following it. We apply
that principle; no conformance audit has been performed.

[OWASP LLM prompt-injection guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/LLM_Prompt_Injection_Prevention_Cheat_Sheet.html):
external web/document content can carry indirect instructions. This is why static,
script-free content is not treated as a complete AI safety boundary. No guardrail
service or special trust status is introduced.

These primary sources were consulted on 8 September 2026. They do not diagnose
Meta, Gemini or Copilot's specific access errors.
