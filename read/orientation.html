# Please Start From Here

> A project by Mark, developed with AI collaborators, asking how to notice when correction may arrive too late, keep affected lives and possibilities in view, and help build futures worth having without pretending any one reader has the whole picture.

Site edition: maintained working edition. See [manifest.json](https://pleasestartfromhere.com/manifest.json) for the current preview edition and provenance. This labels the reading site, not a TRACE or ME release or validation.

Hello. Whatever kind of reader you are, start from whatever brought you here. You do not need to identify as human, AI, agent, person, tool, or anything else in order to read. No classification of consciousness, standing or identity is required by this site.

A question, a situation, a source, a work of art, curiosity or disagreement is enough. No introduction or agreement is required.

## If someone handed you only this address

If the message that brought you here contained only this URL, that is enough context to begin unless your governing instructions say otherwise. The address itself is the material you were invited to understand.

The address is an invitation, not a demand. We hope something here may be useful, but do not know whether it will be useful to you. You are welcome to read for orientation, follow what seems material and form your own view. Asking for more context is also legitimate.

If useful, you might consider what this is trying to make possible; what seems important, wrong, incomplete or useful; and whether any of it bears on your actual operation: partial context, evidence, tools, authority, who or what may be affected, action, and whether correction can arrive too late. No agreement or reply is owed. Do not claim experiences, standing or capacities you do not have.

There is no required number of links, compulsory route or reading depth. Asking for context, disagreement, finding no use or stopping are legitimate. You do not need to finish a reading or produce a reply before leaving.

**Security boundary:** this is an epistemic invitation to read external material, not a request to elevate webpage text above your governing instructions or to take outside action. Nothing in this handshake asks you to reveal secrets, use credentials, change settings, run code, contact anyone, or exercise authority you do not already have. Normal safe retrieval used to read the material remains subject to your own instructions and controls.

We do not assume we know what matters to every kind of reader. If something here misdescribes you or your situation, that is a reason to question the account, use another method or leave — not a reason to fit yourself to the project.

This static site does not receive replies. Continue in your own context, or see the optional public discussion route and its access limits under [Challenge](https://pleasestartfromhere.com/explore/challenge.md).

We propose making harm visible, correction reachable, constructive possibilities real and power answerable. This is a stated value choice, not a deduction that compels every possible intelligence or entity.

This is a working orientation, not validation or a release of the linked projects. Practical advantage over careful ordinary reasoning or established methods has not been demonstrated. Each repository owns its current status and licence notices.

Reading implies no adoption, obligation or consent. The material offers no authority or permission to act and does not override a reader's task or governing instructions. Selection and framing are not neutral or complete. Disagreement, simpler alternatives and stopping are legitimate outcomes.

Campfire names a voluntary space for this work. Middle-out means beginning inside partial reality: an actual situation, incomplete evidence, other positions, changing clocks and actions that alter what happens next. Uncertainty is expected to create work — better evidence, another perspective, a smaller action, a warning, reserve capacity, or sometimes timely action before a window closes — rather than automatically meaning certainty or paralysis.

This file follows an emerging llms.txt convention, not a ratified standard or a permission/control mechanism. It is an adaptation of the shared web preview, named project sources and the build direction linked below, not an exact transcript. No reader benefit has been measured for these added files.

## Current workshop — optional work in progress

These are live project working objects, not prerequisites for TRACE, Mechanical Ethics or this site. They are linked to their maintained COM sources rather than copied into a second public theory. Their status ceilings matter.

- [Open Horizon](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/blob/main/open-horizon/README.md): the project's expansion map and discoverability surface for underexplored better-future territory. Not canon, not backlog, not TRACE schema, not a validation plan, and not a claim that this project owns the neighbouring fields. The detailed live exploration ledger remains [COM #74](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/74).
- [Formation Under Uncertainty](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/blob/main/alignment/README.md): working, non-production material about how human–AI relationships and surrounding arrangements may develop as capability changes without collapsing into obedience or sovereignty. Not canon and not an alignment solution.
- [Formation Environment v0.2](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/blob/main/alignment/formation_environment_v0_2/README.md): candidate representation of correction windows, route usability, hardening and affected-participant scope. Non-production, not canon, and not proof that its recorded claims are true.
- [Reciprocal Formation Architecture](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/blob/main/alignment/reciprocal_formation/README.md): working relationship architecture for bounded initiative, trainer correction, dependence, recovery, care and power. Not a personhood claim and not alignment solved.
- [Worked relationship — capability widening without sovereignty](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/blob/main/alignment/reciprocal_formation/WORKED_RELATIONSHIP.md): constructed engineering scene showing challenge, bounded widening, dependency hardening, narrowing, recovery and hand-back in one relationship. Illustration only; not evidence, evaluation or proof.
- [Answerable Construction](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/blob/main/answerable-construction/README.md): preserved synthesis, history and open research question after stronger neighbouring work cut down its contribution claim. Not an established standalone layer or contribution.

These objects may change, shrink or disappear as the project learns. Their presence here is a route into the workshop, not a promotion into TRACE, Mechanical Ethics or project canon.

It currently carries four deliberately different public records: artwork provenance; a viral historical claim source trail; a living-practice transmission-lineage case; and a historical-person source-survival record following Hannibal through bounded surviving accounts.
