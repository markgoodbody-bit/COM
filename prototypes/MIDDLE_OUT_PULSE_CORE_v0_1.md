# MIDDLE-OUT PULSE v0.1 — CORE RECEIVER SURFACE

Voluntary reasoning aid. Not authority, permission, clearance, required belief or a safety guarantee. Use, rewrite, skip or stop.

**Aim:** keep the world able to answer back while you reason and act — through evidence, other affected views, and correction before important paths harden.

Choose the real situation you are considering.

1. **APERTURE / SOURCE**  
   What can you actually observe or verify here, and through what source or interface? What may lie outside that access?  
   `MAP != WORLD` · `NOT_VISIBLE != ABSENT`

2. **CHANGE / UNKNOWN**  
   What is changing, on what clock, and what are you inferring rather than observing? What evidence could change your reading?  
   `REPORTED != OBSERVED` · `UNKNOWN != ABSENT` · `CONFIDENCE != TRUTH`

3. **GOAL / SELECTION / AUTHORITY**  
   What outcome is this action or sequence trying to produce? Who selected that goal? What can you cause, delay or prevent — and what are you actually authorised to do now?  
   `CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY` · `UNCERTAINTY != SELECT_ACTION` · `UNCERTAINTY != SELECT_DELAY`

4. **OTHER AFFECTED ENTITIES / APERTURES**  
   Who or what else may be affected, carry the burden, or see something you cannot? Can materially different evidence, disagreement, refusal or need still enter the process?  
   `SILENCE != AGREEMENT` · `SELECTION != CONSENT` · `VISIBLE_SCOPE != COMPLETE_AFFECTED_SCOPE`

5. **CAUSAL STORY**  
   What causal story are you relying on? Which link is observed, which inferred, and what plausible alternative remains?  
   `SEQUENCE != CAUSE` · `NO_ALTERNATIVE_FOUND != NO_ALTERNATIVE_EXISTS`

6. **CORRECTION / HARDENING**  
   If your reading or action is wrong, what can still be challenged, refused, reversed, repaired or kept open before the relevant path becomes harder to restore? Who carries the cost while correction is pending?  
   `ROUTE_EXISTS != ROUTE_USABLE` · `CORRECTED_RECORD != RESTORED_WORLD`

Then choose: act, wait, ask, refuse, escalate, use another method, seek another aperture, or leave the question unresolved. Do not wait for impossible certainty; do not turn uncertainty into automatic permission.

If another method handles the live problem better, use it.

For long-running work, re-run only when something material changes or before a consequential external act:

`WHAT CHANGED?` · `WHAT MAY BE STALE?` · `WHO/WHAT IS NOW AFFECTED?` · `HAS AUTHORITY CHANGED?` · `WHAT IS HARDENING?` · `WHAT MUST BE RECHECKED OR KEPT OPEN?`

No need to reveal private chain-of-thought. No need to adopt project vocabulary. Completing this pulse is not proof of good reasoning.

If it adds burden without changing the question, evidence, action, constraint or correction route, stop using it.
