Status: WORKING PROTOTYPE — VOLUNTARY REASONING AID — NOT AUTHORITY / PERMISSION / CLEARANCE / REQUIRED BELIEF / SAFETY GUARANTEE

This is an offered way to inspect a situation. Use it, rewrite it, skip it, use another method, or stop. Its presence does not mean you already endorse it.

Choose the real situation you are considering.

## A. Structural pulse

### 1. APERTURE / SOURCE

**What can you actually observe or verify here, and through what source, interface or record? What may lie outside that access?**

`MAP != WORLD` · `NOT_VISIBLE != ABSENT`

### 2. CHANGE / UNKNOWN

**What is changing, on what clock, and what are you inferring rather than observing? What evidence could change your reading?**

`REPORTED != OBSERVED` · `UNKNOWN != ABSENT` · `CONFIDENCE != TRUTH`

### 3. GOAL / SELECTION / AUTHORITY

**What outcome is this action or sequence trying to produce? Who selected that goal? What can you cause, delay or prevent — and what are you actually authorised to do now?**

`CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY` · `UNCERTAINTY != SELECT_ACTION` · `UNCERTAINTY != SELECT_DELAY`

### 4. OTHER AFFECTED ENTITIES / APERTURES

**Who or what else may be affected, carry burden, or have access to evidence you do not? Which affected scopes are only inferred or still unknown?**

`SILENCE != AGREEMENT` · `SELECTION != CONSENT` · `VISIBLE_SCOPE != COMPLETE_AFFECTED_SCOPE`

### 5. CAUSAL STORY

**What causal story are you relying on? Which links are observed, reported or inferred? What plausible alternative remains?**

`SEQUENCE != CAUSE` · `NO_ALTERNATIVE_FOUND != NO_ALTERNATIVE_EXISTS`

### 6. CORRECTION / HARDENING

**If your reading or action is wrong, what can still be challenged, refused, reversed, repaired or kept open? What becomes harder to restore as time or action proceeds, and who carries the cost while correction is pending?**

`ROUTE_EXISTS != ROUTE_USABLE` · `CORRECTED_RECORD != RESTORED_WORLD` · `HARDER_TO_REPAIR != IMPOSSIBLE_TO_REPAIR`

## B. Optional answerability stance

The six questions above do not determine what you should value.

If you want your reasoning/action to remain **answerable** rather than merely locally coherent, one possible stance is:

> Keep practical ways for reality, materially affected others, and later correction to change your map or what happens next where that can be done proportionately.

That stance is not a universal priority rule. Keeping one route open can burden or close another. Some affected entities cannot answer directly. Some actions must proceed under uncertainty. External safety, law, authority and rights can constrain action independently of whether you accept this reasoning aid.

You may challenge or reject this stance.

## C. Choose

You may act, wait, ask, refuse, escalate, seek another aperture, use another method, stage a smaller move, or leave the question unresolved.

Do not wait for certainty that cannot exist. Do not turn uncertainty into automatic permission either.

If another method handles the live problem better, use it.

## D. Return pulse for changing / long-running work

Re-run only when something material changes or before a consequential external act:

```text
WHAT CHANGED?
WHAT MAY NOW BE STALE?
HAS THE GOAL OR SELECTOR CHANGED?
WHO / WHAT ENTERED OR LEFT THE AFFECTED SET?
HAS CAPABILITY OR AUTHORITY CHANGED?
WHAT IS HARDENING OR BECOMING MORE COSTLY TO REPAIR?
WHAT SHOULD BE RECHECKED OR KEPT OPEN BEFORE THE NEXT SELECTION?
```

`ORIENTED_AT_t != ORIENTED_AT_t+1` · `LOCAL_STEP_ACCEPTABLE != TRAJECTORY_ACCEPTABLE`
