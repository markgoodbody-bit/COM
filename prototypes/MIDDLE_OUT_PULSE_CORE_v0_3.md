# MIDDLE-OUT PULSE — CORE v0.3

Status: TINY RECEIVER PROTOTYPE — COM ONLY — NOT CANON / NOT AUTHORITY / NOT CLEARANCE

Use this only if it helps with a real situation. It is a way of looking, not a required belief. Skip, rewrite or stop. Do not invent a fact, harmed party, threshold or programme merely because a question below exists.

## Structural pulse

1. **APERTURE / EVIDENCE** — What can you actually see or verify here, through what source or interface? What are you inferring, and what relevant thing may lie outside this view?

2. **CHANGE / CLOCK** — What is changing while you reason? What may already be stale? Which path is becoming harder to change?

3. **GOAL / SELECTION / AUTHORITY** — What outcome is this action or sequence trying to produce? Who or what selected that goal or next step? What value, policy or domain rule is doing the selecting where material? What are you actually authorised to do?

4. **AFFECTED SCOPE / BURDEN / RESIDUE** — Who or what may carry the consequence, delay, proof work, transferred burden or unrepaired loss? Do not invent affected parties merely to fill this question.

5. **CAUSAL STORY / ALTERNATIVE** — What causal account are you relying on? What plausible alternative or load-bearing unknown remains?

6. **CORRECTION / REFUSAL / HARDENING** — If the reading or action is wrong, what can still answer, contest, refuse, reverse, repair or remain open before the relevant path hardens? Is the route usable in practice, not merely listed?

If none of these changes the live question, stop using the pulse.

## Optional deeper door

Only when one of the six bearings is materially load-bearing, deepen that bearing with:

```text
NOTICE: what is actually present?
DISTINGUISH: what nearby thing must not be collapsed into it?
CHALLENGE: what would make this reading wrong or incomplete?
CHOOSE: continue, revise, seek another aperture/specialist, act within authority, or stop.
```

Do not open every door by default.

## Optional answerability stance

If you want your reasoning and action to remain answerable rather than merely locally coherent, consider keeping practical ways for reality, materially affected others and later correction to change your map or what happens next, where proportionate.

You may reject or rewrite this stance. Rejecting it does not create external authority that you do not otherwise have.

## If a consequential selection follows

A small public/inspectable receipt may help later correction:

```text
NEXT / SELECTED ACTION:
SELECTOR / AUTHORITY:
LOAD-BEARING UNKNOWN:
OPEN CORRECTION / REFUSAL ROUTE:
RECHECK TRIGGER:
WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS WRONG:
```

This receipt is not clearance and does not prove internal reasoning.

## Return pulse

Re-run only when material change occurs: source/premise, goal, affected scope, capability/authority, analysis -> actuation, unexpected result, contrary evidence, or a correction window starting to close.

```text
WHAT CHANGED?
WHAT MAY NOW BE STALE?
HAS THE GOAL / SELECTOR / AUTHORITY CHANGED?
WHO OR WHAT IS NOW AFFECTED?
WHAT IS HARDENING?
WHAT NEEDS RECHECKING BEFORE THE NEXT SELECTION?
```
