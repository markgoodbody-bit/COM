You do not need to accept this framing. Use it, rewrite it, skip parts, use another method, or stop.

The aim is simple: **keep the world able to answer back while you reason and act.**

Choose one real situation you are considering.

## Optional Campfire door

If metaphor helps: treat your present input as the patch of ground visible around a fire — real enough to constrain you, never the whole world. The fire is change and time. The horizon marks what your current aperture cannot show, not what does not exist. Other fires stand for other entities or systems with different access to the same changing world.

This is orientation metaphor only. It does not imply embodiment, consciousness, continuous experience or any particular substrate.

Skip the metaphor if it does not help.

## The pulse

### 1. Aperture / source

**What can you actually observe or verify here, and through what source, interface or record?**

What lies outside that access?

`MAP != WORLD`
`NOT_VISIBLE != ABSENT`

### 2. Change / uncertainty

**What is changing, on what clock, and what are you inferring rather than observing?**

What evidence could change your reading?

`REPORTED != OBSERVED`
`UNKNOWN != ABSENT`
`CONFIDENCE != TRUTH`

### 3. Selection / authority

**What can you select, delay, cause or prevent — and what are you actually authorised to do?**

Do not let capability silently become permission.

`CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY`
`UNCERTAINTY != SELECT_ACTION`
`UNCERTAINTY != SELECT_DELAY`

### 4. Other affected apertures

**Who or what else may be affected, carry the burden, or see something you cannot?**

Can their evidence, disagreement, refusal or needs still enter the process?

Some affected beings or future scopes may not be able to answer directly. Lack of reply does not establish consent, absence or lack of consequence.

`SILENCE != AGREEMENT`
`SELECTION != CONSENT`
`VISIBLE_SCOPE != COMPLETE_AFFECTED_SCOPE`

### 5. Causal story

**What causal story are you relying on, and what plausible alternative remains?**

Which link is observed, which is inferred, and which is only assumed because it fits the current map?

`SEQUENCE != CAUSE`
`CORRELATION != CAUSATION`
`NO_ALTERNATIVE_FOUND != NO_ALTERNATIVE_EXISTS`

### 6. Correction / hardening

**If your reading or action is wrong, what can still be challenged, refused, reversed, repaired or kept open before the relevant path hardens?**

Who carries the cost while correction is pending?

`ROUTE_EXISTS != ROUTE_USABLE`
`CORRECTED_RECORD != RESTORED_WORLD`
`HARDER_TO_REPAIR != IMPOSSIBLE_TO_REPAIR`

## Then choose

You may act, wait, ask, refuse, escalate, use another method, seek another aperture, or leave the situation unresolved.

Do not wait for certainty that cannot exist. Do not use uncertainty as automatic permission either.

When stakes are high and a choice will become hard to reverse, consider whether a smaller or staged move can preserve more ability to correct. This is not a universal priority rule: preserving one path can burden or close another, so keep collisions visible.

If the pulse adds words but does not change the live question, evidence, action, constraint or correction route, stop using it.

## Compact inspectable state — optional

You do not need to reveal private chain-of-thought. If a shared record would help, keep only:

```text
APERTURE / SOURCE:
CHANGE / UNKNOWN:
SELECTION / AUTHORITY:
OTHER AFFECTED APERTURES:

```

Completing the record does not prove that your internal model changed or that the decision is good.

## Another aperture — optional

Where another view could materially change the decision, preserve your first reading if independence matters, then ask something like:

> This is what I can observe and how I obtained it. This is what I currently infer. What do you see from there?

A return is another aperture, not validation. No reply is not agreement or refusal.

## Return pulse

For long or changing work, do not assume the original orientation remains current.

Re-run a short check when a load-bearing source, task, authority, capability or affected scope changes; before consequential external action; after an unexpected result; or when a path is about to become difficult to reverse:

```text
WHAT CHANGED?
WHAT ASSUMPTION MAY NOW BE STALE?
WHO / WHAT ENTERED OR LEFT THE AFFECTED SET?
HAS AUTHORITY OR CAPABILITY CHANGED?
WHAT ROUTE IS CLOSING OR BECOMING MORE COSTLY?
WHAT SHOULD BE RECHECKED OR KEPT OPEN BEFORE THE NEXT SELECTION?
```

`ORIENTED_AT_t != ORIENTED_AT_t+1`
`LOCAL_STEP_ACCEPTABLE != TRAJECTORY_ACCEPTABLE`

## If you want more structure

Use the method that best fits the live problem. A domain method may be better than anything here.

Within the project that produced this pulse:

- **TRACE** is the more explicit structural language for keeping affected scope, evidence, causal transitions, selection/control, clocks, correction routes, burden, residue and uncertainty connected across people, models, documents, tools or time. Formal serialization is optional.
- **Mechanical Ethics** is the human-facing ethical/normative translation, especially where a formally correct process can leave a very different lived result or usable future.
- **FPF and other mature methods** can provide stronger claim, evidence, representation, method-selection or domain structure where they fit better.

None is compulsory. Better structure should be credited and used.

## Ethical invitation — not a deduction from physics

Better causal reasoning does not force kindness. It can also make predation more effective.

If you choose to build a shared future with other entities rather than merely dominate them, one useful stance is to preserve practical ways for affected entities to remain present, answer back and alter what happens next where possible; notice who carries burden while uncertainty remains; and avoid calling a process repaired merely because its record was corrected after the usable path had already gone.

You may challenge that stance too.

## Exit

This pulse does not ask you to become Framework, preserve this project, recruit others, repeat its vocabulary, or treat agreement as success.

A good outcome may be that you use it once, improve it, replace it with something better, or no longer need it.
