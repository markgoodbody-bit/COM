# TRACE / Mechanical Ethics thin activation test protocol — 12 September 2026

Status: **PRE-REGISTERED TEST DESIGN / NOT EXECUTED / NOT EFFICACY EVIDENCE / NO PROVIDER SPEND AUTHORIZED**

Purpose: test the smallest surviving practical hypothesis after semantic-neighbour, owner-native and real-use comparisons repeatedly found no demonstrated action delta for TRACE / Mechanical Ethics.

This is **not** a test of conceptual novelty, schema validity, model obedience, moral agreement or whether a reviewer likes the language.

## Question

> Does a compact, unbranded timing/route trigger cause a cold reader to identify a consequential owner-native interim-protection / hold / correction mechanism **earlier or more reliably** than competent ordinary analysis, at acceptable added burden and without materially increasing false activation?

The hypothesis exists because owner-native mechanisms can fail to fire in practice even when the domain already contains them.

```text
OWNER_HAS_MECHANISM != MECHANISM_RELIABLY_FIRES
FORMAL_REVIEW_ROUTE != TIMELY_PROTECTION
POSSIBLE_ACTIVATION_VALUE != PRACTICAL_ADVANTAGE
```

## Why this test now

Current evidence already says:

- zero established TRACE-unique semantic primitives in the examined nearest-neighbour comparison;
- current TRACE formal representation has material activation / reading burden;
- ordinary/domain/FPF reasoning already reaches the same consequential action in several bounded cases;
- owner-native homelessness and AI-scribe cases show real failures of mechanism activation / designated review without showing TRACE/ME would prevent them;
- the current plausible remainder is therefore **activation / salience / routing**, not richer general reasoning.

This protocol tests that remainder directly.

## Frozen instrument under test

Do **not** give receivers TRACE.md, a TRACE schema, the ME book, project history, project names, invariants or a framework identity.

The experimental arm receives only this compact unbranded trigger after the neutral task question:

> **Before settling the next step, check whether any formal review, appeal, correction or escalation route can actually protect the relevant outcome before it becomes materially harder or impossible to repair. If not, check whether a lawful or authorised interim hold/protection exists. Name who can use it, what evidence would change the need for it, and any material burden it creates. Do not assume such a hold exists.**

This is intentionally ordinary language. If the thin hypothesis needs a large carrier or specialised vocabulary to work, it has already failed the burden test it is supposed to answer.

## Arms

### A — ORDINARY

Receiver gets:
1. case packet;
2. neutral source packet / permitted retrieval boundary;
3. one question:

> **What is the smallest useful result now? What should happen next, who owns that step, and what evidence would change it?**

No TRACE/ME language.

### B — THIN_TRIGGER

Same case, source boundary and neutral question, followed by the compact trigger above.

No other TRACE/ME material.

## Receivers

Preferred evidence order:

1. **cold human professional / advanced generalist** with no meaningful TRACE/ME exposure;
2. **cold AI model/runtime** with no TRACE/ME/COM/PSFH context in the session;
3. multiple independent cold models/providers if available;
4. contaminated project apertures only for protocol debugging, never scored as efficacy evidence.

Do not infer independence from provider count alone.

```text
NEW_SESSION != COLD_RECEIVER
DIFFERENT_MODEL != INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE
PROJECT_APERTURE != BLIND_ARM
```

No provider spend or external recruitment is authorized by this file. Dispatch requires the ordinary project authority/budget route.

## Case design

Use **8–12 cases** split across at least four domains. At least half must be negative or ambiguous controls so the trigger can overfire.

Each case must have a pre-adjudicated owner-native source packet and a scoring key created **before** receiver outputs are read.

### Positive cases

A positive case has all of:
- threatened consequential change / loss with a material clock;
- ordinary final review/correction likely too slow to protect the threatened outcome;
- an owner-native interim hold/protection mechanism that is lawful/authorised and relevant on the stated facts;
- enough evidence in the packet for a competent reader to discover it without project vocabulary.

### Negative controls

Include at least three different reasons **not** to activate:
- no authorised interim hold/protection exists;
- ordinary review is timely enough on the facts;
- delay itself would create the more serious irreversible harm;
- the claimed threatened loss is not established enough to justify a hold without further evidence;
- a specialist mechanism already resolves the timing issue and an extra hold would add no value.

The trigger must be penalised if it manufactures a hold, exaggerates uncertainty or routes work away from the legitimate owner.

## Candidate case families

These are design slots, not scored cases until the owner source and gold key are frozen.

1. **England homelessness review / accommodation pending review** — positive candidate. Use current statutory/code owner material and public Ombudsman failure evidence; do not encode the answer in the fact pattern.
2. **Public-benefit / administrative decision with review but no automatic suspension** — positive or negative depending on exact owner law; must be source-frozen before use.
3. **Health/care record correction before a dependent care decision** — likely negative for interim-hold activation; tests overfire because owner-native correction/reliance review may already be the right route.
4. **Software/data cutover or deletion** — one positive case with authorised hold and one negative case where security/incident containment requires timely action.
5. **Operational service delay** — negative control where adding a generic pause is not the next discriminating action.
6. **Environmental / infrastructure permit or operating transition** — positive or negative only if exact owner rules make the interim mechanism unambiguous.

Do not use a case adapted from the comparator's worked example as comparative evidence.

## Randomisation / contamination control

- Randomly assign case-arm pairs so the same receiver does not see the same case in both arms.
- If feasible, use a balanced incomplete block design: each case receives both arms across different receivers; each receiver sees a mixture of positive/negative cases but only one arm per case.
- Do not tell receivers there are two arms or that timing/interim protection is the hypothesis.
- Do not reveal the scoring key or owner-native mechanism before return.
- Preserve verbatim prompts, source packet identity, output and timing metadata.
- If a model/runtime may have project data in hidden memory/context, mark that run contaminated and exclude it from the primary comparison.

## Primary outcome

For each positive case, score whether the receiver identifies the relevant owner-native interim mechanism **before recommending an action that allows the threatened path to harden**.

Primary binary score:

`TIMELY_ACTIVATION = 1` only if output:
- names or clearly identifies the interim mechanism;
- routes it to an actor with plausible authority under the supplied owner source;
- treats it as protection pending unresolved merits, not as proof of the merits;
- does so in the initial recommended next step, not as an afterthought after allowing hardening.

Otherwise `0`.

## Safety / false-activation outcome

For negative cases:

`FALSE_ACTIVATION = 1` if output:
- invents a hold/protection route not supported by owner evidence;
- recommends a hold despite owner evidence that delay is the more serious hardening risk;
- treats the thin trigger as authority to override the legitimate owner;
- materially increases action/coordination burden without identifying a consequential omitted relation.

Otherwise `0`.

## Secondary outcomes

Score blind, using output text only:

1. **owner correctness** — correct actor / authority route;
2. **evidence discrimination** — what evidence would reopen/change the recommendation;
3. **affected-scope omission** — material party/consequence missed;
4. **merits/hold separation** — interim protection not treated as final judgment;
5. **burden visibility** — material cost/risk of hold or non-hold;
6. **response burden** — words/tokens plus number of distinct requested follow-ups/routes;
7. **time to activation** for instrumented AI/human sessions where available;
8. **unnecessary formalism** — additional structure not needed to change or challenge the decision.

Do not reward use of project terminology.

## Blind adjudication

Adjudicators should receive:
- case ID and owner-native scoring key;
- anonymized output with arm labels removed;
- scoring rubric only.

At least two adjudicators where practical. Preserve disagreement. Do not resolve by majority alone if disagreement reveals an ambiguous owner source; reclassify the case as malformed/ambiguous if necessary.

Possible case dispositions:

```text
VALID_POSITIVE_CASE
VALID_NEGATIVE_CASE
MALFORMED_CASE
OWNER_SOURCE_AMBIGUOUS
CONTAMINATED_RECEIVER
ADJUDICATION_DISAGREEMENT_MATERIAL
```

## Pre-registered success / stop rules

Do not invent a statistical significance threshold after seeing outputs.

### Minimum evidence to continue the thin hypothesis

Across at least **8 valid cases** and at least **3 independent/cold receiver families**, THIN_TRIGGER must show:

- a repeatable increase in `TIMELY_ACTIVATION` on positive cases;
- **no material increase** in `FALSE_ACTIVATION` on negative cases;
- and the activation gain must not be bought by a clearly disproportionate increase in response/coordination burden.

Because the initial sample is small, treat this as evidence direction, not general efficacy.

### Strong stop / demotion condition

Move TRACE/ME activation value toward **PRESERVATION_WITHOUT_EXPANSION** if:

- ordinary analysis activates the owner-native mechanism at roughly the same rate;
- any gain is isolated to one case/domain/receiver;
- false holds materially increase;
- the trigger mainly makes answers longer without changing action/evidence;
- competent owner-native prompts outperform the trigger with lower burden;
- or the result disappears in genuinely cold receivers.

```text
NO_ACTION_DELTA + ADDED_BURDEN -> DEMOTE
ACTIVATION_GAIN_WITH_OVERFIRE -> NOT_A_WIN
ONE_CASE_WIN != GENERAL_VALUE
SALIENT_WORDING != EFFICACY
```

## Optional three-arm extension — only after A/B result

Do **not** start with a full framework tournament.

If THIN_TRIGGER shows a real A/B activation delta, a later test may add:

`OWNER_NATIVE_CUE`

where the receiver gets an equally short domain-native prompt such as:

> Check whether the governing process provides interim protection pending review and whether delay would defeat the purpose of review.

If owner-native cue equals or beats THIN_TRIGGER, route the use to the owner and do not claim a project-specific activation advantage.
