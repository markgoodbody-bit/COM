## Provisional editorial concerns

- “The owner can reach repair directly” compresses an actual managing-agent
  route and a three-day wait. Prefer naming that route over suggesting no
  intermediary or immediate access. The source contrast is differential access,
  not simply owner acts / tenant cannot act.
- “While the formal route was working” assumes the point at issue. The portal
  accepted a report; that does not establish that the repair route worked.
- “When did repair stop being able to restore the same future?” presumes a
  determinate threshold the short scene does not supply. A more source-bounded
  question is: “What had changed by the time an inspection was offered?”
- The scene includes a rent-increase letter, privacy burden of a wider photograph
  and fewer subsequent messages. A very short version may omit these, but should
  not imply passive delay explains the whole outcome.
- “Later software may...” is explicitly hypothetical in the book. Do not turn
  it into a subsequent event in the website account.

These are editorial cautions, not evidence that a concrete-first page is easier
to use. Reader benefit remains unmeasured.
