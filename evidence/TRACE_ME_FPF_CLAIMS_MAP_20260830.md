## 2. Shortest defensible result

FPF, TRACE, and Mechanical Ethics do not currently form three competing
theories of the same thing.

- **FPF** is a very broad standards-style pattern language for keeping project
  objects, claims, evidence, decisions, work, architecture, publication, and
  improvement relations explicit.
- **TRACE** is a specialised structural carrier that keeps a selected causal
  scene, partial observation, transitions, clocks, routes, correction,
  burden, residue, and future paths in one inspectable representation.
- **Mechanical Ethics** is a human-facing moral argument about what power,
  delay, uncertainty, and correction do to affected lives, centred on keeping
  genuinely usable futures open before loss hardens.

The strongest current relationship is:

```text
FPF can discipline how TRACE or ME claims are stated, evidenced, applied,
published and bounded.

TRACE can provide a specialised representation for a subset of project
questions that FPF can also address through several direct patterns.

ME can supply a contestable normative source for questions that neither FPF
nor TRACE settles structurally.
```

None validates the others.

## 4. Where FPF already owns the stronger general machinery

### 4.1 Claim, evidence, provenance and reliance

FPF `A.10` already gives a mature claim-bound evidence and provenance route.
It keeps carrier identity, source, performed work, result, currentness, bounded
reliance, challenge, authority and later use distinct.

TRACE's corresponding discipline is useful but not conceptually prior. Its
best case is portability: the same protections fire inside TRACE fields,
configuration, metadata and prose whenever a proposition carries a downstream
conclusion.

Current disposition: **FPF owns the broad project pattern; TRACE offers a
specialised projection and firing rule. No uniqueness claim is earned.**

### 4.2 Assurance and evidence aggregation

FPF `B.3` already requires one exact assurance claim and use, explicit
dependencies, an applicable aggregation model, independence and calibration
assumptions, a rival rule, limits, and a reopen condition. It expressly refuses
to manufacture a pooled score when no defensible join rule exists.

This is stronger and more explicit than the current project's informal use of
large return counts. Multiple AI returns with shared authorship, prompts,
providers, training data or source frames cannot be treated as independent
evidence by headcount.

Provider diversity can reduce one correlation class without producing an
independent audit. If this project selects the object, cases, prompt, standard,
reviewers and synthesis rule, the resulting loop remains project-controlled
even when several model providers participate. Any external-audit claim would
need examiner independence, appropriate underlying-record access, and a
standard not manufactured by the evaluated system for its own clearance.

Current disposition: **FPF supplies a direct correction to COM/Campfire
practice. Agreement remains elaboration until dependence and adjudication are
handled.**

### 4.3 Causal and temporal claims

FPF `C.27` and `C.28` separate temporal change from causal use, bound the rung
of causal inference, preserve model and evidence limitations, and keep the
downstream decision outside the evidential result. FPF also has route,
transformation-flow, boundary, measurement, currentness and architecture
patterns.

TRACE does not appear to add a new causal or temporal primitive. It brings a
particular set of these concerns together around correction before hardening,
route executability, affected scope, burden and residue.

Current disposition: **the candidate value of TRACE is integrated emphasis and
transfer, not ownership of causal or temporal discipline.**

### 4.4 Apparatus burden and justified non-use

FPF `C.19.2` already asks whether a model, formalism, ontology, assurance
technique or other apparatus earns its setup and application work for one
declared use. This applies directly to TRACE itself.

TRACE therefore fails its own next stage if it merely demonstrates that it can
represent a case. It must show that, for a bounded consequential use, it avoids
an omission or preserves a relation that competent ordinary analysis or a
smaller FPF route loses, at a cost worth paying.

Current disposition: **TRACE efficacy remains unearned, and its material
activation burden is currently adverse evidence rather than a footnote.**

## 5. What TRACE may still add

### Earned result

The v0.3 RC1 surface is materially clearer and more honest about its limits
than the earlier repository surface. It keeps several dangerous collapses
visible in one place, including:

```text
MAP != WORLD
UNKNOWN != ABSENT
CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY
ROUTE_EXISTS != ROUTE_USABLE
HARDENING != IRREVERSIBILITY
CHECK_EXECUTED != CHECK_DETECTS_TARGET_FAILURE
LOCAL_CASE_REPAIRED != GENERATING_MECHANISM_REPAIRED
```

### Provisional contribution

TRACE may be useful when a consequential case repeatedly loses connections
among affected scope, partial observation, causal transitions, clocks, route
usability, correction feasibility, burden, residue and future-path
correspondence as it passes between people or tools.

That is an arrangement and transfer hypothesis. It is not a new theory of
causation, evidence, time, systems, ethics, or agency.

### Hostile reading

1. A careful ordinary case analysis may preserve the same distinctions with
   much less apparatus.
2. FPF can already recover the relevant direct patterns, although at the cost
   of navigating a much larger specification.
3. TRACE's formal completeness can attract effort toward packet production
   rather than changing the decision.
4. The full reference may be too large for the very transfer problem TRACE is
   meant to solve.
5. A representation that exposes the wrong target set more precisely remains
   wrong.

### Falsifier

On matched consequential cases, blinded readers using TRACE do not find more
material omissions or preserve more decision-changing relations than readers
using competent ordinary analysis or a smaller FPF route, after burden and
false confidence are counted.

If that result persists, TRACE should narrow to a teaching checklist, local
profile, or archive rather than continue as a large general specification.

## 6. What Mechanical Ethics may still add

### Earned result

Mechanical Ethics now presents a coherent human argument with its limitations
visible. Its strongest explanatory distinction is not merely that decisions
can be wrong. It is that the institutional file can be corrected after the
future affected by the decision has already changed.

The central timing statement is a useful necessary condition inside its stated
scope:

> Effective protection must be in place before the threatened path hardens
> beyond comparable repair.

The current conclusion correctly keeps path designation, evidence, standing,
collision and priority contestable.

### Provisional moral thesis

Power should not remove an affected being from decisions about its own life by
closing every genuinely viable continuation. A viable route must be findable,
understandable, affordable, usable, maintainable and open to answer, refusal,
challenge and correction.

This is the part neither TRACE nor FPF can derive from structural discipline.
They can represent and test its inputs and consequences, but they cannot make
the normative choice true.

### Hostile reading

1. **Standing:** the book does not yet justify which entities or interests
   receive protection when status is uncertain.
2. **Collision:** keeping one route open for one being may close or burden a
   route for another. The timing condition detects the collision but does not
   rank it.
3. **Floor grounding:** findable, affordable, correctable and viable are partly
   normative filters. Their minimum content and authorship remain open.
4. **Representation:** infants, unconscious people, animals and future or
   unobserved subjects cannot always remain present directly; representation
   can protect or capture them.
5. **Authority and enforcement:** diagnosing a late or unusable route does not
   identify who may interrupt it or compel repair.
6. **Burden rule:** placing complexity on those with power is a strong
   presumption, not a complete allocation rule. Power, knowledge, causal
   contribution, capacity, duty and legitimate authority may point to
   different actors.
7. **Nearest neighbours:** contestability, interim relief, due process,
   administrative burden, capabilities, care ethics and irreversibility may
   already contain much of the conceptual material. Integration and voice are
   not the same as novelty.

### Falsifier or narrowing result

If established contestability plus interim-relief, due-process and
capabilities traditions already supply the same temporal and agency constraint
with clearer standing, authority and collision rules, ME's defensible role may
be a readable synthesis or domain-facing argument rather than a new moral
framework.

That outcome would not make the book useless. It would change the publication
claim.

## 7. What FPF contributes, and where it remains vulnerable

### Earned result

The inspected FPF patterns repeatedly enforce distinctions this project needs:
claim versus carrier, evidence versus truth, method versus work, work versus
result, assurance versus permission, model versus world, causal support versus
action, and publication form versus adequacy. It also contains explicit stop,
non-use, bounded-result and reopen routes.

### Provisional contribution

FPF may provide a durable reference model and pattern language for maintaining
coherence across long, mixed human/AI projects. That is a practical use claim,
not an empirical result established by the size or normativity of the
specification.

### Hostile reading

1. At more than 75,000 lines, FPF can require an AI or expert navigator before
   an ordinary user can find the relevant pattern.
2. Its breadth creates a retrospective-fit risk: after a failure, some pattern
   can often be found that appears to have anticipated it.
3. Internal completeness and cross-referencing do not establish that users
   apply the right pattern, or that the pattern changes outcomes.
4. A normative specification can precisely govern claims while remaining
   silent on which contested moral premise should govern action.
5. FPF's public status says it is already used in working projects, but this
   audit found no public comparative efficacy result sufficient to treat it as
   a validated standard.
6. FPF's ability to absorb local frameworks as DPFs or LPFs can clarify
   dependency, but can also make independent contribution appear subordinate
   by definition.

### Falsifier

Cold users with ordinary project questions cannot reliably find and apply the
smallest relevant pattern without specialist or AI mediation, or do not make
fewer consequential errors than users of simpler domain methods after burden
is counted.

## 8. Relationship without hierarchy theatre

The most defensible current picture is:

```text
ME supplies a contestable moral pressure:
  preserve usable participation and correction before paths harden.

TRACE supplies a specialised structural lens:
  keep the selected causal, temporal, route, burden and residue relations
  connected and explicit.

FPF supplies project reasoning discipline:
  identify the exact objects, claims, evidence, work, use, assurance,
  publication and stop conditions involved.
```

This does not imply:

```text
ME -> TRACE -> FPF as a mandatory stack
FPF COMPATIBILITY -> FPF CONFORMANCE
FPF COVERAGE -> TRACE REDUNDANT
TRACE FORMALIZATION -> ME TRUE
ME MORAL FORCE -> TRACE AUTHORITY
```

A user should be allowed to use any one of the three, a smaller established
method, or careful ordinary analysis when that reaches the needed result with
less burden.

For COM/Campfire review, three questions must remain separate:

```text
RECEIVER CORRECTNESS AGAINST THE GIVEN OBJECT
OBJECT ADEQUACY TO THE TASK OR WORLD
REVIEW-SCOPE ADEQUACY TO THE COMPOSED CAUSAL UNIT
```

An independent receiver can correctly assess an incomplete frozen object. A
system-level critic can also miss a composed effect when the grouping rule is
wrong. The grouping rule is therefore an auditable claim, not neutral
preprocessing. A defensible grouped review must use information available
before the answer is known and must count invented relations in honest controls
as failures.

The project's central self-certification risk is correspondingly plain: it can
define the distinctions, design the tasks, select the reviewers, integrate the
returns, and then cite that integration as evidence that its distinctions were
useful. Cross-provider agreement does not by itself break that institutional
loop.

## 9. Questions worth taking to the FPF maintainers

The first contact should ask rather than announce.

1. Does FPF already have a preferred way to represent a correction route whose
   formal existence persists while its ability to protect a threatened future
   expires?
2. Where would FPF place the difference between correcting a claim or record
   and restoring the affected continuation, including residual burden after
   correction?
3. Does FPF treat a compact domain-specific integration of several existing
   patterns as a legitimate local framework contribution when it adds no new
   primitive, and what evidence should justify that product boundary?
4. How does FPF prevent its own scale and broad coverage from producing
   retrospective fit or excessive application burden?
5. What would count, for FPF, as evidence that a local framework is useful but
   should remain independent rather than become a DPF or LPF?

These questions do not require the maintainers to read TRACE or ME in full.
The first exchange should include a short ordinary example and disclose that
both projects are unvalidated working candidates.
