## Source-bounded study shape

According to the published abstract:
- randomized controlled trial;
- 72 surgical residents;
- intervention: structured ABCD framework — articulate; brainstorm and benchmark; critique and customize; decide and discuss;
- control: unguided AI use;
- all participants analysed eight standardized gastric-cancer cases using DeepSeek;
- primary outcome: clinical decision quality, rated by blinded experts using the study's Clinical Decision Quality Assessment Scale (CDQAS);
- secondary measures included interaction-process quality, self-efficacy, evidence-solicitation frequency, what-if prompting and match to expert-derived key points.

The abstract reports higher primary decision-quality and interaction-quality scores in the guided arm, plus increased evidence solicitation and what-if questioning.

## Why it matters to #119

The useful import is **study architecture**, not outcome transfer.

It establishes that a contemporary research team has used a bounded shape of:

`SAME TASK DOMAIN + SAME AI TOOL + GUIDED INTERACTION vs UNGUIDED INTERACTION + BLINDED EXPERT OUTCOME RATING`

That is relevant to the design vocabulary around #119 because it avoids comparing a structured aid only against “nothing.” It also measures process separately from final outcome.

But the direct comparator is **unguided AI**, whereas #119's first honest comparator should be a matched generic structured-risk prompt. Against an empty/unguided baseline, almost any systematic scaffold may gain an advantage. Therefore the ABCD trial does **not** solve our comparator problem.

## Important non-transfer boundaries

Do not say this study validates PSFH, TRACE, Mechanical Ethics or correction-window questions.

Reasons include:
- specialised clinical-education domain;
- surgical trainees rather than heterogeneous ordinary readers;
- eight standardized gastric-cancer cases, not independently selected public incident/ombudsman/regulatory accounts;
- DeepSeek as the study LLM;
- intervention content is ABCD, not PSFH;
- control is unguided use, not matched structured reasoning;
- study-specific rating scales and expert-derived clinical key points;
- decision quality is a broader/different outcome from #119's first proposed construct: detecting irreversibility points;
- publication/abstract evidence does not establish persistence after scaffold removal or generalisation outside the sampled task.

`NEIGHBOURING_RCT != OUR_EVIDENCE`

## What #119 can learn

If Phase 0 establishes that irreversibility-point reference sets can be made reliable, a later PSFH study could borrow only generic design discipline:
- freeze cases and conditions;
- keep tool/task budgets matched;
- blind outcome raters where possible;
- separate process/uptake measures from final outcome;
- use a comparator strong enough to answer whether the *specific structure* adds anything beyond generic systematic reasoning.

The current #119 decision remains unchanged: first establish whether the outcome construct itself exists reliably. No recruitment, inference, study execution or public claim follows from recovering this citation.
