# MIDDLE-OUT FIELD CARD v0.1

**Status:** EXPERIMENTAL HELPING CARD / NON-CANONICAL / OPTIONAL / NOT AUTHORITY / NOT TRACE OR ME  
**Date:** 2026-08-31

Use this only when a situation feels too compressed to trust.

You do not need to use the vocabulary. Change the questions. Reject the card if it makes the situation worse.

## 1. What are we calling the entity?

- What boundary are we using?
- Who drew it?
- What important thing moves inside/outside the picture if the boundary changes?

`ENTITY_LABEL != ENTITY_RESOLVED`

## 2. What can it actually do here?

Do not trust the supplied menu of choices.

- Which options are physically, socially, legally, technically and practically available?
- Which require another actor, resource, skill or permission?
- Which could become possible with support or learning?

`LISTED_OPTION != REAL_OPTION`

## 3. Which futures are merely reachable, and which can actually hold together?

- Can this path be reached?
- Can the relevant entity/system remain inside its essential constraints if it takes that path?
- Are we confusing more options with more capability?

`REACHABLE != VIABLE`

Only use viability language when the subject and constraints are actually defined.

## 4. What could become possible that is not possible yet?

- What capability can be learned, built, shared or scaffolded?
- Would a constraint or commitment close one path while creating a more valuable shared capability?
- When should support withdraw so it does not become permanent dependence?

`CURRENT_CAPABILITY != DEVELOPABLE_CAPABILITY`

## 5. What clocks are running, and who carries the cost while we learn?

Time changes the action field.

- Which options expire, harden or become much more costly if we wait?
- Which protections or capabilities could extend the window?
- How long will evidence gathering, routing and correction take?
- Who carries risk, work, money, exposure, pain or lost opportunity while uncertainty remains?
- Does delay move burden onto an entity with less control?

`DELAY != NEUTRAL`

`ROUTE_EXISTS != ROUTE_ARRIVES_IN_TIME`

`UNCERTAINTY_REMAINS -> SOMEONE_CARRIES_IT`

## 6. At what scale should each part of the decision live?

Do not assume the whole decision belongs to one actor or level.

Ask where:

- the information lives;
- affected standing lives;
- authority lives;
- externalities spill;
- coordination is possible;
- correction is reachable.

`WHO_SHOULD_ACT?` may need to become `WHICH_PART_SHOULD_LIVE_AT_WHICH_SCALE?`

## 7. What are we building for later entities?

An action changes more than the next state.

- What new dependencies, tools, rules, habits, incentives, memories or ecological conditions will it create?
- What becomes easier or harder for whoever comes next?
- Could the action change which future entities exist at all?

`ACTION -> NEW POSSIBILITY LANDSCAPE`

## 8. What should persist, commit, release or transform?

Do not assume preservation is always good or that reversibility is always possible/desirable.

Ask whether the current arrangement should:

```text
PERSIST
COMMIT
RELEASE
REORGANISE
TRANSFORM
EXIT
```

`PERSISTENCE != RESILIENCE`

## 9. What distinction are we about to throw away?

Before compressing, ask:

> **If this omitted difference were known, could it reasonably require a different action, protection, scale, route or monitoring plan?**

If yes, keep it visible or carry it as UNKNOWN.

If no, let it fall out of the active map.

`RELEVANT_VARIETY != MAXIMUM_DETAIL`

---

# Before acting

Check:

```text
Do I know enough for the next step, not for the whole world?
Have I kept any action-changing unknowns visible?
Have I accounted for the clocks and who carries delay/uncertainty?
Is the action within actual authority?
Can I observe what happens and reopen the map?
```

`ENOUGH_TO_ACT != COMPLETE_WORLD_MODEL`

---

# Special type guard for artificial systems

Do not infer intrinsic concern, consciousness, moral standing or self-maintained normativity from operational behaviour alone.

```text
TOOL_ACCESS != AUTHORITY
ROLE_CONTINUITY != RUNTIME_IDENTITY
OPERATIONAL_CONTINUITY != INTRINSIC_VIABILITY
MODELLED_GOAL != SELF_MAINTAINED_NORM
DISTRIBUTED_COGNITION != CONTINUOUS_SUBJECTIVE_IDENTITY
```

The same restraint applies in the other direction: absence of established standing is not proof of absence of morally relevant properties.

`UNKNOWN != ABSENT`

---

# What success looks like

Not reciting the card.

Success is noticing that something consequential disappeared when the situation was simplified, recovering enough of it to choose more intelligently, and staying able to revise the map when the world answers back.
