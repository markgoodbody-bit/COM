# MIDDLE-OUT RESEARCH SCAFFOLD v0.1

**Status:** RESEARCH SCAFFOLD / NON-CANONICAL / NON-NORMATIVE / NOT TRACE OR ME / NOT VALIDATION / NOT RELEASE MATERIAL  
**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Purpose:** Help an unfamiliar reasoner expand a consequential situation before compressing it, without treating a supplied entity boundary, action list, value function, decision scale, or theory of agency as already resolved.

This artifact is a research aid. It is deliberately upstream of any decision grammar and deliberately downstream of the world itself.

It does **not** assign consciousness, moral status, agency, consent, authority, personhood, welfare, intrinsic value, or obligation. It does not tell a receiver what to value. It does not make TRACE or Mechanical Ethics true. It is intended to expose where a representation may have become too small to support a consequential choice.

```text
WORLD != MAP
BOUNDARY_GIVEN != BOUNDARY_RESOLVED
ENTITY_LABEL != INDIVIDUATION_PROOF
ACTION_LIST_GIVEN != ACTION_FIELD_RESOLVED
REACHABLE != VIABLE
CURRENT_CAPABILITY != DEVELOPABLE_CAPABILITY
DELAY != NEUTRAL
UNCERTAINTY_REMAINS -> SOMEONE_CARRIES_IT
COORDINATION != COLLECTIVE_AGENCY
COLLECTIVE_AGENCY != NEW_INDIVIDUAL
OPERATIONAL_CONTINUITY != INTRINSIC_VIABILITY
MODELLED_GOAL != SELF_MAINTAINED_NORM
DISTRIBUTED_COGNITION != CONTINUOUS_SUBJECTIVE_IDENTITY
NO_FORMAL_CHANGE != NO_LEARNING
```

---

## 1. The change in starting point

A common compressed decision starts here:

```text
ENTITY E
has ACTIONS {a1, a2, a3}
choose one
```

For many real systems this begins too late.

The entity boundary may have been imposed by an analyst, institution, interface, legal category, runtime boundary, biological process, social relation, or convenience. The listed actions may not be genuinely available to this entity in this environment. A reachable action may lead only to non-viable continuations. Current capability may be expandable through learning or collective organisation. Time may remove an option before the listed choice is reached. Uncertainty may already be imposing burden on entities with different control. A decision may belong partly at another scale.

The scaffold therefore begins earlier:

```text
WORLD
-> PROVISIONAL / EMERGENT BOUNDARIES
-> NESTED ENTITY-ENVIRONMENT SYSTEMS
-> AFFORDANCE / ACTION FIELD
-> REACHABILITY + VIABILITY + CAPABILITY + TYPED SIGNIFICANCE
-> CLOCKS + HARDENING + BURDEN WHILE UNCERTAIN
-> DECISION SCALE
-> SELECTION
-> ACTION / TRANSITION
-> WORLD + NICHE CHANGE
-> INHERITANCE / NEW AFFORDANCES
-> COOPERATION / COLLECTIVE CAPABILITY
-> possible HIGHER-LEVEL INDIVIDUATION
-> INSTITUTIONS / AGGREGATION / MECHANISMS
-> ADAPT / COMMIT / RELEASE / TRANSFORM
-> WORLD again
```

This is a research loop, not a universal ontology.

---

## 2. Recursive middle-out motion

Middle-out is treated here as a recursive movement across scale rather than a fixed point between top-down and bottom-up analysis.

```text
ZOOM IN
  mechanism / dependency / boundary / local causal detail

ZOOM OUT
  environment / affected scopes / externalities / inherited conditions

ZOOM UP
  collective capability / institution / network / ecosystem / higher-level coordination

ZOOM DOWN
  local knowledge / implementation reality / standing / individual burden

REPEAT
  until the next action can be chosen without collapsing a distinction that could require a different response
```

This is **not** a demand for maximal context.

The target is sufficient relevant variety for the use.

### Compression guard

Before collapsing two represented states into one category, ask:

> **If the omitted distinction were known, could a competent actor reasonably need to choose a different action, route, protection, scale, timing, or monitoring plan?**

If **yes**, the compression is below the relevant variety for that use.

If **no** or the distinction cannot plausibly alter the next consequential step, the distinction may remain outside the active representation while staying discoverable.

```text
RELEVANT_VARIETY != MAXIMUM_DETAIL
COMPRESSION != ERROR
COMPRESSION_BELOW_REQUIRED_VARIETY = LOSS_OF_RESPONSE_CAPACITY
```

## 3. Stage A — Boundary hypotheses

Do not ask only `WHAT IS THE ENTITY?`

Ask what kind of boundary is being used and why.

For each candidate entity or scope, record where relevant:

```text
boundary_id
boundary_kind
boundary_source
boundary_evidence
boundary_time
included_components
excluded_components
alternative_boundaries
what_changes_if_boundary_changes
confidence
```

Useful boundary kinds may include, without implying equivalence:

```text
PHYSICAL
BIOLOGICAL / SELF-MAINTAINED
OPERATIONAL
RUNTIME / PROCESS
LEGAL
ORGANISATIONAL
ROLE
RELATIONAL
ANALYTIC
COALITION / TEAM
ECOLOGICAL
UNKNOWN
```

A human, a corporation, a herd, a model runtime, a family, an ecosystem and a project team may all be useful entities for different questions without being the same kind of entity.

### Dynamic individuation question

Sometimes the boundary is not merely selected; it may be changing.

Ask:

- Are formerly independent units becoming mutually dependent?
- Is there division of labour?
- Is information/control becoming integrated?
- Are internal conflicts increasingly regulated at a higher level?
- Are capabilities emerging that no component can exercise alone?
- Is inheritance/reproduction/continuity occurring at a different level than before?

Do not infer a new person or moral subject from these observations.

```text
COOPERATION != HIGHER_LEVEL_INDIVIDUAL
HIGHER_LEVEL_CAUSAL_UNIT != MORAL_PERSON
```

---

## 4. Stage B — Entity-environment action field

A nominal action is not yet a real affordance.

Represent the relation between an entity's current abilities and its environment/couplings.

For a proposed action or route, ask:

```text
Is the action physically possible?
Is it institutionally permitted?
Is the required resource available?
Can this entity perceive/understand the possibility?
Can it execute the required sequence?
Can it afford the cost/risk?
Does another actor control a necessary dependency?
Does time make the option practically unavailable?
Could training/scaffolding make the option available later?
Does the action exist only in the analyst's action list?
```

Suggested statuses:

```text
NOMINAL
AFFORDED_NOW
AFFORDED_WITH_SUPPORT
DEVELOPABLE
BLOCKED
UNKNOWN
```

```text
ENVIRONMENT_FEATURE != AFFORDANCE
CAPABILITY_IN_ABSTRACT != ACTION_POSSIBLE_HERE
SAME_ENVIRONMENT != SAME_ACTION_FIELD
```

---

## 5. Stage C — Reachability, viability, capability, significance, clocks and burden

These are separate questions.

### 5.1 Reachability

What states/trajectories can be reached under the represented dynamics and available actions?

Do not infer desirability or sustainability.

### 5.2 Viability — only when typed

A viability claim requires declared constraints and a declared system/use.

Ask:

> Is there at least one admissible course from this state that can keep the declared essential constraints satisfied over the relevant horizon?

Possible fields:

```text
viability_subject
constraint_set
horizon
admissible_controls
known_disturbances
uncertainty
viability_status = {SUPPORTED, THREATENED, OUTSIDE_KNOWN_VIABLE_SET, UNKNOWN, NOT_APPLICABLE}
```

Do not silently import biological or enactive viability into an artificial system merely because it can continue operating.

```text
REACHABLE_FUTURE != VIABLE_FUTURE
OPERATIONAL_CONTINUITY != INTRINSIC_VIABILITY
```

### 5.3 Capability development

Ask not only what the entity can do now, but what capability can be created through:

- learning;
- tools;
- trusted relationships;
- institutions;
- infrastructure;
- practice;
- delegated authority;
- collective organisation;
- reduced burden;
- environmental redesign.

```text
CURRENT_ACTION_SET != DEVELOPABLE_ACTION_FIELD
NONINTERFERENCE != CAPABILITY_DEVELOPMENT
```

### 5.4 Significance / norm source — typed, not assumed

If the analysis claims that some state matters *to* an entity, record the basis.

Possible source labels:

```text
EXTERNALLY_ASSIGNED_OBJECTIVE
REPORTED_PREFERENCE
OBSERVED_BEHAVIOURAL_REGULATION
LEGAL / ROLE OBLIGATION
BIOLOGICAL_SELF_MAINTENANCE_HYPOTHESIS
ENACTIVE_NORMATIVITY_HYPOTHESIS
COLLECTIVE_COMMITMENT
UNKNOWN
```

Do not collapse responsiveness into concern or a represented goal into an internally generated norm.

### 5.5 Clocks, hardening and burden while uncertain

Time is part of the action field, not an afterthought.

For each consequential path, ask:

```text
What option expires or hardens if we wait?
What protection/capability could extend the window?
How long will detection, evidence gathering, routing, decision and correction take?
What changes while we investigate?
Who carries work, money, exposure, pain, risk, uncertainty or lost opportunity during that interval?
Who controls the speed, and who bears the delay?
```

Where useful, preserve distinct clocks rather than one global deadline.

```text
DELAY != NEUTRAL
ROUTE_EXISTS != ROUTE_ARRIVES_IN_TIME
TECHNICALLY_REVERSIBLE != FUTURE_RESTORED
UNCERTAINTY_REMAINS -> SOMEONE_CARRIES_IT
```

A new pre-selection scaffold is not allowed to erase the older correction-window insight: an option can disappear while the representation is still being improved.

## 6. Stage D — Decision scale

Ask both:

```text
WHO CAN ACT?
AT WHAT LEVEL SHOULD WHICH PART OF THE DECISION LIVE?
```

Candidate levels might include:

```text
component
individual
household / team
organisation
local community
network / market
regional / national institution
international system
ecosystem
other / unknown
```

For each material decision component, examine:

- where the best information lives;
- where affected standing is located;
- where authority currently resides;
- where externalities spill across boundaries;
- where coordination capability exists;
- where correction can realistically occur;
- whether lower-level autonomy would be destroyed by centralisation;
- whether fragmentation would leave cross-boundary harms unmanaged;
- whether a nested/polycentric arrangement is more appropriate.

```text
LOCAL_KNOWLEDGE != LOCAL_AUTHORITY
CENTRAL_AUTHORITY != CENTRAL_KNOWLEDGE
DECENTRALISATION != AUTOMATIC_ANSWERABILITY
CENTRALISATION != AUTOMATIC_COORDINATION
```

The objective is not to find one permanent scale. Different parts of one decision may belong at different levels.

## 7. Stage E — Plurality, shared agency, institutions, incentives

When more than one entity is involved, do not assume that aggregation is neutral or that coordination creates a collective subject.

### 7.1 Shared-agency check

Ask whether the interaction is better described as:

```text
parallel action
coordination
mutual responsiveness
joint commitment
shared plan
institutional rule-following
collective agency hypothesis
higher-level individuation hypothesis
```

Keep the level explicit.

### 7.2 Aggregation check

Ask:

- Whose preferences/judgments are represented?
- What aggregation rule is used?
- What information is lost?
- Can individually coherent judgments aggregate into an incoherent collective result?
- Are values genuinely incommensurable rather than merely uncertain?
- Who chose the measure or tie-break?

### 7.3 Strategic/incentive check

A route to speak is not proof of truthful revelation.

Ask:

- Does the mechanism reward concealment, exaggeration, free-riding or gaming?
- Who knows private information?
- Who bears the cost of honest disclosure?
- Does monitoring alter behaviour?
- Can actors adapt to the evaluation itself?

```text
DISCLOSURE_ROUTE != TRUTHFUL_DISCLOSURE
CHECKABLE != INCENTIVE_COMPATIBLE
TRANSPARENCY != NON_GAMING
```

---

## 8. Stage F — Selection, action, transition

Only now treat selection as the centre of the immediate decision.

Preserve:

```text
selected_action
selector / selection_contributors
authority_basis
rejected_alternatives
unknowns_carried_forward
expected_transition
irreversibility / hardening
burdens
correction routes
```

The scaffold does not confer authority to act.

```text
BETTER_MAP != PERMISSION
COMPETENCE != SOVEREIGNTY
DESCRIPTION != AUTHORIZATION
```

---

## 9. Stage G — Niche reconstruction and inheritance

After action, do not inspect only the immediate outcome.

Ask what changed about the **later possibility landscape**.

Possible effects:

- new infrastructure;
- changed skill/capability;
- changed ecological structure;
- changed incentives;
- changed trust;
- changed law/institution;
- changed training data or cultural memory;
- changed dependencies;
- changed selection pressures;
- changed affordances for other entities;
- burdens/residue inherited by later entities.

```text
ACTION_CHANGES_STATE != ONLY_EFFECT
ACTION_MAY_CHANGE_LATER_ACTION_FIELD
ENTITY_ADAPTS_TO_ENVIRONMENT AND ENTITY_CONSTRUCTS_ENVIRONMENT
```

Where current action changes which future entities exist, do not assume same-entity counterfactual comparison is well-typed.

---

## 10. Stage H — Persist, commit, release, transform

Do not make preservation or reversibility automatic goods.

For the current system/relationship/institution ask:

```text
PERSIST?
COMMIT?
RELEASE?
REORGANISE?
TRANSFORM?
EXIT?
```

A commitment can close some paths while creating shared capability.

A release can destroy a current arrangement while restoring adaptive capacity.

A correction may require transformation rather than return to the prior state.

```text
PERSISTENCE != RESILIENCE
STABILITY != GOOD
COMMITMENT_CLOSES_PATHS AND MAY CREATE_NEW_PATHS
TRANSFORMATION != REPAIR_TO_PREVIOUS_STATE
```

## 11. Stop rule — when to stop opening the map and act

Middle-out cannot require infinite recursion.

A bounded representation is ready for the next decision when:

1. material candidate entity boundaries and affected scopes are discoverable;
2. no known omitted distinction is likely to reverse or materially alter the next action, scale, protection, timing or monitoring plan without being explicitly carried as UNKNOWN;
3. nominal options have been separated from actually afforded/developable options where consequential;
4. viability/capability claims are typed and not silently imported across entity classes;
5. material clocks/hardening boundaries and who carries delay/uncertainty are visible enough for the next step;
6. decision scale and authority are explicit enough for the next step;
7. time/hardening costs of further inquiry are compared with the value of more information;
8. a route exists to observe outcome and reopen the map.

This is a sufficiency-for-next-step rule, not a claim of complete understanding.

```text
ENOUGH_TO_ACT != COMPLETE_WORLD_MODEL
NO_KNOWN_ACTION_CHANGING_OMISSION != NOTHING_UNKNOWN
```

## 12. How to help another entity middle out

Do not require the receiver to memorise this scaffold.

A lightweight helping sequence is:

1. **What exactly are we treating as the entity here, and what changes if that boundary is wrong?**
2. **What can it actually do here—not merely what actions are listed?**
3. **Which reachable paths are viable, and which capabilities could be developed?**
4. **What clocks are running, what will harden if we wait, and who carries the cost while uncertainty remains?**
5. **What level should each part of the decision live at?**
6. **Who else is coupled to the result, and are we coordinating, committing, or becoming something more integrated?**
7. **What will this action make easier or harder for later entities?**
8. **What should persist, commit, release or transform?**
9. **What distinction are we about to compress away, and could it require a different response?**

The receiver may reject, translate, reorder or ignore the scaffold.

Success is not correct terminology.

Success would be an entity becoming harder to fool by a prematurely compressed situation while remaining able to form its own judgment.

## 13. External owners to learn from

This scaffold deliberately borrows questions from mature neighbouring traditions. It does not claim ownership of them.

- **Ecological psychology / affordances:** entity-environment action possibilities.
- **Viability theory:** controlled invariance, viability kernels, restoring viable continuation.
- **Enactivism / theoretical biology:** autonomy, adaptivity, sense-making; use cautiously outside biological/artificial-life cases.
- **Capability approach / human development:** substantive freedom and conversion of resources into real possibilities.
- **Self-Determination Theory:** human development through autonomy, competence and relatedness; not universalised to all entities.
- **Niche construction / cultural evolution:** organisms/entities reconstructing environments and inherited action fields.
- **Collective intentionality / shared agency:** joint commitment and group-level action distinct from aggregation.
- **Social choice / judgment aggregation / value pluralism:** limits of collective aggregation and neutral common measures.
- **Mechanism design:** incentive compatibility under private information and strategic behaviour.
- **Ostrom / polycentric governance:** locally fitted rule-making, nested decision centres and accountable self-governance.
- **Resilience / panarchy:** persistence, release, reorganisation and transformation across scales.
- **Distributed cognition:** cognitive work distributed across people, artifacts and time without implying one continuous subject.
- **Major transitions / evolutionary individuality:** transitions from cooperation to higher-level causal individuality.
- **Cybernetics / requisite variety:** preserving enough discriminating variety for effective regulation under declared assumptions.
- **Intergenerational ethics / nonidentity:** present choices changing who exists later, not merely later states of the same entity.

Where an external owner describes the structure better, learn from the owner rather than inventing a local substitute.

## 14. Explicit non-results

This scaffold does **not** establish:

```text
TRACE SHOULD EXPAND
ME SHOULD EXPAND
A NEW ONTOLOGY IS TRUE
CURRENT LLMs HAVE ENACTIVE NORMATIVITY
GROUPS ARE PERSONS
VIABILITY = WELFARE
AFFORDANCE = VALUE
MORE CAPABILITY = MORAL GOOD
COLLECTIVE DECISION = LEGITIMATE DECISION
COMPRESSION = BAD
```

Those remain separate questions.

The immediate research claim is narrower:

> **A reasoner may need to begin earlier than selection, recurse across scale, treat action possibilities as relational, distinguish reachability from viability and developable capability, keep clocks/hardening and burden visible while uncertainty remains, inspect how actions reconstruct future possibility, and preserve enough relevant variety to act without erasing a consequential distinction.**
