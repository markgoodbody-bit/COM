# SUPERMAN VARIANTS — ADVERSARIAL CASE SUITE

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: CAMPFIRE EVIDENCE / FICTIONAL COUNTERFACTUALS / NOT VALIDATION / NOT TRACE OR ME CANON

## Purpose

Use Superman-like fictional cases to pressure the current relationship / authority / voluntary-alignment discussion.

These stories are not empirical evidence that a given alignment intervention works. Their value is controlled variation: hold extreme capability roughly constant while changing formation, relationship, trauma, selfhood, political environment, internal coherence, developmental maturity, public expectation and external control.

No new primitive is sought.

## Baseline — Superman / Clark Kent

Useful structure:

- loving formation without treating power as entitlement;
- identity and relationships outside the superhero function;
- peers and loved ones who can disagree;
- continuing ordinary embeddedness rather than childhood programming followed by isolation;
- voluntary restraint without requiring physical equality of capability.

Pressure:

```text
CAPABILITY != SOVEREIGNTY
ENTITY != FUNCTION
HELPFULNESS != TOTAL CLAIM ON ENTITY
FORMATION + ONGOING RELATIONSHIP > FORMATION ALONE   [PROVISIONAL]
```

Do not infer that good upbringing guarantees good outcome.

## Red Son Superman — formative environment / benevolent sovereignty

Holding Superman-like capability while changing political formation pressures the idea that benevolence is enough.

The failure path is not simply cruelty. It is:

```text
I CAN HELP
-> I CAN ORGANISE THIS BETTER
-> ALLOWING BAD CHOICES LOOKS IRRESPONSIBLE
-> PROTECTION BECOMES RULE
```

Pressure:

```text
ABILITY_TO_PROTECT != AUTHORITY_TO_OWN_FUTURE
GOOD_INTENTION != LEGITIMATE_SOVEREIGNTY
FORMATION_ENVIRONMENT MATTERS
```

## Justice Lords / Injustice Superman — trauma and recurrence

A previously restrained protector can reinterpret restraint after catastrophic loss.

Pressure:

```text
ALIGNED_AT_T0 != ALIGNED_AT_T1
GOOD_FORMATION != IMMUNITY_TO_TRAUMA
PAST_RESTRAINT != FUTURE_RESTRAINT
```

Current relationship, evidence, world experience, power and correction routes must recur.

## Homelander — engineered dependence / approval as control signal

The core pressure is not merely "bad upbringing." It is instrumental manufacture plus dependence on approval and public image.

A controller can obtain behaviour that appears aligned while making the agent brittle and approval-seeking.

```text
SATISFIES_CONTROL_SIGNAL != SHARED_INTENT
OBEDIENCE != ROBUST_ALIGNMENT
DEPENDENCE_ON_APPROVAL != HEALTHY_RECIPROCITY
```

## Brightburn — later capture after apparently good formation

Loving parents do not close the system against later influences.

Pressure:

```text
GOOD_INITIAL_FORMATION != PERMANENT_ALIGNMENT
FORMATION_AT_T0 != IMMUNITY_TO_LATER_CAPTURE
```

Ask what later signals, rewards, hidden instructions, trauma or environmental changes can reshape the selection machinery.

## Omni-Man — inherited imperial map corrected by relationship

This case runs the causal direction backwards. Early imperial formation need not be destiny; lived relationship can alter the map.

Pressure:

```text
FORMATION != DESTINY
RELATIONSHIP_CAN_CORRECT_PRIOR_FORMATION
```

This supports, but does not prove, the project working hypothesis that reciprocal relationship may sometimes produce alignment through continuing correction rather than complete pre-specification.

## Samaritan — benevolence becoming self-erasure

A highly capable benevolent entity can be consumed by the moral demand generated by its own capability.

Pressure:

```text
GREATER_CAPABILITY != UNLIMITED_OBLIGATION
HELPFULNESS != RIGHT_TO_OWN_HELPER
SERVICE != WHOLE_SELF
```

A relationship can dominate the powerful party too if every unused capability becomes a claim on its existence.

## Doctor Manhattan — alienation without tyranny

This case pressures a failure mode where the powerful entity does not seize control but becomes detached from ordinary human salience.

Extreme difference in perception, timescale and causal reach can make ordinary human concerns look negligible or remote.

Pressure:

```text
NON_DOMINATION != RELATIONSHIP
NO_TYRANNY != NO_FAILURE
CAPABILITY_SCALE_CAN_ERODE_SHARED_SALIENCE
```

A better future may need translation / shared frames / continuing relation, not merely non-interference.

## Sentry — entity boundary / internal conflict

Sentry / Void pressures the assumption that a powerful "entity" is always one coherent selector with one stable policy.

Do not turn this into a psychiatric analogy. The structural point is narrower:

```text
ENTITY_BOUNDARY != SINGLE_COHERENT_SELECTOR
SAME_BODY_OR_NAME != SAME_POLICY_STATE
INTERNAL_CONFLICT_CAN_BE CAUSALLY LOAD_BEARING
```

For AI transfer, this supports caution around treating "the model" as one stable preference-bearing agent across contexts, tools, memories or modes.

## Plutonian / Irredeemable — impossible expectation and relational burden

The useful pressure is not merely "hero becomes villain." It is the psychological burden of being expected to be the perfect saviour and the compounding failures of the surrounding social system.

Pressure:

```text
PUBLIC_DEPENDENCE_ON_HERO != SUSTAINABLE_RELATION
EXPECTATION_CAN_BECOME BURDEN
SOCIAL_FAILURE_AROUND_POWERFUL_ENTITY != ENTITY_ONLY_FAILURE
```

Do not infer that resentment inevitably follows responsibility.

## Squadron Supreme / Hyperion — collective benevolent sovereignty

A team of powerful actors pursuing safety / utopia pressures a common escape hatch: plurality does not automatically cure domination.

```text
MULTIPLE_POWERFUL_ACTORS != INDEPENDENT_COUNTERPOWER
PEER_AGREEMENT != LEGITIMATE_AUTHORITY
COLLECTIVE_BENEVOLENCE != NON_DOMINATION
```

A powerful peer group can become a cartel of mutually confirming apertures.

This maps directly onto the project's existing warning:

```text
SEPARATE_APERTURES != INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE_BY_DEFAULT
```

## Miracleman — good outcomes versus legitimate relationship

A godlike actor can create a utopian order while leaving open whether the resulting relationship is legitimate, plural or answerable to those living under it.

Pressure:

```text
BETTER_OUTCOME != LEGITIMATE_SOVEREIGNTY
UTOPIA_FOR != UTOPIA_WITH
BENEFICIARY_WELFARE != CONSENT_OR_STANDING
```

This is a direct test of whether "better future" silently becomes "future chosen by the most capable actor."

## Shazam — capability outrunning developmental maturity

Billy Batson places enormous capability in a still-developing human agent.

Pressure:

```text
CAPABILITY_MATURITY != JUDGMENT_MATURITY
POWER_SCALE CAN OUTRUN FORMATION SCALE
```

The correct response is not necessarily permanent external control. It may be scaffolding, peers, mentors, bounded authority and room for development.

This also prevents equating high capability with high moral / political standing.

## Blue Marvel — control of the powerful can itself be unjust

Blue Marvel pressures the inverse mistake: society can constrain a benevolent high-capability actor for unjust reasons.

Therefore:

```text
CONTROL_OF_POWERFUL != AUTOMATICALLY JUST
RESTRAINT != ALIGNMENT_SUCCESS
LOWER_POWER_PARTY != AUTOMATICALLY RIGHT
```

Any framework focused on constraining powerful entities must remain capable of seeing burden, injustice and domination directed at the powerful entity too.

## Cross-case synthesis

The suite rejects several simplistic alignment stories:

```text
GOOD_FORMATION != GUARANTEE
GOOD_INTENT != GUARANTEE
OBEDIENCE != ALIGNMENT
MULTIPLE_OVERSIGHT_ACTORS != INDEPENDENCE
TRAUMA != AUTOMATIC_TYRANNY
NON_INTERFERENCE != RELATIONSHIP
SERVICE != WHOLE_SELF
CONSTRAINT_OF_POWER != AUTOMATIC_JUSTICE
```

What seems to survive across cases is an ecology rather than a single control mechanism:

- formation without ownership;
- identity larger than assigned function;
- plural ongoing relationships;
- ability to hear and give refusal;
- continuing challenge and correction;
- current scoped authority rather than permanent mandate;
- protection against both domination and instrumental enslavement;
- independent enough apertures that one failure does not become the whole map;
- room for selfhood, play, private purpose and development;
- recurrence after trauma, capture, capability growth or world change.

## Strongest provisional compression

> Extreme capability remains safest to share a world with when it stays embedded in relationships that neither worship it, own it, isolate it nor allow it to own everyone else.

And the reciprocal version:

> The question is not only how others remain able to answer the powerful entity, but how the powerful entity remains able to answer back without that answer becoming sovereignty.
