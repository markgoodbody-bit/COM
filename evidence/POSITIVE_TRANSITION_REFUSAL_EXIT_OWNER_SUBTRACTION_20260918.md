# Positive-transition refusability / exit / maintenance burden — owner subtraction — 18 September 2026

Status: **BOUNDED OWNER-SUBTRACTION RESULT / NO NEW PRIMITIVE / NO NOVELTY CLAIM / NOT POLICY EVALUATION**

Parent quarry: COM #365.

## Question under test

After the two-case positive-construction view was shrunk, one residual question remained:

> In a transition presented or intended as beneficial, who could not refuse it, who cannot exit it, and who carries its continuation or maintenance without having chosen to?

The question was retained only for stronger-owner search. It was **not** established as an ME/TRACE-specific contribution.

## Strong owners found

### 1. Evaluation already reaches unintended burdens on affected people

OECD's current guidance on applying the DAC evaluation criteria says significant unexpected or unintended effects are often omitted from monitoring and that evaluators should look beyond formal project documents, listen to people affected positively or negatively, and assess effects whose significance differs across stakeholders.

Owner:
https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/applying-evaluation-criteria-thoughtfully_543e84ed-en/full-report/understanding-the-six-criteria-definitions-elements-for-analysis-and-key-challenges_2843ff7d.html

BetterEvaluation's participation/power guidance explicitly warns that imposed ideas can cause harm and that tokenistic participation can leave affected people without meaningful influence over evaluation/recommendations.

Owner:
https://www.betterevaluation.org/blog/positioning-participation-power-spectrum

This weakens any claim that evaluation simply accepts the intervention's goals and cannot ask who was harmed or excluded.

### 2. Justice frameworks already separate participation from burden distribution

Energy/environmental justice literature has an established three-part structure:

```text
PROCEDURAL JUSTICE
-> who participates / how decisions are made

DISTRIBUTIVE JUSTICE
-> who receives benefits / who bears costs and burdens

RECOGNITION JUSTICE
-> whose needs / identities / knowledge are recognised
```

Representative current/owner surfaces:
- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sustainable-cities/articles/10.3389/frsc.2021.632020/full
- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/energy-research/articles/10.3389/fenrg.2019.00099/full
- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/environmental-economics/articles/10.3389/frevc.2026.1835455/full

This directly occupies much of:

```text
WHO COULD INFLUENCE THE TRANSITION?
WHO BEARS ITS COSTS?
WHO RECEIVES ITS BENEFITS?
WHOSE NEEDS WERE NOT RECOGNISED?
```

### 3. Exit / voice is already a mature analytic owner

Hirschman's exit/voice tradition and later work explicitly distinguish ability to leave from ability to answer back, including cases where constrained exit changes collective voice.

Representative surfaces:
- https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/perspectives-on-politics/article/taking-voice-seriously/09EEDA9520180DAC0709D0742ABC4BE6
- https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/exits-voices-and-social-investment/EF1CFBF47538999EA63EBF6C7EE98B56

Therefore:

```text
CAN_EXIT != CAN_VOICE
EXIT_COST != ZERO
NO_EXIT != NO_AGENCY
MORE_EXIT_OPTIONS != AUTOMATICALLY_BETTER_COLLECTIVE_OUTCOME
```

are not project discoveries.

### 4. Maintenance burden itself is already a real studied failure mode

A concrete infrastructure case from participatory nature-based infrastructure in East Africa reports that local/community management sometimes created higher/additional maintenance responsibilities, costs and risks for communities and CBOs, raising ethical questions about burdens on already disadvantaged groups.

Owner/example:
https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0162243920916235
and:
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sustainable-cities/articles/10.3389/frsc.2022.927723/full

A 2026 Indonesian community climate-program study likewise reports households absorbing material/maintenance costs and volunteers carrying responsibilities without matching institutional resources:
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/communication/articles/10.3389/fcomm.2026.1751626/full

This is close to the exact supposed residue:

```text
BENEFICIAL / RESILIENCE PROJECT
-> NEW CAPABILITY
-> CONTINUATION / MAINTENANCE REQUIRED
-> COST / RESPONSIBILITY SHIFTED TO AFFECTED COMMUNITY
```

## Result

The narrow question remains useful, but the **exclusive project residue is not established**.

```text
PARTICIPATION / VOICE = OWNER FOUND
EXIT / VOICE RELATION = OWNER FOUND
BENEFIT / BURDEN DISTRIBUTION = OWNER FOUND
UNINTENDED NEGATIVE EFFECTS = OWNER FOUND
MAINTENANCE BURDEN = OWNER FOUND IN INFRASTRUCTURE LITERATURE

PROJECT-SPECIFIC POSITIVE-TRANSITION RESIDUE = NOT ESTABLISHED
NEW TRACE PRIMITIVE = NO
NEW ME DOCTRINE = NO
NEW DERIVED NINE-STEP VIEW = NO
```

What survives is narrower and non-proprietary:

> When using ME/TRACE on a transition that is being described as positive, do not let the positive framing suppress ordinary questions about participation, burden distribution, exit/voice, imposed maintenance and unintended effects.

That is a **routing/checking discipline**, not a novel theory.

## Consequence for #365

Drop the claim that the refusability/exit/unchosen-continuation question is the one line established owners lack.

Keep only:

```text
POSITIVE_FRAMING != BURDEN_ABSENT
PARTICIPATION != CONSENT
VOICE != EXIT
PROJECT_COMPLETED != MAINTENANCE_BURDEN_RESOLVED
BENEFIT_TO_GROUP != BURDEN_ACCEPTED_BY_EACH_AFFECTED_SCOPE
```

These may remain useful ME/TRACE reading prompts, but their components are substantially owner-supported.

## Disposition

**OWNER CONVERGENCE / SHRINK AGAIN.**

No further positive-construction framework building is earned from the current cases.

Wake only for:
- a real case where ME/TRACE reveals a consequential cross-owner relation the established methods actually lose;
- or evidence that the portable compression changes a real decision/correction outcome with less burden than ordinary owner methods.

```text
KNOWN_ELSEWHERE != USELESS
COMPRESSION != NOVELTY
PORTABLE_PROMPT != NEW_PRIMITIVE
OWNER_FOUND -> STOP BUILDING THEORY
```
