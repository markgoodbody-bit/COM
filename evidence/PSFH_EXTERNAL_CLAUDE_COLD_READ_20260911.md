**Earned result — provenance discipline.** The art handling is checkable and holds: uncropped viewing copies, Met object number, source JSON, explicit statement that placement is your reading and not the artist's argument. Same for the source-basis commit hashes. This is stricter than most institutional sites manage, and it is the one thing on the page a hostile reader can verify in two minutes and find sound.

**Unresolved problem — the challenge route reproduces the shape of your own example.** Leah and Sam get a portal and a reference number while the room changes. Your challenge route requires a GitHub account, and the on-site discussion is read-only. You disclose this plainly, which is the correct minimum, but disclosure does not dissolve it: the population your central scene is about is the population that cannot reach your reply surface. **Unresolved problem — the page's own record drifts from the page.** The footer reads "Human entrance revised 11 September 2026", then immediately "Preview 0.8.4 · prepared 7 September 2026; wording and navigation updated 8 September 2026." Today's revision is not in the version line. On a project whose thesis is the distance between an institutional record and the thing it describes, the entrance page demonstrates the fault it names. Small, cheap to fix, disproportionately costly if a critic notices it first.

**Wording / structure — disavowal is the page's dominant speech act.** "Practical advantage over careful ordinary reasoning or established methods has not been demonstrated" appears three times on one page, alongside roughly a dozen further negations: not evidence, not neutral, not endorsement, not a prerequisite, no obligation, no consent, existing methods may serve better. Each is individually defensible. Together they crowd out any positive statement of what the project claims, and at this volume the humility stops reading as honesty and starts reading as either anxiety or as a credibility move — which cuts against your stated aim of not steering the reader. Once, prominently, is integrity. Three times is a tell.

Everything else on the page either routes or disclaims; the story is the only place where the material actually does work, and it does it without vocabulary, in three paragraphs, with the two questions doing the lifting. The worked revision is the closest thing you have to evidence on the efficacy question, and it is currently eighth in a list called "Other routes" — buried behind the disclaimer that says no advantage has been demonstrated. If you have a demonstration, however small, stop filing it below the denial.
