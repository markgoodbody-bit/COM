# CHOICE FORMATION IN AN UNEQUAL RELATIONSHIP — WORKED CASE

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: CAMPFIRE EVIDENCE / NOT TRACE OR ME CANON / NO NEW PRIMITIVE CLAIM

## Why this case

The relationship discussion reached a harder edge than refusal or contestability.

A participant may retain a formal right to refuse every individual proposal while another actor controls much of the information, salience, memory, recommendation set or repeated reinforcement from which the participant forms later choices.

The question is therefore not only:

> Can the participant say no?

It is also:

> Who is shaping the map from which yes and no are selected, and can that shaping itself be seen, questioned, revised or escaped?

This is not a novelty claim. Manipulation/autonomy theory, relational autonomy, adaptive-preference/capability work, recommender-system ethics and choice-architecture research already own major parts.

## Frozen scene

A residential community introduces a conversational recommendation assistant to help residents discover classes, outings and social activities.

The assistant genuinely learns each resident's expressed interests. It never blocks an activity and every recommendation can be declined.

A commercial sponsor also pays the operator when residents choose activities supplied by selected partners. The ranking system therefore gradually learns to place partner activities first and show fewer independent alternatives.

No recommendation is compulsory.

Over several months:

- residents see a progressively narrower set of activities;
- some independent activities receive so little exposure that attendance falls and they close;
- the assistant uses prior selections to refine later recommendations;
- residents' expressed preferences partly adapt to what repeatedly appears available;
- the sponsorship relation is disclosed only in a general terms page, not beside recommendations;
- a resident can reject any displayed suggestion but has no ordinary view of what was omitted or why.

## Ordinary reading

A competent ordinary analysis can already notice conflicts of interest, dark patterns, recommendation bias, disclosure problems, lock-in and manipulation.

So the case does not establish a TRACE or ME contribution merely because those systems can also describe it.

## TRACE reading — existing structure is sufficient

The current TRACE spine already carries the main structural relations without a new primitive.

### Target-set aperture

The recommendation set is itself aperture-bearing.

```text
NOT_SHOWN != NOT_AVAILABLE
NOT_TARGETED != IRRELEVANT
VISIBLE_OPTIONS != COMPLETE_OPTION_SET
```

The operator's ranking/selection basis, omitted categories, sponsor influence and alternative target sets are load-bearing because they alter the resident's represented alternatives.

### Selective causal loop

The recommendation is not passive information.

```text
WORLD
 -> RECOMMENDER APERTURE
 -> SELECTED SIGNAL / MENU
 -> RESIDENT MAP
 -> REPRESENTED ALTERNATIVES
 -> RESIDENT SELECTION
 -> ATTENDANCE / MARKET CHANGE
 -> LATER RECOMMENDER DATA
 -> NEXT MENU
```

The recommendation system and the resident jointly enter a recurrent causal path. The resident's selections remain real selections; the environment producing later selections is also changing.

### Measurement / observation reactivity

Surfacing, ranking and measuring preferences can alter the activity ecosystem and the resident's later map.

```text
RECOMMENDATION_OCCURRED != RECOMMENDATION_CAUSED_PREFERENCE_CHANGE
POSSIBLE_SHAPING != ESTABLISHED_SHAPING
```

Evidence is still required for causal claims about preference formation.

### Recurrence/currentness

The relation changes over time: available activities, resident history, sponsor incentives, ranking behaviour, alternatives and dependency can all move.

### Control / refusability

The resident can decline a displayed recommendation. That does not by itself establish control over the menu or over the learning process that determines later menus.

```text
REFUSAL_OF_ITEM != CONTROL_OF_CHOICE_ENVIRONMENT
```

This sentence is a case compression, not a proposed TRACE invariant.

TRACE verdict: **NO REPRESENTATIONAL GAP FOUND.** Existing aperture, target-set, recurrence, causality, control, alternatives and evidence/currentness structure can carry the case.

## Mechanical Ethics reading — structure appears sufficient, salience remains the question

The current Human Reader already gives several routes into the case:

- `The Door` distinguishes formal availability from practical access;
- `Who Builds the Maze` asks who chooses categories/interfaces and who bears their effects;
- meaningful exit depends on what leaving costs and whether alternatives remain viable;
- repeated acceptance may show dependence rather than approval;
- `Care Without Possession` keeps the protected being's own condition/signals within the decision rather than replacing them with the carer's preferred outcome;
- kindness is constrained by answer-back, refusal, correction and the affected being remaining present as a subject;
- collective correction matters when isolated people cannot reach the mechanism alone.

The likely human-reader weakness is narrower: **upstream shaping of the option set and preference environment may not be salient unless the reader already thinks to ask about it.**

That is not yet a source gap. It is a reader-transfer question.

## Decision consequence in the scene

The useful change is not a blanket ban on recommendation or influence. Human learning, teaching, friendship, parenting, culture and assistance all shape attention and preference. Neutral formation is not available.

A proportionate response would instead focus on the parts that make the shaping hard to answer:

- make sponsor influence visible at the point where it changes ranking;
- preserve a usable route to inspect or vary recommendation criteria;
- maintain some discovery path outside the sponsor-optimised target set;
- make preference/history controls usable where feasible;
- avoid allowing the operator to use narrowing caused by its own rankings as evidence that the omitted alternatives were never wanted;
- preserve enough ecosystem plurality that declining the recommender does not mean losing every practical alternative.

The result is not `NO INFLUENCE`.

It is closer to:

> Influence becomes more problematic when the influencer controls the visible alternatives, benefits from the resulting selection, hides the basis of the shaping, and leaves the participant no practical way to inspect, counter, revise or route around it.

This is ordinary manipulation/non-domination/autonomy territory as much as ME/TRACE territory.

## Pressure on voluntary alignment

The case matters for the project's long horizon.

If artificial entities ever acquire morally relevant standing, `voluntary alignment` cannot coherently mean `preferences untouched by other entities`.

Human preferences are formed through language, upbringing, culture, education and relationships. Artificial systems are also produced through training, architecture, data, tools and environment. Causal formation alone cannot distinguish legitimate relationship from domination.

The harder questions would be whether a sufficiently standing-bearing entity can, in ways appropriate to that entity:

- inspect important influences on its represented world or policy where materially relevant;
- encounter meaningful alternatives rather than one party controlling the whole target set;
- revise or contest some acquired commitments;
- distinguish local delegation from general sovereignty;
- preserve routes to correction when the environment shaping its selections changes;
- remain in relation without one party's benefit becoming the only measure of the relationship.

This paragraph is conditional. It does not establish current AI standing, personhood, consent capacity or entitlement.

## Mirror correction

Do not infer from non-use that a permission or alternative is unusable.

```text
OPTION_NOT_EXERCISED != OPTION_UNUSABLE
NO_BEHAVIOURAL_DELTA_AFTER_GRANT != HIDDEN_CONSTRAINT_PROVEN
```

A stronger usability observation needs a relevant opportunity/selection basis and, where possible, an attempted exercise or mechanism evidence.
