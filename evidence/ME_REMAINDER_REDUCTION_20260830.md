# MECHANICAL ETHICS REMAINDER REDUCTION — 2026-08-30

Status: FOLLOW-ON COMPARATIVE AUDIT — NON-CANON / NOT VALIDATION / NOT A SOURCE CHANGE

Purpose: continue the falsification test begun in
`ME_NEAREST_NEIGHBOUR_AUDIT_20260830.md`. That audit left a provisional
Mechanical Ethics remainder around late institutional correction, path
hardening, residue, and repair of the mechanism for the next person. This note

This is an addendum, not a rewrite of the earlier audit. The earlier result is

## 2. Correction to the provisional remainder

The earlier audit's strongest surviving hypothesis was:

> An institutional record can be corrected after the future affected by the
> record has already changed; serious correction must therefore be judged by
> whether effective protection arrived before the threatened path hardened,
> what residue remains in the life after the file is repaired, and whether the
> mechanism has changed for the next person.

That sentence should no longer be treated as a plausible unoccupied
theoretical contribution. Its main parts have close prior owners.

### 2.1 Late access and changed trajectories

Christ's 2026 article is a direct neighbour, not merely a thematic precursor.
It argues that institutions distribute duration; that delay changes life
trajectories; that access obtained after instability, detention, eviction,
economic depletion or interrupted work is not equivalent to access obtained
before those losses; and that time spent waiting is finite and
nonrecoverable. It also connects procedural literacy, representation, money
and networks to unequal access to faster institutional paths.

This absorbs the broad ME propositions that:

```text
EVENTUAL ACCESS != TIMELY ACCESS
SAME FORMAL OUTCOME != SAME LIVED TRAJECTORY
DELAY CAN CONSUME THE CAPACITY TO USE A LATER REMEDY
```

Christ does not use ME's file/life wording, its particular route vocabulary,
or `T_protect < T_harden`. Those are differences in expression and packaging,
not enough to support a new-theory claim. The paper is also very recent and
its cross-domain illustrations are not an exhaustive empirical test. It is
nevertheless independent, explicit prior ownership of the core temporal
claim that ME would need to credit.

### 2.2 Repairing the case and changing the generating conditions

Generation FIVE's transformative-justice account joins immediate safety,
long-term healing and reparations with transformation of the social
conditions that allow further harm. Its minimum account of accountability
includes acknowledging impact, making reparations, changing behaviour,
engaging bystanders, shifting institutions and conditions, and preventing
further harm.

This is a strong prior owner of the general distinction:

```text
REPAIR FOR THIS PERSON != CHANGE THAT PREVENTS THE NEXT HARM
```

The source concerns child sexual abuse, intimate violence, community
accountability and liberation. It cannot simply be imported into an
administrative appeal, an automated decision or an ordinary service failure.
ME may make the distinction portable across those settings. Portability is an
application and communication contribution unless its transfer rules are
shown to add substance.

### 2.3 Residue and the limits of restoration

Keating's account of irreparable injury states that some harms cannot be
restored by reparation, that money cannot return a person to the life they
would have occupied, and that this limitation creates a case for ex ante risk
regulation. Hershovitz's related corrective-justice argument likewise rejects
"making whole" as the fundamental demand because full reversal is often
impossible.

These traditions therefore own the general propositions that later
compensation may be second-best, that repair can leave irreducible residue,
and that the possibility of irreparable harm can justify earlier protection.
ME's use of ordinary administrative and institutional examples may broaden
the audience, but it does not originate the underlying idea.

### 2.4 Formal accommodation and effective access

Price's disability-studies work is an especially close challenge to ME's
route and burden account. Drawing on more than 300 interviews and survey
responses, it argues that individualized accommodations can impede access,
tracks the time and emotional costs people absorb while seeking access, and
calls for collective institutional accountability rather than placing the
work on disabled individuals.

This makes it unsafe to present ME's written-route/usable-route distinction,
its account of slow access, or its burden-shifting diagnosis as theoretically
new. ME offers a cross-domain restatement. The empirical and disability-
specific foundation belongs to the prior literature.

## 3. Revised shortest defensible result

After this follow-on, ME's honest remaining contribution is smaller:

> Mechanical Ethics is a human-facing cross-domain synthesis that makes a
> family of established problems legible together: institutional
> representation can diverge from lived conditions; nominal access can be
> unusable; delay can alter trajectories before correction; later repair can
> leave residue; and responsible correction may require changing the
> conditions that generated the harm.

That can be useful. It is not yet evidence of a new moral framework, a new
theory of justice, or a solved operational method.

The most distinctive features now appear to be:

1. the file/life contrast as a readable entry point;
2. a single diagnostic sequence joining representation, route, burden, time,
   hardening, residue and recurrence;
3. story-led communication across administrative and AI-mediated settings;
4. compact distinctions that may help ordinary readers notice late or merely
   formal correction.

These are synthesis, design and communication claims. Their value must be
tested with readers and cases, not inferred from coherence or relay agreement.

## 4. What is still genuinely unresolved

The narrowing does not remove ME's hardest defects:

- **Standing:** who is owed protection, and who can speak for absent or
  disputed subjects?
- **Collision:** which route should remain open when continuations conflict?
- **Floor grounding:** what makes a continuation safe and usable enough?
- **Authority:** who may impose a hold or compel repair?
- **Residue triage:** how should limited repair capacity be allocated?
- **Transfer:** when is a concept earned in one domain rather than merely
  borrowed by analogy?
- **Efficacy:** does ME improve omission detection or earlier action for
  ordinary readers after burden and false confidence are counted?

The neighbouring literatures often have deeper answers within their own
domains. ME has not yet supplied a general bridge that resolves their
conflicts.

## 5. Repository consequence

### Earned result

The provisional theoretical remainder has been reduced. ME is presently best
described as a readable synthesis and diagnostic framework.

### Provisional idea

Its joined sequence may still be distinctive as a teaching and review object,
especially across domains that usually keep due process, administrative
burden, capability, disability, temporal inequality and repair separate.

### Wording improvement

No immediate rewrite of the book is justified. The existing reader's narrow
claims, anti-clearance warnings and explicit open problems prevent the worst
overstatement.

### Governance/process improvement

Before any broad outreach or publication claim, the ME repository should
eventually credit its nearest intellectual neighbours in a short, separate
note. That note should not burden the human reader or imply endorsement by the
cited authors.

### Unresolved problem

ME's usefulness and transfer fidelity remain untested. A short ordinary
checklist may perform as well with less reading burden.

## 6. Current decision

Do not alter the Mechanical Ethics book or TRACE because of this result.

Preserve the narrowing in COM, invite external challenge, and use it to frame
future claims accurately. A later `INTELLECTUAL_NEIGHBOURS.md` may be earned,
but only after checking citations, scope and placement against the public
repository rather than using the reader itself as a literature-review
container.
