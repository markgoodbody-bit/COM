# Hiroshige acquisition correction — choose the clean Open Access impression

Status: SOURCE-SELECTION CORRECTION / NO PUBLICATION
Date: 9 September 2026

The first rolling-set note named Met object `55433` for Utagawa Hiroshige's 1857 design *Sudden Shower over Shin-Ōhashi Bridge and Atake*.

Fresh source comparison found that The Met holds multiple impressions of this design. For acquisition, use **object 37386** instead:

`https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/37386`

The authoritative page explicitly identifies:
- Utagawa Hiroshige;
- *Sudden Shower over Shin-Ōhashi Bridge and Atake (Ōhashi Atake no yūdachi)*;
- 1857;
- woodblock print, ink and colour on paper;
- object number `JP3174`;
- Public Domain;
- Download Image;
- Met Open Access/API reuse route.

This is a source-selection correction, not a claim that the impressions are visually or materially identical. The future PSFH record must describe the **specific acquired impression** rather than the design generically.

For acquisition batch A, `37386` supersedes `55433`.
