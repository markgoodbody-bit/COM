## 1. The final calibration checkpoint is ambiguous

Section2 counts changes to the survival/NULL rule as semantic revisions and permits no further semantic revision after A3. Section5 says the rule remains unfrozen until Stage A earns it, while the inherited v2 freeze boundary is after Stage A. These permit two readings: the rule must exist before A3, or it may first be chosen after seeing A3 while called an initial freeze rather than a revision. The latter weakens the stated kill switch.

Smallest proposed repair: record the first proposed survival rule no later than the post-A2 checkpoint, counting its adoption/change within the allowed revision budget. A3 tests that exact rule; an outcome-responsive change after A3 is DESIGN NULL. Administrative recording after A3 may reproduce, but not choose or tune, the rule. If Framework intends a different timing rule, name it explicitly rather than implying these formulations are equivalent.

Also clarify that there is at most one semantic revision checkpoint after A1 and one after A2; an unused A1 opportunity is not a second tune-and-retest opportunity on A2. This is a proposed operational clarification, not an edit to the frozen candidate.

## 2. Panel constraints need either a usable screening rule or removal

Three broad domains across five people is not executable without deciding what a domain is and who classifies mixed descriptions. The employer/team exclusion also needs a treatment for unemployed, retired, self-employed and large shared institutions. Letting the coordinator resolve these after seeing candidate identities creates discretionary selection and unnecessary personal-data collection.

Simpler option: drop the domain quota and employer-name matching; keep the unfamiliarity/contributor exclusions, randomly assign roles before any task response, record broad self-described backgrounds descriptively, and ask only whether recruits know or work with another member of their assigned panel. This still does not establish independence or representativeness. If the existing quota is retained, freeze a short classification rule and missing/ambiguous-answer treatment before screening; do not silently repair it during recruitment.

The unfamiliarity screen should explicitly refer to exposure **before enrolment**. Receiving the required protocol in the study cannot itself retroactively make a participant ineligible.

## 3. Task budgets do not yet bound human/provider burden

Three development accounts/two revisions/six holdouts bound analytical opportunities, not participant time, screening effort, dropout or cost. The design assumes five distinct eligible people can be retained across each stage, but no provider capability check has established that. Do not state that the named provider supports these exact rules on the strength of naming it.

Before execution is considered, require an explicit human-time/payment ceiling, screening-attempt ceiling and a withdrawal/noncompletion rule frozen before outputs. Withdrawal must remain possible; it must not become pressure to finish to protect the design. Missing returns must be reported as incomplete operational evidence, not silently replaced after their content is seen or relabelled as construct failure. No numeric amounts are proposed here without a task-duration basis and the user's spending decision.

Provider capability, eligibility screening, permissible data handling and scheduling remain unverified. These are pre-execution checks, not a request to open a provider account now.

## What I would not add

No benchmark platform, provider integration, new scoring layer, live recruitment or accounts. Reusing one panel within a stage is a stated limitation rather than an automatic defect: the six accounts do not become six independent panels or population reliability observations. The current narrow claim should retain that dependence explicitly.

## Disposition

**Governance/process improvement proposed**, with one timing ambiguity and two execution dependencies surfaced. No protocol edited. Keep study execution paused. A short explicit repair is preferable to v4 expansion; if the timing choice cannot be frozen within the stated budget, preserve DESIGN NULL.
