## Question

> Can released ME/TRACE describe a real case in which something became possible or remained alive through attention, learning, practice and new training — without turning that story into a universal good, a causal fairy tale, or a new primitive?

## Owner-supported transition

The current public lineage supports the following bounded sequence:

```text
2009
Mike Turnock publicly described as, to his knowledge, the last British handmade sieve-and-riddle maker

2010
Heritage Crafts reports Turnock retired

2017
Heritage Crafts listed the craft extinct

early 2018
Heritage Crafts says Steve Overthrow began making after encountering the Red List

2019
Heritage Crafts reports two makers had revived the craft and were beginning commercial production

current
Heritage Crafts classifies the craft as critically endangered, not extinct;
reports 0 main-income professional, 1 side-income professional and 1 trainee;
says Overthrow is focusing on training a new maker
```

Heritage Crafts' 2019 Red List also says Overthrow received **advice and tuition from Mike Turnock** and undertook product development/refinement before commercial production.

The THR record independently preserves a published Homo Faber first-person account in which Overthrow says he learned partly from Turnock and received knowledge he could not find through research.

These sources support a reported learning relation. They do not establish the exact content/completeness of transmission or every route by which the craft was relearned.

## Released ME reading

Part VI of Mechanical Ethics already carries the relevant positive-side structure:

- hope is tied to a route that remains practically available and maintainable, not to optimistic feeling;
- skills can move between people;
- somebody who once needed a route can later become part of a route for another;
- viability is more than option count and can remain fragile;
- cooperation can combine capability without certifying the arrangement as good;
- positive language does not prove consent, safety or durable success.

The real craft case therefore does not expose an absent positive primitive.

It supplies a non-composite field witness for a narrower statement:

> one way a future can remain/re-become reachable is through preserved attention, person-to-person learning, practice, refinement and further training — while the result can remain fragile and partially unknown.
