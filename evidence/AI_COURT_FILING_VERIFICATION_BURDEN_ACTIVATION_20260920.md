## Strong owner account already carries

The legal system already provides:
- named signer/filing responsibility;
- independent verification duties;
- duties of competence, candour and professional judgment;
- judicial authority to reject or disregard filings;
- correction and sanctions routes;
- formal procedural clocks;
- explicit separation between AI assistance and transfer of legal/judicial responsibility.

The Florida opinion adds an important current observation:
**real citations can coexist with bad reasoning**.

Federal judiciary guidance independently says:
- core judicial functions should not be delegated to AI;
- AI-generated work should be independently verified;
- the human user remains accountable.
