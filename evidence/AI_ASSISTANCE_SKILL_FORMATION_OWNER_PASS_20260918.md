## Owner evidence

### Shen & Tamkin (2026) — randomized developer skill-formation experiment

Paper:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20245

The authors randomized developers learning an unfamiliar asynchronous programming library with or without AI assistance.

Their reported result:
- AI assistance impaired later conceptual understanding, code reading and debugging in the main experiment;
- average efficiency gain was not significant;
- full delegation could improve task performance for some participants while reducing learning;
- observed interaction patterns with greater cognitive engagement and less reliance preserved stronger learning outcomes.

Important ceiling:
- preprint;
- small/short experiment;
- immediate skill assessment rather than long-horizon professional performance;
- interaction-pattern analysis is observational within the randomized treatment and should not be read as a randomized causal comparison of the six patterns.

### Cruces et al. (NBER 2026) — productivity gap and unassisted follow-up

Paper:
https://www.nber.org/papers/w34851

Randomized online experiment with 1,174 adults on a workplace-style business problem-solving task.

Reported result:
- generative AI improved assisted task performance across education groups and sharply narrowed the observed education-based performance gap;
- a later module removed AI assistance;
- treated participants did not perform worse than controls without AI;
- lower-education participants retained part of the earlier gain, but a sizable education gap re-emerged;
- stronger unassisted follow-up performance was associated with intensive AI use combined with sustained human effort.

Important ceiling:
- working paper;
- one task family / online experiment;
- retention/transfer is bounded to the study's follow-up, not a general proof of durable skill formation.

### Wang et al. (2026) — performance without measured learning

Paper:
https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7282556

Randomized field experiment in introductory accounting and microeconomics courses.

Reported result:
- solution-providing AI and interactive tutoring increased practice performance;
- none of the AI modes improved the immediately following unassisted quiz;
- performance-enhancing modes widened the practice-versus-quiz gap;
- assistance mode changed where students spent effort/time.

Important ceiling:
- SSRN working paper;
- immediate unassisted quiz is not long-term retention;
- different pedagogical designs may produce different results.

### OECD Digital Education Outlook 2026 — stronger synthesis owner

Report:
https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2026/01/oecd-digital-education-outlook-2026_940e0dd8/062a7394-en.pdf

OECD explicitly frames a "false mastery" risk: AI-enabled output can look strong while underlying independent human skill remains weak.

This is the strongest general owner found in this pass for the distinction:
