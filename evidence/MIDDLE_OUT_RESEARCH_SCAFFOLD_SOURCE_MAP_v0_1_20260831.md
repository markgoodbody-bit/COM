## 1. Viability theory — reachability is not viability

**Owner:** Jean-Pierre Aubin / viability theory.  
**Useful structure:** viability kernels; existence of at least one feasible trajectory/control that keeps declared constraints satisfied over time; controlled invariance distinctions.  
**Primary source route:**
- Viability Theory, Springer: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-16684-6
- Viability Kernel definition: https://viability-theory.org/en/node/43
- Example formal treatment connecting viability and controlled invariance: https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sastry/pubs/OldSastryALL/AubinViability.pdf

**Do not infer:** viability = welfare; viability = moral priority; biological viability for every artificial system; one universal constraint set.

---

## 2. Ecological psychology — affordances and organism/environment unit

**Owner:** J. J. Gibson tradition / ecological psychology.  
**Useful structure:** affordances as possibilities for action at the organism-environment level; action possibilities are relational rather than properties of the environment alone.  
**Source routes:**
- Historical/philosophical review with affordance definition: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6280920/
- Organism-environment systems discussion: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7358536/

**Do not infer:** every perceived affordance is valuable; ecological psychology and enactivism are the same theory; organism-environment framing transfers unchanged to every software system.

---

## 3. Enactivism / theoretical biology — autonomy, adaptivity, sense-making

**Owners:** Varela/Thompson/Rosch; Di Paolo; enactive cognition traditions.  
**Useful structure:** adaptive autonomy and sense-making as candidate accounts of how some self-maintaining systems regulate interactions relative to conditions of their own continued organisation.  
**Source routes:**
- Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy overview: https://iep.utm.edu/enactivism/
- Di Paolo & Thompson overview: https://ezequieldipaolo.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/di-paolo-and-thompson-enactive-approach.pdf

**Do not infer:** current LLMs possess intrinsic concern; operational continuity is enactive viability; responsiveness proves consciousness or moral standing.

---

## 4. Capability approach — substantive freedom and conversion conditions

**Owners:** Amartya Sen; Martha Nussbaum; capability-approach literature.  
**Useful structure:** capabilities as real/substantive opportunities rather than mere resources or formal rights; conversion depends on personal, social and environmental conditions.  
**Source route:**
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Capability Approach (2025 revision): https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2025/entries/capability-approach/

**Do not infer:** more capability is always morally better; one human capability list applies to all entities; capability replaces standing/authority questions.

---

## 5. Self-Determination Theory — human development and motivational conditions

**Owners:** Edward Deci, Richard Ryan and SDT research community.  
**Useful structure:** human motivation/development research emphasising autonomy, competence and relatedness as basic psychological supports for high-quality motivation, persistence and well-being.  
**Source route:**
- Official SDT overview: https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/the-theory/

**Do not infer:** autonomy/competence/relatedness are universal primitives for animals, institutions or artificial entities; human empirical findings transfer without type guards.

---

## 6. Niche construction / ecological inheritance — futures are constructed

**Owners:** Odling-Smee, Laland and niche-construction literature.  
**Useful structure:** organisms modify environmental conditions and selection pressures; persistent modifications can become ecological inheritance for descendants and other species.  
**Source routes:**
- Introduction to niche construction theory: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4922671/
- Niche construction, sources of selection and trait coevolution: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5566808/
- Human niche construction / cultural inheritance: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3048995/

**Do not infer:** all downstream causality is niche construction; residue and ecological inheritance are identical; constructed environments are necessarily beneficial.

---

## 7. Shared agency / collective intentionality — coordination is not automatically a WE

**Owners:** philosophy of collective intentionality / shared agency.  
**Useful structure:** shared intention, joint attention, collective acceptance and other forms of genuinely joint activity; distinctions between shared activity and mere aggregation.  
**Source routes:**
- Collective Intentionality, SEP: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/collective-intentionality/
- Shared Agency, SEP: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/shared-agency/

**Do not infer:** collective agency = group consciousness; shared outcome = shared intention; composite system = moral person.

---

## 8. Social choice / judgment aggregation / value pluralism — aggregation has structural limits

**Owners:** Kenneth Arrow and social-choice theory; judgment-aggregation literature; value-pluralism/incommensurability literature.  
**Useful structure:** under stated conditions there is no preference-aggregation rule satisfying all Arrow desiderata; plural judgments/values may resist lossless common ranking; incommensurability does not automatically imply irrationality.  
**Source routes:**
- Arrow's Theorem, SEP: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/arrows-theorem/
- Social Choice Theory, SEP: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/social-choice/
- Incommensurable Values, SEP: https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2026/entries/value-incommensurable/
- Value Pluralism, SEP archive: https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2022/entries/value-pluralism/

**Do not infer:** every collective decision is impossible; no rational choice can be made under plural values; Arrow's theorem applies outside its assumptions without translation.

---

## 9. Mechanism design — a route for disclosure is not incentive compatibility

**Owners:** Leonid Hurwicz, Eric Maskin, Roger Myerson and mechanism-design literature.  
**Useful structure:** institutions/mechanisms must account for private information and incentives; social goals may require rules that make strategic behaviour compatible with desired outcomes.  
**Source routes:**
- Nobel 2007 summary: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2007/summary/
- Maskin lecture, “Mechanism Design: How to Implement Social Goals”: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economics/2007/maskin/lecture/
- Myerson lecture, “Perspectives on Mechanism Design in Economic Theory”: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economics/2007/myerson/lecture/

**Do not infer:** incentive compatibility solves legitimacy or ethics; every relationship should be designed as a mechanism; transparency alone makes a system truthful.

---

## 10. Ostrom / commons / polycentric governance — legitimate collective capability

**Owner:** Elinor Ostrom and commons/polycentric-governance literature.  
**Useful structure:** durable self-governance can emerge through locally fitted rules, monitoring, conflict-resolution routes, participation in rule change, and nested/polycentric arrangements rather than a simple state/market binary.  
**Source routes:**
- Nobel 2009 summary: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/summary/
- Scientific background on economic governance: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/advanced-information/

**Do not infer:** decentralisation is always superior; every commons can self-govern; polycentricity eliminates power or coordination failure.

---

## 11. Resilience / adaptive cycle / panarchy — persistence is not the only adaptive mode

**Owners:** C. S. Holling; Resilience Alliance; resilience/panarchy literature.  
**Useful structure:** resilience, self-organisation, learning/adaptation; adaptive-cycle attention to exploitation/growth, conservation, release and reorganisation; transformation may be necessary rather than a failure to restore the prior state.  
**Source routes:**
- Resilience: https://www.resalliance.org/resilience
- Adaptive Cycle: https://resalliance.org/adaptive-cycle

**Do not infer:** destruction/release is automatically good; every social system follows one ecological cycle; resilience = moral desirability.

---

## 12. Distributed cognition / cognitive ecology — cognition can be system-distributed without identity collapse

**Owner:** Edwin Hutchins / distributed cognition / cognitive ecology.  
**Useful structure:** cognitive phenomena may depend on dynamic patterns across people, artefacts and environments; choosing the correct unit of analysis is itself a central problem.  
**Source route:**
- Hutchins, “Cognitive Ecology” (2010): https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1756-8765.2010.01089.x

**Do not infer:** distributed cognition = one subject; external records = subjective memory; system-level cognition proves personhood or continuous experience.

---

## 13. Major evolutionary transitions / individuality — individuation can be a transition

**Owners:** Maynard Smith & Szathmáry tradition; West, Fisher, Gardner, Kiers and evolutionary-individuality literature.  
**Useful structure:** distinguish formation of a cooperative group from transformation into a more integrated higher-level individual; relevant features include division of labour, communication, mutual dependence and reduced within-group conflict.  
**Source routes:**
- Major evolutionary transitions in individuality: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4547252/
- Human cooperation and evolutionary transitions in individuality: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9869453/
- Conflict and conflict resolution in major transitions: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10565403/

**Do not infer:** human teams are evolutionary individuals; higher-level causal integration = moral personhood; cooperation alone proves individuation.

---

## 14. Cybernetics / requisite variety / good regulator theorem — compression and response capacity

**Owner:** W. Ross Ashby; Roger Conant & W. Ross Ashby.  
**Useful structure:** under Ashby's regulatory setup, a regulator's capacity is limited by its variety/channel capacity; Conant-Ashby proves a modelling result for regulators that are maximally successful and simple under the theorem's assumptions.  
**Source routes:**
- Ashby, *An Introduction to Cybernetics* (Requisite Variety chapter): https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf
- Conant & Ashby, “Every good regulator of a system must be a model of that system”: https://doi.org/10.1080/00207727008920220

**Do not infer:** model everything; high detail = high regulatory competence; the Good Regulator theorem justifies a universal philosophical claim outside its assumptions.

---

## 15. Intergenerational justice / nonidentity — future action can change who exists

**Owner:** population ethics / intergenerational-justice literature.  
**Useful structure:** present choices can affect the identity/composition of future populations, creating cases where ordinary same-person counterfactual harm comparisons become difficult or ill-typed.  
**Source route:**
- Intergenerational Justice, SEP: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/justice-intergenerational/

**Do not infer:** future generations have no claims; nonidentity resolves practical policy choices; all future-scope comparisons are impossible.

# Source-return rule

When a scaffold question becomes load-bearing, return to the strongest relevant owner rather than treating the scaffold's paraphrase as sufficient.

Examples:

```text
formal viability claim -> viability theory / declared dynamics + constraints
collective aggregation claim -> social-choice / judgment-aggregation owner
strategic truthfulness claim -> mechanism-design / game-theoretic owner
AI intrinsic normativity claim -> do not infer; require separate evidence/theory
higher-level individuality claim -> major-transitions / individuation evidence
requisite-variety claim -> Ashby assumptions and exact regulator/disturbance setup
```

The scaffold should function as a **router to stronger knowledge**, not as a substitute for it.
