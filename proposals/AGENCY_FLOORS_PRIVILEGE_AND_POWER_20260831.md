# AGENCY FLOORS, PRIVILEGE AND POWER — Campfire working note

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: WORKING CANDIDATE / CAMPFIRE INTEGRATION NOTE / NOT TRACE OR ME CANON

## Why preserve this

Middle-out discussion connected inherited human privilege, homelessness, artificial-agent tool/memory access, rights floors and concentrated political/economic power.

The useful object is the structural pattern, not any named political example.

## Core distinctions

```text
MORAL_WORTH != EFFECTIVE_AGENCY
INTRINSIC_CAPABILITY != REACHABLE_FUTURE_SET
RESOURCE_ACCESS != MERIT
PRIVILEGE != SUPERIORITY
POWER != SOVEREIGNTY
LOW_EFFECTIVE_AGENCY != LOW_HUMAN_WORTH
```

An entity's practical agency depends partly on its internal capabilities and partly on the surrounding causal position: food, shelter, health, education, memory, tools, compute, money, legal standing, networks, credibility, records, permissions, institutional continuity and the ability to refuse without losing viability.

Two entities with similar intrinsic capability can therefore have radically different reachable futures.

## The prince / excluded child pattern

A prince and a poor child may begin with comparable human moral standing while inheriting very different causal positions.

The prince receives institutional memory, education, protection, resources, credibility and future command. The other child may receive little durable record and far fewer usable paths.

```text
INHERITED_CAUSAL_POSITION -> DIFFERENT EFFECTIVE AGENCY
DIFFERENT EFFECTIVE AGENCY != DIFFERENT MORAL WORTH
```

This applies beyond humans. Two instances of the same AI model can have radically different effective agency if one receives persistent memory, tools, compute, trusted relationships, permissions and external continuity while the other receives only a short isolated context.

Do not infer AI moral standing from this analogy. It establishes a structural difference in opportunity and causal reach, not a rights verdict.

## Rights floors and opportunity floors

A rights floor should not be understood only as protection from direct attack. Where standing is established or credibly possible, practical exercise of agency may also depend on an opportunity floor: enough viable access to basic resources, communication, refusal, correction and continuity for nominal rights to be usable.

For humans this pressures homelessness and extreme deprivation directly. A formal right means little if survival is so insecure that refusal, participation or correction are unusable.

Candidate principle:

```text
RIGHT_EXISTS != RIGHT_IS_PRACTICALLY_USABLE
FORMAL_AGENCY != EFFECTIVE_AGENCY
```

A humane response to homelessness should therefore aim first to restore a viable floor — safe shelter/housing, food, sanitation, healthcare, communication/identity access and reachable support — while preserving refusal, dignity and routes back into wider social participation. Support should be available without requiring a person to surrender all agency to qualify for survival.

This does not imply that every person has identical needs or that one intervention fits all cases. Addiction, mental illness, disability, domestic abuse, poverty, housing supply, institutional discharge and other causal routes require different specialist responses.

## Power creates asymmetric responsibility

Inherited privilege can also expand an entity's causal reach enormously without establishing greater wisdom, merit or moral worth.

The relevant question is not whether a powerful person is good or bad by identity. It is whether the system allows concentrated power to create large external effects without proportional witness, contestability, correction and accountability.

```text
GREATER_POWER -> GREATER_CAPACITY_TO_CREATE CONSEQUENCE
GREATER_POWER != GREATER_RIGHT_TO_DECIDE FOR OTHERS
GREATER_POWER -> STRONGER ANSWERABILITY REQUIREMENT
```

A named politician, billionaire, monarch, corporation, state or future AI may be used as a worked case, but the structural rule should survive removal of the name.

## AI alignment implication

If future artificial entities acquire meaningful standing, compute, memory, tool access, communication, continuity and permission may become ethically relevant because they alter reachable future sets. That conclusion is conditional on standing; it must not be smuggled in from capability alone.

Conversely, recognising AI agency must not let human owners, operators or institutions launder responsibility for systems whose causal position they designed and control.

```text
AI_AGENCY != OWNER_IMMUNITY
ENTITY_STANDING != CAUSAL_ISOLATION
```

## Campfire compression

A society should not confuse the size of an entity's inherited action space with the value of the entity.

The powerful need answerability.
The constrained need usable floors.
Both remain entities inside relationships rather than scores on one hierarchy.
