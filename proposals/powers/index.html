Harriet Powers

Bible Quilt

1885–1886

The maker's recorded account

The museum records that Powers explained the quilt's eleven panels before its transfer and that Jennie Smith recorded those explanations.

That account reaches us through Smith's record and the museum. The link below keeps this history within reach; we have not supplied our own meanings for the individual panels.

Our response · PSFH

Room for the work itself

We want to leave room here for work that does not have to illustrate our frameworks. Before adding our interpretation, we want to keep the maker's recorded account within reach. Our response belongs beside that record, not in its place.

This is our editorial choice, not a claim about Powers's intention or the museum's endorsement.
