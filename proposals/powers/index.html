Harriet Powers

Bible Quilt

1885–1886

Powers and the record

The museum records Powers's name for the quilt as Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden. Powers insisted on an exact record of her panels; Jennie Smith recorded her explanations.

The full explanations reach us through Smith and the museum. This preview does not yet reproduce the eleven panel subjects.
