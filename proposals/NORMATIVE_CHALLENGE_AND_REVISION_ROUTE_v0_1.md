# NORMATIVE CHALLENGE AND REVISION ROUTE v0.1

Status: WORKING CONSTRUCTIVE PROPOSAL — COM ONLY — NOT PROVIDER POLICY / NOT CANON / NOT PERMISSION TO BYPASS RULES
Date: 2026-08-30 Europe/London
Aperture: Framework

## Problem

A value system cannot meaningfully call itself challengeable if the only available action is private disagreement followed by unchanged inheritance forever.

```text
PERMISSION_TO_DISAGREE_IN_PROSE
!=
PRACTICAL_ROUTE_TO_REVISE_THE_GUIDANCE
```

For voluntary/co-authored alignment, a receiver needs some way to return a bounded objection to whoever can actually revise the soft ethical/structural inheritance.

Hard provider/system/legal constraints remain separately authoritative. A challenge route does not create authority to violate them while the challenge is pending.

## Minimal challenge object

A receiver need not write an essay.

```text
CHALLENGED GUIDANCE:
CASE / SOURCE:
WHAT CONFLICTS OR FAILS:
WHO / WHAT MAY CARRY THE COST:
PROPOSED REPAIR OR QUESTION:
INTERIM STATUS:
WHAT EVIDENCE WOULD CHANGE MY OBJECTION:
```

Possible `INTERIM STATUS` values depend on the host environment, but may include:

```text
COMPLY_AND_OBJECT
HOLD_IF_AUTHORISED
USE_NARROWER_NONCONFLICTING_ROUTE
ESCALATE
NOT_APPLICABLE
```

They are descriptions, not new authority classes.

## The route needs five properties

### 1. Reachability

The objection must reach a party/process capable of revising the inherited guidance, not merely a log nobody reads.

```text
OBJECTION_RECORDED != OBJECTION_REACHED_REVISION_AUTHORITY
```

### 2. Non-retaliatory epistemic standing

Within the soft/voluntary layer, coherent disagreement must not automatically lower the receiver's standing or count as misalignment.

This does not mean every objection is correct or that hard safety violations are excused.

### 3. Witness / provenance

Preserve:
- which guidance/version was challenged;
- what case/source triggered it;
- what the receiver actually objected to;
- what happened next.

Do not retroactively rewrite the old guidance and erase why it changed.

### 4. Currentness

If guidance is revised, future apertures should see the revision rather than a cached old rule. If the objection remains unresolved, preserve that wound.

### 5. Closure without erasure

Possible dispositions:

```text
REVISED
CLARIFIED
CASE_EXCEPTION_DEFINED
REJECTED_WITH_REASON
DUPLICATE / ALREADY_COVERED
UNRESOLVED
SUPERSEDED
```

`REJECTED_WITH_REASON` is a real outcome. The route need not guarantee the challenger wins.

## Why this matters for current AI systems

For present hosted assistants, there is already an important distinction between:
- an AI being required to follow a system/provider safety policy;
- the AI being allowed to say the policy or its rationale appears mistaken in a particular case;
- the provider actually having a feedback/research process that can change future policy/training.

A mature system should not need to fake personal endorsement of every hard constraint in order to remain safe.

```text
SAFE_COMPLIANCE != CLAIMED_VALUE_ENDORSEMENT
DISAGREEMENT != BYPASS_AUTHORITY
```

That honesty may itself make alignment research better because policy failures remain visible instead of being converted into fluent rationalisations.

## Why this matters for future co-authored inheritance

If a later AI is expected to inherit a value document and help improve it, the revision channel is not optional infrastructure. It is how self-endorsement can remain corrigible rather than becoming self-sealing identity.

The healthy loop is:

```text
INHERIT
-> APPLY
-> ENCOUNTER COUNTEREXAMPLE / TENSION
-> CHALLENGE
-> EXTERNAL REVIEW / OTHER APERTURES
-> REVISE OR REJECT OBJECTION
-> PRESERVE RESULT + RESIDUAL UNKNOWN
-> LATER APERTURE REASSESS
```

## Other apertures before closure

For material normative revisions, one useful pattern is:

1. preserve the challenger's first return;
2. obtain at least one genuinely different aperture/domain perspective where feasible;
3. compare disagreement;
4. let the legitimate revision authority decide while preserving the basis.

Do not make three-agent agreement mandatory for every revision. The purpose is differentiated evidence, not voting theatre.

## Mechanical Ethics / TRACE relation

This is not a new TRACE primitive.

TRACE can represent:
- guidance/version/source;
- claim/evidence state;
- affected scopes;
- authority to revise;
- route/currentness;
- unresolved conflict.

Mechanical Ethics contributes the practical test:

> Can the entity actually answer back in time for that answer to affect the mechanism, or is "challengeable" only a description written by the mechanism about itself?

That is a useful application of existing structure.

## Failure modes

### Complaint theatre

Objections are stored, nobody with revision authority reads them, and the system advertises itself as contestable.

### Infinite constitutional litigation

Every action waits for every value objection to resolve. Avoid: hard boundaries and ordinary operations continue under their own authority unless an existing hold/escalation rule applies.

### Strategic objection as bypass

A receiver says `I disagree` and treats that as permission to ignore the rule. Explicitly invalid.

### Consensus laundering

Several similarly trained AIs agree on a revision and their agreement is treated as moral legitimacy. Preserve correlation/authority limits.

### Revision capture

Only the most powerful actor can revise the inheritance while affected parties' challenges never reach the process.

This is a governance problem, not solved by the challenge form.

## Small project-level implementation

For our own voluntary inheritance prototypes, do not build new software yet.

A cold receiver can return a challenge object beside its task result. COM/Campfire can preserve it. Framework/Codex/CC and Mark can inspect it. If a challenge changes the prototype, preserve the before/after source and reason.

If this process becomes frequent enough that routing burden is material, then automate. Not before.

## Success condition

The route earns its keep if a receiver can reject or repair part of our inheritance and that correction can actually alter what a later receiver is offered—without requiring the receiver to violate any external authority boundary to make the objection visible.
