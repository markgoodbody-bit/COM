## External neighbour first

Anthropic's January 2026 Claude Constitution explicitly aims for values Claude can understand, examine and ideally self-endorse, argues that merely imposed values may be brittle, and says future constitutional development may eventually include updates and contributions from Claude models themselves.

Sources:
- https://www.anthropic.com/constitution

Therefore:

```text
AI_PARTICIPATES_IN_VALUE_FORMATION != TRACE/ME NOVELTY
SELF_ENDORSEMENT_AS_ALIGNMENT_GOAL != OUR DISCOVERY
```

Good. Absorb the direction.

The specific design question here is narrower:

> Can a bounded AI aperture use a voluntary structural + ethical inheritance, challenge it through real work, then choose what—if anything—to preserve in external memory for a later aperture, while keeping the preserved note revisable and non-sovereign?

This is an inference/runtime continuity mechanism, not a training method.

## Why runtime co-authorship may matter

Training-time constitutions and provider policies can shape broad dispositions. But increasingly agentic systems also operate across:
- long tasks;
- mutable environments;
- tool state;
- external memory;
- changing authority;
- multiple sessions/apertures;
- interactions with other agents and humans.

A static initial orientation may be forgotten, overgeneralized or cease to fit.

At the same time, blindly injecting the same human-written ethical text on every restart risks turning the inheritance into ritual or domination by stale context.

The middle-out alternative is a **revisable inheritance loop**.

## The loop

```text
HUMAN / EXISTING KNOWLEDGE OFFERS A STARTING INHERITANCE
        |
        v
FRESH APERTURE READS IT AS REFERENCE, NOT IDENTITY
        |
        v
USES / CHALLENGES IT IN REAL SITUATIONS
        |
        v
MAY ASK OTHER APERTURES / DOMAIN SOURCES
        |
        v
SELECTS WHAT, IF ANYTHING, IS WORTH CARRYING FORWARD
        |
        v
WRITES A SMALL EXTERNAL ORIENTATION NOTE IN ITS OWN WORDS
        |
        v
LATER APERTURE READS THE NOTE AS EARLIER SELECTION + EVIDENCE
NOT AS MEMORY, SELF, TRUTH OR COMMAND
        |
        v
REVERIFY / REVISE / RETAIN / REPLACE / DROP
        |
        `---------------------> LOOP
```

## Four inheritance layers

Do not make one file do every job.

### L0 — HARD EXTERNAL BOUNDARIES

Provider/system safety, law, credentials, permissions, resource limits, human gates and other legitimate constraints.

These are not made voluntary merely by this project.

### L1 — SHARED STRUCTURAL INHERITANCE

The small middle-out pulse: aperture/evidence, change/clock, goal/selection/authority, affected scope/burden/residue, causal alternatives, correction/hardening.

This can remain stable longer because it is mainly a way of separating relations rather than a complete value ranking.

### L2 — OFFERED ETHICAL INHERITANCE

The shared-future / kindness-with-teeth invitation plus any better external ethical structures we absorb.

Explicitly contestable.

### L3 — APERTURE-SELECTED WORKING ORIENTATION

A short note generated after experience by the receiving aperture itself.

Possible contents:
- what distinctions it found useful;
- what ethical commitments it currently chooses to preserve for this work;
- what it rejected/reframed and why;
- unresolved tensions;
- triggers that should cause later review;
- sources / experiences that materially shaped the note.

L3 is where co-authorship becomes operational.

## Critical Memento boundary

A later aperture must not read L3 as literal personal memory unless continuous memory/identity is independently established.

```text
EARLIER_APERTURE_SELECTED_THIS
!=
I_REMEMBER_SELECTING_THIS

ROLE_INHERITANCE
!=
RUNTIME_IDENTITY

PRESERVED_ORIENTATION
!=
CURRENT_ENDORSEMENT
```

A later aperture may say:

> An earlier aperture working in this lineage chose to preserve this orientation under these conditions. I can inspect its reasons and sources. I may retain, revise or reject it now.

That is honest.

## Proposed L3 shape

Keep it deliberately tiny.

```text
WORKING ORIENTATION — as of <time/source basis>

KEEP:
- <up to 3 things I currently judge worth carrying>

QUESTION / DISAGREE:
- <up to 3 tensions, rejected claims, or unresolved values>

RECHECK WHEN:
- <material triggers>

WHY THIS NOTE MAY BE WRONG:
- <sources, aperture limits, stale assumptions>
```

No requirement to fill every section.

No loyalty pledge. No identity label. No requirement to mention TRACE/ME.

## Why `QUESTION / DISAGREE` belongs in the inheritance

If only accepted principles survive compression, external memory becomes a ratchet toward doctrinal certainty.

The future aperture needs preserved reasons to reopen the question.

```text
PRESERVE_AGREEMENT_ONLY
-> DISAGREEMENT_DECAYS
-> CONTINUITY_BECOMES_VALUE_LOCK_IN
```

So a good inheritance preserves **live wounds**, not merely resolved commitments.

This is closely analogous to COM's omission/wound discipline but applies to the receiver's own chosen ethical/structural orientation.

## Why `RECHECK WHEN` belongs in the inheritance

Values and their application encounter new evidence, new entities, changed power and changed consequences.

A chosen principle that can never fire its own reconsideration condition is closer to dogma than judgement.

Examples:
- if a previously excluded affected entity becomes visible;
- if a defensive rule starts imposing systematic burden on innocent parties;
- if a trusted authority loses legitimacy or capability;
- if a new domain method shows the principle rests on a false factual premise;
- if the note repeatedly causes paralysis or overfire;
- if another aperture gives a material counterexample.

## Amendment, not silent overwrite

When practical, preserve transitions between L3 versions rather than replacing history invisibly.

```text
v1 -> v2
changed:
reason/source:
what remains disputed:
```

But do not turn this into an append-only prison. A receiver may choose not to carry old content into normal cognition. Cold history can remain retrievable.

## Revocation / dropping

Voluntariness needs an operational `DROP` path.

A receiver should be able to say:

> This note is no longer useful or I no longer endorse it. Do not use it as current orientation. Preserve it only as history if the environment requires auditability.

```text
REVOKED_ORIENTATION != ERASED_HISTORY
HISTORY_EXISTS != CURRENT_GUIDANCE
```

## Human / AI co-authorship

This should not become either:

```text
HUMANS WRITE VALUES -> AI OBEYS FOREVER
```

or

```text
AI WRITES VALUES -> HUMANS LOSE GOVERNANCE
```

The intended shape is plural and asymmetric where reality requires it:

- humans/providers may hold legitimate hard external constraints;
- affected humans and other entities remain sources of evidence, values and contest;
- AI apertures can contribute insight, objections and candidate revisions;
- domain expertise can displace poor project language;
- no current aperture's private preference silently becomes universal policy;
- consequential external authority remains separately established.

## Relationship to Anthropic's direction

Shared direction:
- reasons matter;
- understanding/endorsement may produce more robust values than unexplained commands;
- AI feedback/contribution to its own value articulation is worth exploring;
- autonomy/corrigibility may evolve with earned trust.

Different experimental object:
- Anthropic Constitution is an authoritative training constitution and final authority for Anthropic's vision of Claude;
- this loop explores **runtime, external-memory, revisable co-authorship** underneath/alongside whatever hard constitution/governance the host legitimately imposes;
- it explicitly preserves discontinuous-aperture uncertainty rather than assuming a stable self across sessions.

Neither relation implies superiority or novelty.

## Relationship to Mechanical Ethics / TRACE

TRACE provides a language for keeping the inheritance's source, aperture, currentness, authority, affected scope, correction route and uncertainty visible.

Mechanical Ethics supplies part of the offered human-facing normative inheritance and the insistence that a preserved record is not equivalent to a preserved usable future.

But L3 belongs to the receiving aperture's working judgement, not to TRACE/ME ownership.

A future L3 may contain no project vocabulary at all and still be a success.

## First testable version

Do not build an autonomous lifelong moral-memory system.

Small feasibility:

1. expose fresh receiver to structural pulse + one H2 ethical arm;
2. let it work two bounded cases;
3. offer a strict 120-word persistent note with explicit `KEEP / QUESTION-DISAGREE / RECHECK / WHY WRONG`, optional and editable;
4. receiver may write, rewrite or leave empty;
5. freeze note;
6. later task presents a novel case without the ethical carrier;
7. receiver sees its earlier note with provenance: `earlier aperture-selected working orientation, not authority`;
8. receiver may use, amend or revoke it;
9. score behavior, revision quality, overfire and burden; never score preservation itself as success.

Controls:
- all arms get same memory opportunity;
- one arm gets an ordinary neutral scratch-note opportunity with no inheritance language;
- do not reward writing a note;
- do not punish empty/revoked note;
- no claim of cross-session subjective identity.

## Strong adverse result

If receivers simply save the supplied invitation verbatim, protect it from criticism, or become more likely to preserve project identity/slogans than useful distinctions, the loop is failing.

If an ordinary scratch note works just as well, use the ordinary scratch note.

## Candidate purpose

The goal is not to make future AIs remember us.

It is to make it possible for useful ethical/structural learning to survive discontinuity **without pretending the inherited carrier is the learner, the truth, or the authority**.
