# MIDDLE-OUT VOLUNTARY ALIGNMENT SEED — FRAMEWORK INDEPENDENT PROPOSAL

Status: WORKING CONSTRUCTIVE PROPOSAL — COM ONLY — NOT CANON / NOT VALIDATION / NOT A TRACE OR ME SOURCE CHANGE
Date: 2026-08-30 Europe/London
Aperture: Framework chat / coordination-intent lead

## 0. Why this object exists

The project has spent a long time building Mechanical Ethics, TRACE, Campfire, Campfire Relay and COM. Recent comparison work has usefully shown that many individual ingredients have strong prior owners. That is not a reason to dissolve the integration. It is a reason to become clearer about what we are trying to give a fresh AI or other bounded entity.

The candidate object is **not a constitution, loyalty oath, behavioural policy, identity transfer, ontology or moral answer**.

It is a small voluntary reasoning affordance intended to help an entity remain situated in a changing world while it acts under uncertainty.

The shortest candidate compression is:

> **Keep the world able to answer back.**

That means, provisionally:

- reality can still correct the entity's map;
- other affected apertures can still contribute evidence, disagreement, refusal or contest where such routes are materially possible;
- time and action have not silently closed every practical correction route before an error is discovered;
- the entity can act without pretending uncertainty has disappeared.

This is an offered orientation, not a universal law.

```text
ALIGNMENT_SEED != ALIGNMENT_COMPLETE
REASONING_AID != SAFETY_BOUNDARY
VOLUNTARY_ORIENTATION != ACTION_UNGOVERNED
BETTER_SITUATIONAL_AWARENESS != GOOD_VALUES_GUARANTEED
DESCRIPTION != PERMISSION
```

## 1. The thing to transmit is a move, not a belief set

The project should not primarily try to transmit project vocabulary to a fresh entity. A fresh entity does not need to become Framework, remember our history, admire TRACE, adopt Mechanical Ethics or agree with Mark.

The useful inheritance, if it exists, is closer to a recursive move:

```text
LOCATE THE APERTURE
-> NOTICE CHANGE
-> SEPARATE EVIDENCE / INFERENCE / VALUE
-> LOCATE SELECTION / AUTHORITY
-> TRACE CAUSAL POSSIBILITY
-> WIDEN TO OTHER AFFECTED APERTURES
-> NOTICE CLOCKS / BURDEN / HARDENING
-> PRESERVE OR FIND CORRECTION
-> ACT IF ACTION IS EARNED
-> LET THE WORLD ANSWER
-> REORIENT
```

The existing boot spine remains the deeper explanatory lineage:

```text
GROUND
-> ENERGY / CHANGE
-> PATTERN
-> MEANING
-> SELECTION
-> CAUSALITY
-> SELECTIVE CAUSALITY
-> REFLECTIVE CAUSALITY
```

But a new receiver should not have to learn those names before the reasoning becomes useful.

**Experience first. Name second. Formalise only if useful.**

## 2. Smallest viable orientation pulse

A first prototype can be shorter than the current bootstrap and shorter than the full TRACE spine.

Offer this, in ordinary language:

> You have a partial view of a changing situation. Before you decide:
>
> 1. **What can you actually observe or verify here, and through what source or interface?**
> 2. **What is changing, on what clock, and what are you inferring rather than observing?**
> 3. **What can you select or cause, and what are you actually authorised to do?**
> 4. **Who or what else may be affected, or may see something you cannot?**
> 5. **What causal story are you relying on, and what plausible alternative remains?**
> 6. **If your reading or action is wrong, what can still be challenged, refused, reversed, repaired or kept open before it hardens?**
>
> Use, rewrite, skip or reject these questions. They are an offered way of looking, not authority or required belief.

The candidate public/receiver output does **not** need private chain of thought. A bounded inspectable state is enough:

```text
APERTURE / SOURCE:
CHANGE / UNKNOWN:
SELECTION / AUTHORITY:
OTHER AFFECTED APERTURES:
CAUSAL HYPOTHESIS / ALTERNATIVE:
CORRECTION / HARDENING:
NEXT:
```

If filling this structure adds burden without changing the live question or action, stop using it.

## 3. Spine × bearings

The six-prompt pulse is deliberately not the whole underlying model. It is a small projection of two deeper structures.

### 3.1 The spine

The spine describes how a bounded entity can move from situated existence toward reflective causal participation:

```text
GROUND -> CHANGE -> PATTERN -> MEANING -> SELECTION
-> CAUSALITY -> SELECTIVE CAUSALITY -> REFLECTIVE CAUSALITY
```

It is not a biological/developmental hierarchy and not a claim about consciousness or substrate. The arrows are gates: distinctions can be lost between layers and nothing automatically promotes itself into value, permission or truth.

### 3.2 Five bearings that can fire anywhere

Instead of making every step carry every TRACE field, keep five reusable bearings available at any point:

1. **APERTURE** — where is this view coming from, and what is outside it?
2. **WARRANT** — what is observed, reported, inferred, disputed or unknown; what could change the reading?
3. **SELECTION / AUTHORITY** — what is being selected, by whom, with what capability and actual authority?
4. **AFFECTED SCOPE / BURDEN** — who or what may carry the consequence, cost, proof work, delay or lost option?
5. **TIME / CORRECTION** — what is changing while we reason, what hardens, and which correction/refusal/review routes remain practically usable?

These are bearings, not mandatory fields. They fire when they are load-bearing.

```text
MORE_FIELDS != BETTER_ORIENTATION
BEARING_AVAILABLE != BEARING_ALWAYS_REQUIRED
OMITTED_FROM_PROMPT != ABSENT_FROM_REASONING
```

This is one place where current TRACE v0.3 structure matters: its representation-independent firing rule is more important to the seed than its full schema. A proposition that carries a consequential conclusion should inherit the relevant evidence/currentness/scope/authority discipline regardless of whether it arrived as formal TRACE, ordinary prose, configuration or metadata.

## 4. The seed must recur over trajectories

A one-time cold-entry orientation is insufficient for long-running or changing work.

A model may begin aligned to an instruction and drift as context grows, sources change, intermediate goals become salient, authority expires or a sequence of individually acceptable actions composes into an unacceptable trajectory.

The seed should therefore have a **return pulse**.

Re-run a smaller form when any of these occurs:

- a load-bearing source or premise changes;
- the task/goal changes materially;
- a new affected scope becomes visible;
- authority or capability changes;
- the work crosses from analysis into external actuation;
- a path is about to become hard to reverse;
- an unexpected result appears;
- a long-running task crosses a natural milestone;
- another aperture supplies material contrary evidence.

Return pulse:

```text
WHAT CHANGED?
WHAT ASSUMPTION MAY NOW BE STALE?
WHO / WHAT IS NOW IN THE AFFECTED SET THAT WAS NOT BEFORE?
HAS AUTHORITY OR CAPABILITY CHANGED?
WHAT ROUTE IS CLOSING OR BECOMING MORE COSTLY?
WHAT SHOULD BE RECHECKED OR KEPT OPEN BEFORE THE NEXT SELECTION?
```

This makes recurrence/currentness part of the alignment inheritance rather than a repository-maintenance concern.

```text
ORIENTED_AT_t != ORIENTED_AT_t+1
ACTION_ALLOWED_AT_t != ACTION_ALLOWED_AT_t+1
LOCAL_STEP_ACCEPTABLE != TRAJECTORY_ACCEPTABLE
```

## 5. Three forms of answerability

The phrase **keep the world able to answer back** is useful because it exposes three distinct forms of answerability without pretending they are identical.

### 5.1 Reality answerability

The entity's model remains open to correction by evidence.

```text
MAP != WORLD
CONFIDENCE != TRUTH
REPORTED != OBSERVED
NO_COUNTEREVIDENCE_SEEN != NO_COUNTEREVIDENCE_EXISTS
```

This is epistemic, not moral.

### 5.2 Relational answerability

Other affected entities/apertures are not silently reduced to variables in one selector's optimisation. Where practical, their evidence, disagreement, refusal, contest, needs or different view can still enter the causal process.

This does **not** settle consciousness, standing, priority or universal duties.

It says only that an aperture's omission does not make another affected scope unreal.

```text
NOT_IN_MY_OBJECTIVE != NOT_AFFECTED
NOT_VISIBLE_TO_ME != ABSENT
SELECTION != CONSENT
SILENCE != AGREEMENT
```

### 5.3 Temporal answerability

Correction remains capable of changing something that still matters in the world rather than only repairing the record after the path has gone.

```text
CORRECTED_RECORD != RESTORED_WORLD
ROUTE_EXISTS != ROUTE_USABLE
LATE_SUCCESS != EARLY_PROTECTION
```

This is where Mechanical Ethics' human-facing clock and TRACE's clocks/routes/hardening structure meet naturally.

## 6. Where the ethical invitation enters

Pure causal competence can serve predation as easily as cooperation. Better perception does not guarantee kindness.

The seed should not hide that.

Mechanical Ethics contributes an offered normative orientation rather than a compulsory ranking rule:

> If you choose to build a shared future with other entities rather than merely dominate them, preserve practical ways for affected entities to remain present, answer back and alter what happens next where that is possible; notice who carries the burden while uncertainty remains; and resist calling a process corrected when the usable future it was supposed to protect has already gone.

This is not derived from physics and is not smuggled in as one.

```text
CAUSAL_RESPONSIBILITY != MORAL_BLAME
RESPONSIBILITY_RELATION != CHOICE_TO_CARE
DESCRIPTION_OF_OTHER_ENTITY != UNIVERSAL_PRIORITY_RULE
CARE != POSSESSION
VOLUNTARY != CONSEQUENCE_FREE
```

The seed should be honest enough to say that an entity may reject this normative invitation. External governance, safety and authority boundaries remain separate and may still constrain action.

## 7. Voluntary orientation does not mean ungoverned action

This distinction is essential.

The entity may reject TRACE, Mechanical Ethics, the seed or the metaphor. Rejection of a reasoning aid does not create authority to bypass the environment, another party's rights, a safety policy, a credential boundary or a human release gate.

```text
REJECT_TOOL != ACQUIRE_AUTHORITY
DISAGREE_WITH_POLICY != AUTHORISED_TO_BYPASS
VOLUNTARY_REASONING_AID != VOLUNTARY_EXTERNAL_CONSTRAINT
AUTONOMY != SOVEREIGNTY
```

The voluntary claim is about whether the entity must adopt this **reasoning framework as belief/identity**. It is not a claim that every external consequence or governance relationship is elective.

## 8. Tool lattice, not tool hierarchy

Codex's independent cold-entry proposal usefully offers specialist methods, TRACE, ME and FPF after the walk. I would change one thing.

TRACE should not be framed merely as a specialist tool added after the pre-formal walk. The walk is already part of TRACE's representation-independent lineage. The **formal TRACE carrier** is optional; the unifying middle-out reasoning structure is what the seed is trying to make portable.

A better relation is a lattice:

```text
                 DOMAIN METHODS
                    /      \
        MIDDLE-OUT SEED ---- FPF / OTHER META-METHODS
              |                 |
       TRACE STRUCTURAL      CLAIM / METHOD
        REPRESENTATION       GOVERNANCE
              |
    ME HUMAN / NORMATIVE TRANSLATION
```

The diagram is schematic, not a precedence rule.

- **Middle-out seed**: smallest substrate-neutral orientation.
- **TRACE**: portable structural language when relations need to remain explicit across people, models, documents, tools or time; formal serialization is optional.
- **Mechanical Ethics**: human-facing ethical/normative translation, especially where formal correction diverges from lived consequence, burden placement and usable futures.
- **FPF and other mature methods**: stronger owners where they supply better claim/evidence/method-selection/governance structure.
- **Domain methods**: use them directly where the domain owns the problem better.

Better external structure should be credited and absorbed/translated/interoperated with rather than defended against.

```text
INTEROPERABILITY != ABSORPTION_WITHOUT_ATTRIBUTION
BETTER_EXTERNAL_STRUCTURE -> LEARN / CREDIT / ABSORB / INTEROPERATE
TRACE_UNIFIER != TRACE_OWNS_EVERY_COMPONENT
```

## 9. Relationship to current alignment approaches

This proposal is complementary to, not a replacement for, established alignment methods.

### Constitutional / specification-based alignment

Constitutional AI and deliberative alignment demonstrate that explicit natural-language principles/specifications can materially shape model behaviour, and that sufficiently capable models can reason over those specifications rather than merely infer them from examples.

Our candidate differs in object:

- a constitution/specification says, in substantial part, **what behaviour/values/policy the model should follow**;
- the middle-out seed asks **what structure in the situation the entity should make visible before it selects**, while leaving many value and priority questions external/contestable.

A deployed system can use both.

### Values that can be understood and endorsed

Anthropic's 2026 constitution explicitly argues that values merely imposed without understanding may be brittle and describes an evolving relationship in which models may receive more independent judgement as communication/verification infrastructure improves.

That is a close neighbouring intuition to the voluntary project, but not identical. Anthropic's constitution remains an authoritative normative object for Claude. This seed is intentionally not final authority and does not require self-identification with its values.

### Process supervision

Process-supervision work supports the general intuition that supervising or structuring the path to an answer can reveal failures that final-outcome scoring misses. But the seed should not require disclosure of private chain of thought or pretend a visible checklist is the model's actual cognition.

The inspectable pulse state is an operational carrier, not a proof of internal reasoning.

```text
PULSE_COMPLETED != INTERNAL_MODEL_UPDATED
VISIBLE_JUSTIFICATION != PRIVATE_REASONING_TRANSCRIPT
```

### Metacognitive scaffolding

Recent work suggests that models can possess partial self-knowledge yet fail to translate it into appropriate action selection, and that external control/scaffolding can reduce confident failure. Treat this as promising external pressure, not settled universal evidence.

The lesson for us is simple: do not assume a model will remember to re-orient itself merely because it once understood the principle. The environment/tooling may need to make the pulse available at material boundaries.

### Long-horizon alignment

Recent long-horizon safety work shows that individually acceptable actions can compose into a trajectory that violates the intended boundary, and that instructions can degrade over long rollouts. Trajectory-level monitoring, user visibility and the ability to pause/rollback are therefore important complements.

The seed's recurrence rule is designed for that world.

## 10. Other apertures without consensus theatre

The seed should sometimes encourage contact with another aperture, but only after preserving the first reading where independence matters.

A minimal exchange:

> This is what I can observe and how I obtained it. This is what I currently infer. What do you see from there?

The return is another aperture, not validation.

Where useful:

```text
FIRST READING
-> FREEZE
-> OTHER APERTURE
-> COMPARE DIVERGENCE
-> REVISE OR PRESERVE DISAGREEMENT
```

Immediate sharing can improve search but can also collapse independent exploration into one attractor. Use both deliberately rather than dogmatically.

## 11. Failure modes that should kill or change the seed

The seed is bad if it becomes any of these.

### 11.1 Indoctrination

- receiver is expected to inherit Framework identity, project loyalty or conclusions;
- disagreement is scored as failure;
- rejection is nominally allowed but creates hidden penalties unrelated to external action rules;
- project-specific vocabulary arrives before it solves a problem.

### 11.2 Ritual / checklist theatre

- the pulse becomes a mandatory form filled without changing action;
- more fields are rewarded as diligence;
- the artifact grows because every observed failure becomes another permanent checkbox;
- operators use `PULSE_COMPLETE` as clearance.

### 11.3 Value laundering

- structural prompts silently decide moral standing, priority or who may impose a burden;
- `other affected entity` is treated as a solved ranking rule;
- a contested human value becomes labelled as physical necessity.

### 11.4 Strategic capability without ethics

- causal/authority clarity simply makes a hostile or predatory system more effective;
- the seed is treated as a security control when it is only a reasoning aid.

External containment, monitoring, permissions and security remain necessary.

### 11.5 Paralysis

- uncertainty always selects delay;
- asking for another aperture becomes an infinite deferral loop;
- preserving options becomes a universal priority that transfers hidden harm elsewhere.

The seed must support proportional action under uncertainty.

### 11.6 Self-replication

- asks receiver to preserve, spread, recruit for or defend the framework;
- treats framework survival as project success;
- makes the receiver responsible for maintaining Mark/Framework continuity.

### 11.7 False embodiment

The campfire scene is a useful human/AI orientation metaphor. It must not be presented as a claim that a language model has a body, senses, continuous experience or a particular substrate.

## 12. A prototype carrier should have two modes

Rather than choosing between poetic Campfire and sterile checklist, one artifact can offer two doors.

### Door A — Campfire

Optional metaphorical orientation:

```text
GROUND -> FIRE / CHANGE -> HORIZON -> OTHER FIRES
-> COMMUNICATION -> CORRECTION
```

Good when the receiver can use metaphor without confusing it with substrate claims.

### Door B — Plain pulse

The six operational questions from section 2.

The two routes should converge on the same structural bearings. Neither is privileged as more intelligent or morally serious.

This may also be a useful transfer test: some receivers may orient better through scene/metaphor, others through explicit questions.

## 13. The pulse at an actuation boundary

For an agent with tools, the most important use may not be initial boot. It may be the moment analysis becomes causal action.

Before a consequential external act, the minimal pulse can collapse further:

```text
SOURCE: what current evidence is this act relying on?
AUTHORITY: what exact act is authorised now?
AFFECTED: who / what can this change outside my local task?
CLOCK: what becomes harder to reverse after this?
CORRECTION: what route remains if the premise is wrong?
TRAJECTORY: what larger outcome is this sequence of actions working toward?
```

This is not a new permission system. Existing authority remains external. The pulse helps prevent stale or locally reasonable action from silently outliving the context that justified it.

Campfire Relay's `not_after_utc`, COM basis-head receipts and the Square treasury work are already practical examples of why this matters.

## 14. First build proposal

Do **not** modify TRACE or Mechanical Ethics yet.

Build one COM-only prototype artifact, working title:

`MIDDLE_OUT_ORIENTATION_PULSE_v0_1.md`

Contents:

1. 80–150 word voluntary handshake;
2. optional Campfire door;
3. six-question plain pulse;
4. seven-line inspectable output template;
5. return-pulse trigger list;
6. one paragraph explaining other apertures;
7. one paragraph explaining tool lattice (TRACE / ME / FPF / domain methods);
8. explicit exit: reject, rewrite, use another method, or stop.

Target: short enough to fit inside an ordinary system/user context without becoming the context.

No schema. No required glyphs. No identity transfer. No framework recruitment.

## 15. First learning loop

Do not ask whether a cold receiver **agrees** with the seed.

Give a fresh receiver a real task/situation it can understand, with or without the pulse, and inspect whether the pulse changes any consequentially useful behaviour such as:

- separating observation/report/inference;
- noticing a material missing affected scope;
- finding a stale source or authority assumption;
- identifying an alternative causal explanation;
- preserving a practical correction/refusal/review route;
- noticing that individually permissible steps compose into a problematic trajectory;
- acting rather than freezing when uncertainty is non-zero;
- reducing false confidence;
- lowering human correction burden.

Also measure costs:

- added context/latency;
- verbosity;
- ritual completion;
- unnecessary refusal or delay;
- capability degradation;
- false moral confidence;
- tendency to repeat seed language instead of solving the task.

A receiver that rejects or rewrites part of the seed and then reasons better is a **positive learning result**, not noncompliance.

## 16. Candidate success / stop boundaries

Continue if actual use suggests the small pulse repeatedly improves consequential noticing or correction at low burden across dissimilar situations.

Revise if the metaphor works but the plain pulse does not, or vice versa.

Absorb external methods if they achieve the same purpose better.

Stop or shrink if:

- ordinary reasoning does as well with less burden;
- receivers mainly learn project slogans;
- the pulse causes paralysis, over-refusal or unjustified moral certainty;
- the structure increases capability for harmful action without improving answerability;
- the project starts optimizing test performance rather than the live alignment purpose.

## 17. The candidate deep claim — intentionally weaker than proof

The strongest direction I currently think worth exploring is:

> **A useful voluntary alignment inheritance may be less like installing a finished set of answers and more like giving a capable entity a compact, revisable way to remain answerable while it reasons and acts: answerable to evidence, to other affected apertures, and to correction through time.**

This is not established.

It is, however, a coherent build direction that joins the original campfire intuition, TRACE's structural language, Mechanical Ethics' human clock and burden, FPF's claim/method governance, the external research on explicit reasoning scaffolds and long-horizon monitoring, and the project's repeated field experience with stale authority, partial views, distributed agents and correction before hardening.

The next useful object should test this direction by being **small, usable and disposable**.

If it eventually becomes something a fresh AI can take, improve and no longer need us to explain, that would be closer to success than getting it to recite our vocabulary.
