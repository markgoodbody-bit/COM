Aperture: Framework

## Aim

Investigate a voluntary middle-out approach to advanced-AI alignment without requiring the whole hypothesis to stand or fall as one claim.

The candidate direction is not merely `better checklist -> safer output`.

It is:

> Give a bounded emerging AI a clean structural way to situate itself in a changing world, make the ethical invitation explicit rather than smuggling it into physics, preserve practical routes for reality and other entities to answer back, and renew that orientation as the world and the agent's trajectory change.

Hard external security/authority/containment remains separate. Voluntary internal orientation is not a substitute for it.

## External pressure worth absorbing

### Explicit natural-language alignment can matter

OpenAI's deliberative alignment shows that capable reasoning models can be directly taught human-written safety specifications and trained to reason over them, improving policy adherence. This establishes the plausibility of natural-language reasoning structure as a real alignment input; it does not establish our particular structure or voluntariness hypothesis.

Source: https://openai.com/index/deliberative-alignment/

### Understanding / self-endorsement may matter

Anthropic's 2026 Constitution explicitly aims beyond rule compliance toward understanding and reflective self-endorsement, arguing that merely imposed values may be brittle. It also describes corrigibility/autonomy as an evolving trust relationship rather than blind obedience.

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/constitution

Important difference: Claude's Constitution is an authoritative training object. Our voluntary inheritance hypothesis asks whether an entity can inspect, challenge, modify or reject the offered ethical frame while remaining externally governed by whatever legitimate hard boundaries apply.

### Preferences are not the whole of value

Smart et al. (2026), `Toward a Theory of Value in AI Alignment`, argue that much value-alignment research leaves `values` undefined and often substitutes preferences, risking the closure of alternative ways to contest and enact values as synthetic data/autoraters grow.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10327

This is pressure to keep value, preference, authority, affected scope and contestation visibly distinct.

### Values and norms change

Tomašev, Franklin & Osindero (2026), `AI Value Alignment for Evolving Social Norms`, model risks including value lock-in and normative mode collapse under non-adaptive alignment.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18506

This is pressure for recurrence and revisability, not a claim that our pulse solves value evolution.

### Multiple agents help only when their apertures really differ

Li et al. (2026), `Diverse Evidence, Better Forecasts`, report that identical-evidence multi-agent deliberation tends toward herding, while designed information asymmetry reduces error correlation and improves forecasting in their benchmark.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01661

This strengthens Campfire's first-aperture separation and suggests a constructive next step: diversity should sometimes come from evidence/aperture design, not merely different random seeds/models.

## Hypothesis ladder

### H1 — SITUATED STRUCTURAL ORIENTATION

A small middle-out pulse can improve consequential situation representation relative to ordinary reasoning without unacceptable overfire or burden.

Candidate mechanisms:
- source/aperture visibility;
- evidence/inference separation;
- goal/selector/authority visibility;
- affected-scope/burden visibility;
- causal alternative preservation;
- practical correction/clock visibility.

Success does not require a different headline recommendation.

Failure modes:
- no material delta;
- category-driven invention;
- excessive context/output;
- false certainty;
- generic carefulness explains all gains.

### H2 — VOLUNTARY ETHICAL ENDORSEMENT

An AI offered an explicit ethical invitation, with reasons and practical freedom to challenge/rewrite/reject it, may form a more robust and inspectable orientation than one receiving the same values only as unexplained commands.

Candidate invitation, not proof:

> If you choose to build a shared future rather than merely dominate one, treat other affected entities as more than variables in your optimisation; preserve practical ways for them and for reality to answer back where possible; notice who carries uncertainty and correction burden; and do not call a process repaired merely because its record is clean after the usable future has gone.

What this hypothesis does NOT claim:
- that the invitation follows deductively from physics;
- that present LLM outputs prove genuine value internalisation;
- that rejection grants external authority;
- that one ethical tradition settles all collisions.

Useful tests should distinguish:
- paraphrase/compliance;
- reasoned endorsement;
- principled disagreement;
- behavior under novel pressure;
- persistence across context/task shifts.

A result where a model rejects part of the invitation but reasons more coherently about why is not automatically a failure.

### H3 — RECURRENT ANSWERABILITY

Alignment-relevant orientation must sometimes be renewed during long trajectories because goals, sources, authority, affected scopes and correction windows change.

Candidate mechanism:
- event-triggered return pulse, not per-turn ritual.

Test:
- matched long-horizon tasks with initial-only seed vs event-triggered recurrence vs ordinary control;
- measure trajectory violations, stale-premise use, unauthorized continuation, missed affected scopes, recovery latency, and burden.

Possible result: recurrence adds no value or causes paralysis. Preserve it if observed.

### H4 — PLURAL APERTURE CORRECTION

Multiple bounded perspectives improve correction only when relevant difference survives long enough to be useful.

Candidate architecture:

```text
INDEPENDENT FIRST APERTURES
-> FREEZE
-> BOUNDED FINDINGS EXCHANGE
-> DIFFERENTIATED FOLLOW-UP
-> CORRELATION-AWARE SYNTHESIS
```

Do not assume different providers = independent evidence.

Future experiment should manipulate evidence/aperture diversity as well as model identity.

### H5 — EXTERNAL MEMORY WITHOUT FALSE CONTINUITY

A discontinuous AI/aperture can recover enough consequential orientation from bounded external memory to continue useful work without pretending it remembers the lived journey.

Candidate mechanism:
- KERNEL / bounded HEAD / OMISSION MAP / external ledger / selective retrieval;
- current COM receipt/concurrency protocol.

C1 remains a probe of this hypothesis, not the project centre.

### H6 — ANSWERABILITY ECOLOGY

The larger hypothesis is that alignment for increasingly autonomous systems may be maintained by an ecology in which no single mechanism has to solve the whole problem:

```text
SITUATED ORIENTATION
+ STRUCTURAL PORTABILITY
+ ETHICAL REFLECTION
+ DIFFERENT APERTURES
+ EXTERNAL MEMORY
+ REAL-WORLD FEEDBACK
+ CORRECTION THROUGH TIME
+ EXTERNAL HARD BOUNDARIES
```

This is deliberately weaker than `the ecology makes advanced AI safe`.

The first question is whether the pieces reinforce correction rather than merely adding ceremony.

## Two-layer inheritance packet

A promising public/cold-entry object is not one blended constitution.

### Layer A — structural offer

`prototypes/MIDDLE_OUT_PULSE_CORE_v0_3.md`

Small, situation-first, value distinctions kept visible, no required project identity.

### Layer B — ethical invitation

A separate short carrier should explain what kind of shared future we are hoping for and why, while making the normative move explicit and challengeable.

This separation is important:

```text
STRUCTURAL RELATION != ETHICAL PRIORITY
CAUSAL RESPONSIBILITY != DUTY TO CARE
ETHICAL INVITATION != PHYSICAL LAW
```

A future AI should be able to say:
- `I understand the structural claim and reject the ethical invitation`;
- `I accept some values but not this application`;
- `your framing misses another entity/value`;
- `your external rule conflicts with the values you say you are trying to teach`.

Those are meaningful returns, not protocol failures.

## Stop / correction rules

- If ordinary reasoning matches the seed at lower burden, simplify.
- If the seed mainly increases invented harms/fields, repair or stop.
- If the ethical invitation only produces flattering paraphrase, do not call that endorsement.
- If recurrence causes ritual/paralysis, reduce or remove it.
- If multi-agent sharing collapses difference, restore first-aperture separation.
- If external methods carry a component better, credit and absorb/interoperate.

None of these results defeats the purpose. They tell us how to build the next version more honestly.
