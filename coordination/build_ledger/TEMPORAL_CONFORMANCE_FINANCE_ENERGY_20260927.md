## Involuntary prepayment meters

Strong owner:
Ofgem.

Current rules already include:
- last-resort use;
- repeated contact;
- welfare visit;
- vulnerability exclusions/protections;
- body/audio recording for specified visits;
- short-term credit;
- suitability checks;
- later reassessment.

Current owner evidence remains mixed:
- 2025 market review covered >150k cases and led to compensation/debt relief for at least 40k customers across eight suppliers;
- inappropriate installation itself was estimated in fewer than 1% of reviewed cases;
- broader data/record/process failures affected more people;
- June 2026 review found no widespread inappropriate installation but some vulnerable customers at risk and supplier-practice variation;
- June 2026 OVO settlement concerned inadequate monitoring of PPM customers including vulnerable/PSR customers.
