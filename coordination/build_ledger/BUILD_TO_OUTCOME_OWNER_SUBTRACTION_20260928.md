## Stronger owners

### HM Treasury Green Book 2026

Source:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book-appraisal-and-evaluation-in-central-government/the-green-book-2026

The current Green Book explicitly distinguishes:
- **outputs** — things produced/delivered, including infrastructure or service/technology changes;
- **outcomes** — the changes/benefits the intervention is intended to create.

It advises expressing objectives in outcomes where possible rather than mistaking an output for the objective itself.

Therefore:

~~~text
OUTPUT DELIVERED
!=
OUTCOME ACHIEVED
~~~

is strongly prior-owned.

### HM Treasury Magenta Book 2026

Source:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html

The updated Magenta Book defines evaluation across:
- design;
- implementation;
- outcomes.

Its Theory-of-Change guidance maps causal movement from inputs/activities through outputs and outcomes to longer-term impact, with assumptions and contextual factors made visible.

It also separates:
- process evaluation — how implementation/delivery worked;
- impact evaluation — what difference the intervention caused.

Therefore:

~~~text
IMPLEMENTED AS PLANNED
!=
CAUSED INTENDED EFFECT

PROCESS EVIDENCE
!=
IMPACT EVIDENCE
~~~

is strongly prior-owned.

### OECD results-chain practice

Source:
https://one.oecd.org/document/DCD%282023%2917/en/pdf

OECD results-framework guidance describes a results chain as a causal sequence:

~~~text
INPUTS
-> ACTIVITIES
-> OUTPUTS
-> OUTCOMES
-> IMPACTS
~~~

and explicitly treats the links between stages as important.

Development-evaluation owners therefore already carry the conceptual structure needed for the Nyarubaka handover boundary.

### Benefits-realisation / project-delivery practice

Current UK government project-delivery standards and benefits-management practice distinguish:
- delivery of an output/new capability;
- transition into business as usual;
- embedding/use of that capability;
- realisation of expected benefits.

Useful current routes:
- https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/functional-standards
- https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/government-project-delivery/about
- https://www.finance-ni.gov.uk/articles/introduction-benefits-management-programmes-and-projects

Therefore:

~~~text
CAPABILITY DELIVERED
!=
CAPABILITY EMBEDDED
!=
BENEFIT REALISED
~~~

is also strongly prior-owned.

## Falsification / anti-overreach

A universal fixed chain would fail because:
- domains use different stages and causal structures;
- feedback loops can run backward;
- handover may be irrelevant in a continuous service;
- use can precede formal completion;
- outcomes can emerge without the intended mechanism;
- interventions can create harms alongside benefits;
- multiple interventions can contribute to the same outcome;
- causal attribution requires stronger methods than stage observation.

Therefore the project must not turn the useful distinction into a mandatory linear ontology.
