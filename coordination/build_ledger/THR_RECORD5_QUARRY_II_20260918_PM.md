## Candidate 2 — Scott de Martinville 1860 phonautogram reconstruction

Recoverability shape:
- physical trace was never designed for playback;
- 2008 reconstruction used a wrong absolute speed and produced a misleading higher voice;
- 2009 work reinterpreted the tuning-fork reference and corrected playback speed;
- later work refined lyrics and reconstruction.

Strongest owner found:
- First Sounds.

First Sounds preserves the earlier releases, explains the wrong-speed interpretation and correction, documents the evolving reconstruction methods, identifies the physical/source lineage and states that it has digitally preserved every known airborne sound recording from before 1861.

Disposition:
OWNER FOUND / NO THR DELTA.

This is almost a model THR case, which is exactly why THR should route to the owner rather than duplicate it.
