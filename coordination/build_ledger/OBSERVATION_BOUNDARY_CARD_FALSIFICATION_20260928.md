# Observation-boundary card — hostile falsification pass

Date: 28 September 2026

Status: **BOUNDED HOSTILE TEST / CARD NARROWED / SURVIVES AS SALIENCE TOOL / NOT VALIDATION**

Target:
coordination/OBSERVATION_BOUNDARY_APERTURE_CARD_v0_20260928.md

Question:

**Can a compact observation-boundary check help without turning absence into imagined harm, inventing denominators, or duplicating stronger domain methods?**

## Candidate claim under attack

The weak form worth testing is:

~~~text
IF
a metric begins after a gate
AND
material states can occur before that gate

THEN
the downstream metric may be insufficient for a claim about
the whole affected population/pathway.
~~~

This is deliberately weaker than:
- "the metric is wrong";
- "everyone outside it was harmed";
- "a missing route caused the observed outcome";
- "the project can estimate the missing denominator".

## Falsifier 1 — legitimate clinical triage

A referral may be returned because the booked service is clinically inappropriate.

Failure mode:
the card labels every non-entry as unmet need or harm.

Required repair:
explicitly separate legitimate ineligibility/triage from material foreclosure.

Result:
**SURVIVES AFTER GUARD.**

~~~text
NOT ENTERED
!=
WRONGLY EXCLUDED
~~~

## Falsifier 2 — informed non-use

An eligible person may know a programme exists and rationally choose not to use it.

Failure mode:
non-take-up is automatically classified as access failure.

Required repair:
separate informed choice from burden-induced or constrained non-take-up.

Result:
**SURVIVES AFTER GUARD.**

~~~text
NON-USE
!=
INACCESSIBILITY
~~~

## Falsifier 3 — low ridership with no latent-demand evidence

A bus route may have low observed use because demand is genuinely low.

Failure mode:
low throughput is converted into an invented suppressed-demand population.

Required repair:
owner-evidenced pre-count state is mandatory; the card cannot infer latent demand itself.

Result:
**SURVIVES / OWNER METHOD REQUIRED.**

~~~text
LOW RIDERSHIP
!=
SUPPRESSED DEMAND ESTABLISHED
~~~

## Falsifier 4 — junior workforce share falls while junior employment does not

The current 41-country labour owner case reports a decline in junior workforce share driven mainly by senior employment growth.

Failure mode:
a relative-share change is narrated as vanished junior jobs.

Required repair:
the observation boundary cannot substitute for causal/decomposition analysis.

Result:
**SURVIVES / LABOUR OWNER RETAINS MEASUREMENT.**

~~~text
JUNIOR SHARE DOWN
!=
JUNIOR EMPLOYMENT DOWN
~~~

## Falsifier 5 — returned route later recovers

A referral can be returned and subsequently redirected or re-referred successfully.

Failure mode:
the same episode is counted as permanent route loss and later successful access, exaggerating harm.

Required repair:
distinguish state transition from terminal outcome; check reroute/recovery and clock.

Result:
**SURVIVES AFTER RECOVERY GUARD.**

~~~text
RETURNED
!=
ABANDONED

PRE-GATE FAILURE
!=
TERMINAL FAILURE
~~~

## Falsifier 6 — metric is honest about its scope

A waiting-list statistic may accurately claim only to count patients currently inside the defined RTT pathway.

Failure mode:
the card attacks a metric for not answering a question it never claimed to answer.

Required repair:
only invoke the check when someone uses a bounded metric to support a broader material inference.

Result:
**SURVIVES / SCOPE MISMATCH REQUIRED.**

~~~text
BOUNDED METRIC
!=
DEFECTIVE METRIC

BROADER INFERENCE FROM BOUNDED METRIC
= 
THE ACTUAL TEST TARGET
~~~

## Falsifier 7 — more routes can increase harm

An unsafe, exploitative or clinically inappropriate route may properly be closed.

Failure mode:
route preservation is treated as intrinsically good.

Required repair:
the card must not supply the normative target. Stronger domain owners determine whether the route should exist.

Result:
**SURVIVES / NO ROUTE-MAXIMISATION CLAIM.**

~~~text
MORE OPTIONS
!=
BETTER FUTURE
~~~

## Falsifier 8 — visibility itself can create harm

Tracking people before formal entry may require intrusive collection, linkage or persistent profiling.

Failure mode:
a desire to make hidden states legible becomes an excuse for universal surveillance.

Required repair:
the card asks a reasoning question; it does not authorize new collection. Existing privacy, minimisation, custody and purpose limits remain.

Result:
**SURVIVES / OBSERVABILITY DOES NOT GRANT INSPECTION AUTHORITY.**

~~~text
USEFUL TO KNOW
!=
AUTHORITY TO COLLECT
~~~

## Transfer check against current cases

### Labour hiring margin

Useful:
separations alone do not describe entry hiring.

Guard prevents:
claiming a specific never-hired person lost a specific counterfactual job.

### NHS referral pre-queue state

Useful:
waiting-list entry is downstream of referral state.

Guard prevents:
equating all returned/rejected referrals with inappropriate capacity denial or permanent unmet need.

### Citizens Advice channel lock

Useful:
nominal route availability does not prove practical movement between channels.

Guard prevents:
claiming every offline user needs or wants digital switching.

## Net result

The card survives only in a narrow role:

**scope-check a material inference whose measurement aperture begins downstream of consequential states.**

It does not survive as:
- a new ontology;
- a universal missing-population estimator;
- a harm detector;
- a causal method;
- a route-maximisation principle.

No new dimension was earned by this hostile pass.
