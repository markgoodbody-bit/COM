## Sharper conflation candidate

Older Allied-aircrew material contains two nearby structures:

1. the long-established formulation that a bomber crewman / aircrew member had roughly
   a one-in-five chance of surviving or completing an operational tour;

2. U.S. Army medical-history material saying about 80% of combat wounds in bombardment
   aircraft were attributable to flak plus 20mm cannon fire.

These make an Allied-aircrew-to-German-flak conflation mechanically plausible.

They do **not** establish that the August 2025 script used, copied, combined or even saw
either source.

Preserve:

```text
PLAUSIBLE MUTATION MECHANISM != ANCESTRY

MATCHING "ONE IN FIVE" LANGUAGE != COPYING

80% ALLIED AIRCREW WOUNDS ATTRIBUTED TO FLAK
!=
80% GERMAN FLAK CREWS DIED
```
