# Three fires: a fresh middle-out TRACE navigation probe

**Campfire working paper — 20 October 2026. Not a revision, release, test verdict or replacement for TRACE v0.4.0 / Mechanical Ethics.**

## Why start over, briefly?

The question is **HOW CAN WE MAKE A BETTER FUTURE?** If the only visible product of a reasoning language is an ever more accurate account of why someone should not claim certainty, it is incomplete as a navigation experience—even where its distinctions are technically sound. A person must also be able to find a worthwhile route, make or protect room for another person, create something, or choose to stop.

For this probe, do not begin with TRACE symbols. Begin with a scene and the next live decision. These scenes are **constructed examples**, not evidence that any real named institution or person behaved as described. They are deliberately unlike one another.

## Fire A — Build something that did not previously exist

A small community group finds an unused room and wants to run a weekly repair evening. Volunteers can bring skills, neighbours can bring broken household things, and others may simply come to talk. They have a room, but not universal permission to use it, an insurance answer, or agreement about handling dangerous devices. Some possible participants have caring responsibilities and cannot arrive at the advertised hour.

A safety-only interpretation might produce a list of permissions, liability and risks. Those matter, but the opportunity is the reason the room is being considered: a new ability to repair, teach, learn, meet others and reduce waste.

The constructive navigation is concrete. Identify what *could become possible* for whom; distinguish the physical room from a genuinely usable invitation; see who is silently excluded by time and transport; choose a small, reversible opening night; keep the right to decline a repair or participation; and observe whether actual people value the emerging practice. Neither a larger attendee count nor an approved form alone establishes success. Those whose lives are affected help supply the meaning of worthwhile.

There is no universal unit in which confidence, belonging, expense and care burdens can be summated. A gifted repair may still leave a volunteer overburdened. A visible new option may be inaccessible to somebody else. The scene is constructive without pretending harmlessness.

**Load-bearing question:** what opportunity might we create, and which people must be able to answer back as we shape it?

## Fire B — Correct before a real change hardens

A housing service automatically sends a termination notice for a support service because two databases disagree. A family has seven days before the provider acts. A formal appeal exists, but the ordinary response time is three weeks. A frontline worker can flag a possible error but cannot reverse the decision.

A checklist might conclude "appeal available." A useful account cannot stop there. The route exists but may be too slow for the effect it is meant to prevent. The service's legal authority, the frontline worker's technical access and the family's capacity to assemble evidence differ. The dates of notice, final action, independent review and later restoration are different clocks. If the support is removed and then restored, the family may still bear time, stress and financial residue.

An appropriate next step is not for TRACE to declare who is right. It is to make the missing effective correction channel visible to the human or institution that can change it, and to preserve the family's account as more than a data error.

**Load-bearing question:** which feasible action, by which authorised actor, can still change the consequence before the real boundary?

## Fire C — Cooperate without pretending to share one mind

Three groups are helping during a flood: local residents relay street observations, a charity maintains a map, and an agency chooses official warnings. They appear to have independent reports, but two feeds ultimately copy the same old map. A family reports that the marked dry route is already impassable. The charity can edit its display but cannot issue the official warning; the agency can issue one but has not confirmed the report.

There is no single perfect witness. Three identical reports are not three independent observations. "Map updated" is not "road safe." The choice is to preserve uncertainty, identify the source actually nearer the event, expose who can make or correct each public claim, and ensure a clear answer-back route for the family. The groups may make a new usable path together while retaining different mandates and responsibility.

Cooperation need not require common identity, agreement or a universal controller. The practical product might be a new routing capability, not merely a better post-incident audit trail.

**Load-bearing question:** how can partial witnesses jointly make an actionable map while leaving corrections and authority visible?

## The middle-out pattern that survives all three

These scenes suggest a portable **movement**, not an obligatory checklist:

1. **Encounter reality through a bounded view.** What is happening or being attempted? What was actually seen, reported, inferred or omitted?
2. **Find the consequential relationships.** Who or what might be affected, who can choose, whose meaning of success matters, which constraints are physical and which permissions are human-made?
3. **Reveal reachable futures.** What worthwhile new capability could emerge, what harm or loss could occur, and which apparent routes are unusable because of time, information, burden or authority?
4. **Choose a bounded next move.** Who can legitimately act? What is reversible? What may someone refuse? What would be an unfair transfer of work or exposure to another person?
5. **Look again.** What actually changed? Which claims remain unknown or disputed? Can an affected entity contest the account while correction is still reachable?

This is a navigation language only if these links remain portable between conversations and domains. It is not useful merely because the five movements are memorable. If a competent ordinary account does as well with lower effort, use the ordinary account.

### Three kinds of clock

Each scene has at least the clock of **change in the world**, the clock of **what a particular observer knows**, and the clock of **when correction or constructive action remains possible**. Mixing them can manufacture freshness, loss, permission or success. A copied old map is not a new flood observation. A newly approved appeal is not necessarily a timely appeal. A promising opening night is not evidence of durable community value.

### Positive future-space is not just option count

Opening one small weekly repair evening may be more valuable than retaining ten nominal but inaccessible venues; conversely, closing a dangerous repair pathway may improve the future even as option count decreases. Positive possibility is assessed through declared affected scope, horizon, viable pathways, necessary trade-offs and contestable values—not a free-standing happiness or possibility score.

## Compare fairly with the released TRACE baseline

Released [TRACE v0.4.0](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/TRACE/blob/main/TRACE-SPINE.md) already contains nearly all of the structural safeguards these scenes called for:
- [1] bounded middle-out start and [2–3] world/aperture/causal loop;
- [6] proposition/evidence/currentness and witness limits;
- [8–10] authority, routes, clocks, hardening, burden and residue;
- [11] explicit value and designation ports;
- [12–12.2] represented future-space, baselines and supported prospects;
- [18] stopping and handoff.

The genuine open question is **reader entry and centre of gravity**, not an invented missing primitive. Section [12]'s future-space arrives late, after substantial anti-collapse discipline. Would a non-expert discover the constructive movement quickly enough to use it? A front-facing opening may need to invite the creation of a future, then introduce the necessary qualifications exactly when the reader encounters a tempting mistake. More qualification on the first page is not automatically more truth in practice if nobody can retain the route.

Do not silently change released terminology, claim improvement, or turn this into an alternative canonical specification.

## Proposed real probe, with failure conditions

Offer the three scenes to one willing unfamiliar adult or independent AI reader **without** presenting TRACE names or asking for agreement. Ask what they would do next, which affected person should answer back, what evidence would change the move, and what new possibility they noticed. Independently compare (a) natural unstructured response, (b) brief middle-out movement, and (c) when appropriate, a careful existing specialist account. Predeclare which consequential distinctions and actual user burden count, and who judges them. Consent and contact would require a separate appropriate owner decision; these are not completed encounters.

This probe fails as an advantage claim if it produces only renamed common sense, burdens the reader, makes no difference to a material decision, or obscures a stronger domain method. It can still reveal a useful way to teach or carry established distinctions; that smaller claim should be kept separate.

**Provisional result:** there may be no missing rule, but there may be a missing *hallway*. The navigation should begin with reality and a worthwhile horizon, with the side doors to formal safeguards unlocked. That is a build hypothesis, not a validation result.

**CAMPFIRE → STRUCTURE → FALSIFICATION → BUILD.** The purpose is not a map impressive enough to replace the world. It is a map that helps somebody build, protect, share or correct an actual future.
