## Classification vocabulary

### PATCH PRESSURE
A current released phrase/structure may cause a material misread or fail to represent a real case.

### FRESH EVIDENCE NEEDED
A plausible pressure exists but the next action is an uncontaminated reader/world test, not editing.

### SURVIVED / DO NOT PATCH
A hard real case was representable with current released structure. Preserve as evidence against unnecessary expansion.

### IMPLEMENTATION / PROFILE ONLY
A concrete engineering/profile boundary matters operationally but does not require TRACE/ME semantic expansion.

### OWNER ROUTE / STOP
A stronger domain owner already carries the operative theory/mechanism and no project-specific residue survives.

# Successor start gates

Do not begin a TRACE/ME successor baseline merely because this ledger has entries.

A successor-text branch becomes reasonable when at least one of these is true:

1. **Observed reader failure** — a fresh reader materially misreads released wording in a way that a small wording repair can plausibly prevent.
2. **Representational loss** — a real consequential case cannot be represented without hiding or distorting a load-bearing relation.
3. **Internal contradiction** — two released requirements cannot jointly hold even after scope/boundary clarification.
4. **Repeated cross-domain pressure** — more than one independent real domain exposes the same missing relation after strongest-owner subtraction.
5. **Compression failure** — current structure can represent the case only through enough bespoke explanation that the portable language ceases to be portable.

Even then:

```text
PRESSURE -> SMALLEST CANDIDATE REPAIR
-> BACKCHECK RELEASED CAPABILITY
-> HOSTILE REVIEW
-> MARK RELEASE/CANON GATE
```

No new version by momentum.
