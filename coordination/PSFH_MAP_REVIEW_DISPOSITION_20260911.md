## Provisional judgement

KEEP the current human map and authored edges. Retain the observed asymmetry as a known property, not a newly invented claim about original authorial intent. Do not add links solely to make incoming degree fall within 3 +/- 1. An equal quota would need its own justification and could erase or manufacture substantive relationships. Uniform outgoing degree does not establish authorial neutrality, and unequal incoming degree does not establish a normative ranking or a reader's interpretation. Repeated exposure is a plausible concern, not a measured effect here.

DEFER the proposed closed relation vocabulary. Thirty unique strings do prevent grouping multiple edges by identical relation value. They do not remove target-based grouping, adjacency or traversal, and uniqueness alone does not prove a malformed graph. The reviewed question-index scope promises copied source questions/edges, not a closed cross-edge type ontology.

Concrete counterpressure: the five aperture arrivals include `whose_account_is_missing` from Power and `recheck_the_view` from Wisdom. The first points to absent testimony in a power relation; the second invites reconsidering a familiar view. They can share a broad family without being interchangeable. Choosing five or six families is a new semantic design decision, not merely moving bytes from `relation` to `note`. Keeping the original string would preserve the wording but would not validate the new grouping.

CC's example query for all aperture-related edges is already structurally expressible by `target == "aperture"`. That does not satisfy every possible semantic query. A concrete query crossing targets, its expected membership, and the reason target/question/source fields are insufficient would establish a better basis for a bounded type proposal. Do not demand a large benchmark or actual failure log merely to consider the design; a reproducible counterexample is enough.
