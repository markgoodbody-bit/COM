# Observation-boundary aperture check — minimal teaching card v0

Date: 28 September 2026

Status: **FROZEN PROVISIONAL TEACHING CARD / OWNER-DERIVED / NOT CANON / NOT VALIDATED / NO TRACE OR ME PATCH**

Purpose: prevent a bounded metric from being silently mistaken for the whole affected world when consequential states can occur before the metric begins.

This card does **not** estimate a missing population. It does not declare unobserved entities harmed. It routes the reader back to stronger domain owners.

## The card

~~~text
1. COUNT START
Where does the metric begin?
Name the first state/gate that becomes visible.

2. PRE-COUNT STATES
What owner-evidenced consequential states can occur before that gate?
Do not invent a counterfactual population.

3. MEANING
Separate:
- legitimate ineligibility / triage;
- informed non-use / choice;
- temporary delay with recovery;
- reroute / substitution;
- material foreclosure / unmet need;
- UNKNOWN.

4. OWNER
Who already measures the missing state?
Use their method: non-take-up, unmet need, hiring flows,
opportunity structure, latent/suppressed demand, etc.

5. INTERPRETATION
Could omitted pre-count states materially change the decision
being made from the downstream metric?

YES -> qualify the inference and route to the owner measure.
NO / UNKNOWN -> do not manufacture a gap.
~~~

## What this card is for

Use it when a consequential claim is being inferred from a post-gate count such as:
- accepted referrals;
- waiting-list entries;
- hires or separations;
- benefit applications/take-up;
- realised ridership;
- successfully completed digital journeys.

The card asks whether the observation boundary excludes a materially relevant pre-entry state.

It does not assume the answer is yes.

## What this card is not for

Do not use it to:
- infer a harmed individual from aggregate absence;
- infer causation from a missing event;
- treat every unused route as a failed route;
- treat clinically or legally appropriate exclusion as harm;
- double-count an episode that later reroutes successfully;
- estimate latent demand without the domain owner's method;
- create a universal "missing-door" denominator.
