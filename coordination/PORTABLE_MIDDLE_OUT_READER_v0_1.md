# Portable middle-out reader — one consequential turn

**3 November 2026 · Campfire research candidate v0.1 · NOT CANON · NOT A NEW TRACE PRIMITIVE · NOT A PARSER · NOT A RELEASE · NOT AN ETHICAL VERDICT**

**Purpose.** A human or AI enters a situation it does not fully understand. The task is not to manufacture a judgement from a list of boxes. It is to **carry the smallest honest account that still helps the next intelligence see what is changing, for whom, what might become possible, what is hardening, and how a consequential action could be answered and corrected.** The question remains: *How can we make a better future?*

This reader is an optional *surface over existing ME/TRACE distinctions*, not a competing language. Read alongside released [ME v1.0.0](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/mechanical-ethics/blob/v1.0.0/working-editions/v1.0/ME10_DEV4R_CORR2_Source_Preserved_Master.md) (particularly chapters 1–2, 5, 7, 9–13) and released [TRACE v0.5.0](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/TRACE/blob/v0.5.0/TRACE-v0.5.md) (§§1–2A). Neither source claims that this turn is executable. The related provisional [Campfire sketchbook](CAMPFIRE_SKETCHBOOK.md) develops KNOWING ↔ FORMING/DOING ↔ ANSWERABILITY; this small reader makes that relationship usable at first contact.

## A turn is a relationship, not a checklist

Begin **inside an encounter**, not with a score. An encounter may be a change in the world, in somebody's evidence or map, in a participant's expressed aim, or in the set of authorised/usable actions. These are different kinds of change, even when described with the same verb.

The account is followed simultaneously in three directions:

- **Knowing:** through what aperture, from which source, for what scope and time is the change claimed? What is observation, report, inference, dispute or unsupplied? What would actually distinguish the world changing from a record changing?
- **Forming/doing:** whose separate aims, interests and boundaries become relevant? Does a route merely appear in the map, become technically feasible, become permitted, get chosen, get attempted, or actually help the intended bearer? What might be learned, made or sustained, even if no immediate harm is being repaired?
- **Answerability:** who bears gains, losses and delay, including those not present at the decision? What independent answer or refusal could travel back, to whom, before which consequence hardens? Which commitments, unassigned tasks and residues survive a later correction?

These are **coupled readings of one situation**, not mandatory successive gates. A child's learning, a flock's habitat, an inaccessible library, a social agreement and a live software deployment are not the same kind of thing. The relations may transfer; the evidence, standing and domain authority do not.

A useful short return, when a return is needed, can take this shape (ordinary English is preferred):

> From **[bounded source/aperture and relevant time]**, **[what is actually known or contested]** changes **[which possibilities, for which distinct bearers]** relative to **[named baseline]**. **[A specified actor]** could **[propose/choose/attempt a bounded action or refuse]** if **[material capability, permission and clock]** hold. **[What would count as an observed effect and independent answer]** remains **[open, closed or unknown for this route]**; **[material uncertainty, old commitments and residue]** still travel forward.

Do not fill a slot by invention. Omit irrelevant clauses; expose material UNKNOWN rather than padding. A question, a refusal, or an explicit *not enough evidence to act* can be a valid next move. A new apparent path does not compel any participant to take it.

## A small cross-domain move

**Synthetic library encounter, derived from ME's continuing illustration.**

A city publishes an extra evening opening hour. Attendance rises to thirty according to a manager's report. One visitor separately reports that the step-free entrance was locked before the new closing time, and reports missing the last class session they hoped to attend.

**Poor compression:** “The hours change worked: thirty people attended. The complaint is resolved because the timetable was updated.”

**Faithful return:** “The manager reports thirty attendees during extended hours, but this does not establish access for the visitor using the step-free entrance. That visitor reports a locked door and a missed session; a door log, direct check or other independent account could help establish what happened. Staff may be able to restore entrance access for future evenings, subject to actual authority and time. Repairing the door would not restore the missed class. The cause of the lock, present access and any further remedy remain open questions.”

The helpful shift is **not** merely an extra warning. The visitor's changed route, the difference between reported attendance and actual access, the staff's potential corrective power, and the irreversible timing of one lost session become visible *together*. We have moved from a metric to an affected person's possibility and back to an independent corrective witness, without declaring a universal obligation.

**Synthetic positive-formation encounter, derived from ME's plant/workshop examples.**

A young learner, helped by a volunteer, repairs a bicycle in a supervised session. They get it rolling in the workshop; the learner asks to try another repair alone next week. The volunteer has offered only this session and is uncertain whether the workshop can supply tools next week.

**Poor compression:** “The learner has become independently skilled and the volunteer will provide ongoing support.”

**Faithful return:** “A bicycle rolled after a supervised repair, which is a useful produced result. Whether the learner can repair another bicycle independently remains untested; that is a *possible new capability*, not an established one. A further session might help the learner test it, but the volunteer has not committed, and workshop access and tools are unresolved. The present shared work can be worthwhile without claiming a durable training outcome.”

This turn must not be reduced to harm detection. It carries constructive possibility, the learner's evolving choices, the helper's independent agency, present enjoyment and the cost of sustaining a future opportunity.

## Material losses a short return must resist

- **World / record:** a completed form, updated policy, delivered message or revised aim does not establish a changed physical world. A new aim does not retrospectively fulfil an earlier one.
- **Actor / bearer:** a collective label or benefit total cannot substitute for the separate people or other beings bearing effects. The controller, contributor, beneficiary, witness and person owing a duty may differ.
- **Routes:** visible, feasible, authorised, chosen, attempted, observed effective and maintained are separate claims. Formal permission is not practical access; a proposed joint plan is not everyone's consent.
- **Time / residue:** a newly opened correction route may miss the relevant deadline; repair can help future cases without restoring past losses.
- **Return / independence:** a forwarded report is not independent verification, a monitored inbox is not a completed correction, and a successful result for one participant is not all aims met.

Do not carry all five distinctions as ritual boilerplate. Keep the ones whose omission would **change what the next intelligence could legitimately understand or do**. If there is no consequential loss, plain language or a brief source-linked sentence is enough. Complexity must earn its cost.

## Next turn, not final verdict

One return alters the next **View** by adding attributed claims, alternatives, new evidence, changed possibilities and unresolved conditions. An **EncounterStep** in released TRACE v0.5 §2A is a proposed derived reading across those records; it is **not implemented end to end** as a general executable reader. `AimRevision` is likewise a proposed relation retaining earlier commitments; source review did not find a full executable view. No private preference, valid consent, legal authority or moral standing is inferred from a convenient structured representation.

The reader fails if it makes a correct source look like an observed world result; makes one person's consent stand in for another's; converts harm prevention into guaranteed restoration; deletes a productive possibility because it is not a safety hazard; or ends with *UNKNOWN* when there is already a supported, bounded route that could help before hardening. **Neither automatic ACT nor automatic STOP is the language.** Preserve the consequential fork and its rightful chooser.

**Disposition:** This is an initial portable *reader handout*, source-aligned and open to correction. It does not demonstrate receiver efficiency, expert superiority, a runtime implementation or successful field transfer. If it proves useful, its meaning should be preserved in a shorter human/AI interchange rather than multiplying separate primitives or checklists.
