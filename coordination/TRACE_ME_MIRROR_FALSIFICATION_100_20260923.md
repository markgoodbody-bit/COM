## Mirror findings

### File improved faster than world

The project declared `WORLD / REAL USE` primary, then spent a substantial bounded period in beta -> AI review -> beta -> AI review -> preregistration loops. The work produced real repairs, but marginal review value is now below world-contact value.

```text
BETTER FILE != BETTER WORLD
REVIEW CONVERGENCE != WORLD CONSEQUENCE
FALSIFIER WRITTEN != FALSIFIER RUN
STOP RULE PRESENT != STOP RULE APPLIED
```

### Reviewer independence is limited

Fresh model chats reduce conversational contamination but do not establish epistemic independence: major models share public literature, common benchmark culture and similar prompt incentives. Returning reviewers are warm by definition. AI convergence is useful aperture evidence, not an independent population.

### The preregistration risks becoming process-as-distance

The v0 preregistration improves methodological honesty but asks for 24 cases, domain-naive authors, separate mappers, multiple assessors, arbitration, blinding and exposure tracking. Without an executable source of cases and adjudication, the protocol can become the very kind of nominally correct unusable door ME criticises.

### Asymmetric case ecology remains

ME is substantially stronger on institution-fails-person cases than on mirror cases:
- institution/file is right and lived inference is mistaken;
- reform/correction creates the next exclusion;
- delay benefits claimant/weak party or protects another party;
- answerability is weaponised in bad faith;
- protective observation becomes surveillance;
- diffuse algorithmic exclusion has no single visible refusal or clock.

These are test vectors before they are new prose requirements.

## Candidate world route

The UK Financial Ombudsman Service publishes a large anonymised database of final individual decisions, including consumer-credit cases. This is a plausible source of naturalistic cases because the case narratives and independent final decisions already exist outside TRACE/ME. A pilot could hide the final outcome during A/B analysis and use the published decision later as domain context/adjudication evidence, while preserving the Ombudsman's own warning that individual decisions are case-specific and not definitive statements of law.
