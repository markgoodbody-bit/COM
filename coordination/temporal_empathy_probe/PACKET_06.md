# Temporal-empathy reader packet 06

Status: **FROZEN SOURCE PACKET / PUBLIC OMBUDSMAN DECISION SUMMARY / NO PROJECT ANALYSIS**

## Source

Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman:

London Borough of Bromley (22 016 360), decision 17 May 2023.

https://www.lgo.org.uk/decisions/education/special-educational-needs/22-016-360

## Source facts

A parent complained that the council took too long to produce an Education, Health and Care Plan for her daughter.

The Ombudsman found that the council took too long to produce the plan and did not properly consider whether alternative education should be provided.

According to the decision summary, the delay also delayed the parent's right of appeal.

The child missed education during the period.

The council agreed to apologise, make a payment recognising the loss of education and provide staff training/guidance.

## Ceiling

Use only the source facts above.

Do not infer:
- medical or educational needs not stated by the source;
- motives of the council;
- that money literally restores missed education;
- that every delay in an Education, Health and Care Plan has the same effect.
