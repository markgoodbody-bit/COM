## Source

Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman, 9 July 2026:

*Gaps in NHS dentistry and confusion about care left patients in unnecessary pain*

https://www.ombudsman.org.uk/news-and-blog/news/gaps-nhs-dentistry-and-confusion-about-care-left-patients-unnecessary-pain

## Source facts

The Ombudsman reported problems involving gaps and confusion over responsibility between parts of NHS dental care.

One case concerned a woman who had broken teeth after a fall. According to the Ombudsman's public summary, she waited eight years for treatment after falling between local dental and hospital services.

A second case concerned a patient who remained in severe pain for more than a year after faulty NHS root-canal work. The public summary says the dental practice failed to arrange appropriate follow-up treatment.

The Ombudsman presented the cases as examples of wider concerns about how services are commissioned and delivered and about confusion over who is responsible for patients receiving needed treatment.
