# Temporal empathy — owner-routing matrix

Date: 27 September 2026

Status: **RESEARCH TOOL / NOT CANON / NOT A TRACE OR ME PRIMITIVE**

Purpose:

Compress the stronger-owner map into a routing surface that can be used during later case testing.

This matrix does **not** claim ownership of the listed concepts. It is explicitly an outward router.

Primary source note:
`coordination/resources/TEMPORAL_EMPATHY_STRONG_OWNER_MAP_20260927.md`

## Routing rule

```text
TEMPORAL QUESTION
-> IDENTIFY WHICH KIND OF TIME PROBLEM THIS IS
-> ROUTE TO STRONGER OWNER
-> KEEP ONLY THE CROSS-DOMAIN DISTINCTION NEEDED FOR THE NEXT ACT
```

Do not turn the rows below into one synthetic theory merely because they can be placed in one table.

| Temporal question | Stronger owner / literature | What that owner already gives us | Do not collapse into | Compact routing distinction | Example case class |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Are multiple legitimate rhythms or uncertain temporalities being forced into one uniform schedule? | Temporal Design / HCI — Pschetz; Pschetz & Bastian; Bowler/Bach/Pschetz Haze | plural/situated temporalities; design critique of uniform/accelerative time; uncertainty-aware scheduling; temporal empathy as prior term | generic accessibility or "be nicer about deadlines" | `UNIFORM CLOCK REPRESENTATION != ALL LIVED TEMPORALITIES` | digital calendar / appointment system that demands false certainty |
| Does the person actually control any of the time nominally available to them? | Temporal justice / time poverty — Goodin; Williams/Masuda/Tallis; Giurge/Whillans/West | discretionary-time distribution; necessary vs discretionary time; consequences of time poverty | money poverty alone | `CLOCK TIME AVAILABLE != DISCRETIONARY TIME` | person has 48 hours on paper but most of it is already committed to care/work/basic needs |
| Is duration being imposed by an institution, and does waiting change the trajectory before the result arrives? | Institutional time inequality / waiting — Christ; Holt & Vinopal | unequal waiting; speed capital; delay as trajectory-changing; empirically distributed service-wait burdens | mere inconvenience or customer satisfaction | `EVENTUAL ACCESS != TIMELY ACCESS` | benefit, legal, health, housing or administrative route that arrives after meaningful loss |
| Is the person carrying learning, compliance or psychological work just to use the route? | Administrative burden — Moynihan/Herd/Harvey; disability-access literature including Price | learning/compliance/psychological costs; individualized access burden; institution can shift work onto claimant | "user effort" as neutral friction | `ROUTE EXISTS != ROUTE BURDEN IS CARRYABLE` | repeated forms/evidence/chasing/appeals required before access |
| Does the person know what to do but need help remembering when to act? | Prospective memory / intention offloading — Gilbert et al. | delayed-intention memory burden; external cues/reminders as measurable offloading | incompetence or lack of motivation | `KNOWING WHAT TO DO != REMEMBERING WHEN TO DO IT` | medication, appeal, renewal, follow-up or periodic check where a cue restores feasibility |
| Is the system adding work to a person whose existing life/treatment workload may already exceed capacity? | Minimally Disruptive Medicine / Cumulative Complexity Model / treatment burden — May, Montori, Mair, Shippee and neighbours | workload-capacity balance; treatment/self-care as work; life demands; capacity support; vicious cycles when added work worsens outcomes | nonadherence as motivation failure; one scalar capacity score | `SAME TASK LIST != SAME BURDEN` / `MORE HELP != LESS WORK` | multiple appointments, monitoring, forms or self-care added to work/care/family obligations |
| Are multiple tasks competing for different kinds of limited capability? | Human factors / Multiple Resource Theory — Wickens | task interference depends on modality/resource overlap, priority and difficulty; no single capacity pool | scalar "mental capacity" | `SPARE CAPACITY SOMEWHERE != CAPACITY FOR THIS TASK` | simultaneous caregiving, driving, monitoring, communication or decision tasks |
| Is acute pressure, fatigue, chronic stress or physiological load being treated as one thing? | Stress physiology / allostatic-load / workload literatures | timescale-specific stress/load mechanisms; cumulative dysregulation; cognitive associations | one generic `stress -> capacity` equation | `ACUTE PRESSURE != CHRONIC LOAD != FATIGUE` | repeated high-demand process with insufficient recovery |
| Who controls scheduling, deferral and renegotiation? | Schedule-control / sociology of time / time work — Gerstel & Clawson; Kelly/Moen; Flaherty et al. | temporal agency; schedule control; negotiated/contested timing; worker control distinct from nominal flexibility | "flexibility" as automatically beneficial | `FLEXIBLE SYSTEM != AFFECTED PERSON CONTROLS FLEXIBILITY` | employer/institution offers movable appointments but only on its terms |
| Can all deadline-bearing tasks actually be serviced before their local windows close? | Real-time scheduling / deadline scheduling | feasibility depends on deadlines, interruptibility, execution demand and blocking; utilization alone can miss failure | queue length alone | `AVERAGE LOAD FITS != EVERY DEADLINE FITS` | several time-sensitive obligations where one non-interruptible task causes another to harden |
| Does missed work wait, accumulate, or disappear / harden? | Queueing / backlog / service systems | arrival vs service capacity; backlog growth; instability under sustained overload | hard-deadline failure | `QUEUE WAITS != PLATE FALLS` | unresolved cases accumulate versus one missed deadline permanently closing a route |
| What cannot later be restored even if the record or decision is corrected? | Remedies / irreparable injury / corrective and transformative justice; ME's already-owned neighbours | incomplete restoration; residue; ex-ante protection where later repair cannot recreate prior future | "correction succeeded" | `CORRECTED RECORD != RESTORED TRAJECTORY` | eviction, detention, missed education/work route, irreversible health or relationship consequence |
| Is a future action weak because the future self/path is poorly represented rather than because the route is absent? | Future-self continuity / intertemporal choice — Hershfield and neighbours | representation of future self affects present intertemporal decisions | temporal empathy generally | `FUTURE EXISTS != FUTURE IS PSYCHOLOGICALLY REACHABLE` | saving, treatment adherence, long-horizon preparation |
| Is the observer projecting their own present temporal assumptions onto another historical/social position? | Historical empathy / contextual perspective-taking | contextual reconstruction; anti-presentism; empathy distinct from sympathy | current practical timing/capacity | `MODEL ANOTHER TEMPORAL POSITION != PROJECT OUR PRESENT ONTO IT` | historical record or institution whose temporal norms differed |

## Cross-domain prompts that survive owner subtraction

The smallest useful shared distinctions currently appear to be:

```text
HOW MUCH CLOCK TIME?
!=
WHO CONTROLS THE TIME?
!=
WHO IMPOSES THE WAIT?
!=
WHAT CHANGES WHILE WE WAIT?
```

and:

```text
DEADLINE EXISTS
!=
ROUTE IS USABLE IN TIME

NO SCHEDULED TASK
!=
AVAILABLE CAPACITY

CUE SUPPORT
!=
DECISION CONTROL

FLEXIBILITY
!=
TEMPORAL AGENCY

EVENTUAL CORRECTION
!=
RESTORED FUTURE
```

## Use discipline

Use this matrix only to:
- notice the kind of temporal problem;
- identify the likely stronger owner;
- avoid obvious collapse errors;
- formulate the next question.

Do **not** use it to:
- diagnose health or cognitive state;
- assign a capacity score;
- decide moral worth;
- infer that a person cannot act;
- override the affected entity's own account without evidence;
- replace domain-specific methods.

## Falsifier

If an ordinary reader with direct access to the relevant stronger owner performs as well or better without this routing surface, then the cross-domain layer has not earned extra complexity.

```text
ROUTER USEFUL
!=
ROUTER NECESSARY

OWNER MAP
!=
NEW THEORY
```
