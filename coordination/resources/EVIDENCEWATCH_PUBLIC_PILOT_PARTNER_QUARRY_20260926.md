## Candidate 1 — EPPI Centre / NIHR living-evidence work

Current public evidence:
- EPPI-Reviewer was updated in April 2026 and is actively maintained.
- It supports systematic reviews at large scale.
- EPPI-Reviewer can link Zotero libraries.
- Its OpenAlex-based auto-update service can periodically suggest new relevant records for living reviews.
- The NIHR Evidence Synthesis Groups Living Evidence Synthesis Toolkit is ongoing from June 2025 with no stated end date.
- The associated Best Practice Working Group held 2026 sessions on automation, Cochrane living evidence and a survey of current LES practice.
- The SWARs Living Map was actively maintained during 2026 and reads data from EPPI-Reviewer in real time.

Public owner surfaces:
- https://eppi.ioe.ac.uk/cms/er
- https://eppi.ioe.ac.uk/cms/Default.aspx?tabid=3816
- https://eppi.ioe.ac.uk/cms/research/living-evidence-synthesis-toolkit
- https://eppi.ioe.ac.uk/cms/research/nihr-evidence-synthesis-group-laces/esgs-best-practice-working-group-on-living-evidence-synthesis
- https://eppi.ioe.ac.uk/eppi-vis/Review/Index/745

Why this is a strong test:
- real living-review/update infrastructure already exists;
- Zotero is already connected to the review environment;
- auto-update/new-record discovery is already a stronger owner;
- the remaining question can therefore be stripped down to whether **already-relied-on** source state changes need any additional monitoring/routing beyond EPPI's existing workflow.

Subsumption risk:
**HIGH.** If EPPI-Reviewer or its surrounding methods already handles the relevant source-state/correction path with low burden, EvidenceWatch should shrink or stop.

Public organizational route:
EPPI Support / EPPI Centre contact pages exist.

Disposition:
**HIGH-VALUE STRONGER-OWNER TEST / NO CONTACT.**

## Candidate 2 — Australian Living Evidence Collaboration (ALEC) / LEAPP-AI / Cochrane Evidence Synthesis Unit Australia

Current public evidence:
- ALEC is governed through Cochrane Australia at Monash University.
- Its current public site records 2026 funded living-evidence projects, including early-onset bowel cancer guidance, the LATEST project and an urgent influenza guideline completed in four weeks.
- ALEC and Monash are co-designing a Living Evidence Architecture with WHO and regional partners.
- **LEAPP-AI is now a direct current stronger-owner test.** It is a collaboration between Monash FIT, MADA and ALEC using the LEAPP pregnancy/postnatal living guideline as a real-world case study.
- LEAPP-AI explicitly:
  - maps the current living-guideline workflow through document review and contributor interviews;
  - decomposes it into discrete task-level components;
  - systematically searches for AI-enabled tools;
  - maps tools to specific guideline-development tasks;
  - evaluates tools for practical functionality, strengths, limitations and risks;
  - aims to reduce ongoing maintenance burden while preserving expert time for decision-making, equity and implementation.
- Its public page explicitly welcomes collaboration with **technology partners**, as well as guideline developers, clinicians, researchers and policymakers.
- Cochrane's Evidence Synthesis Unit Australia includes ALEC and provides evidence-synthesis services.

Public owner surfaces:
- https://livingevidence.org.au/research-initiatives/leapp-ai/
- https://livingevidence.org.au/research-initiatives/
- https://livingevidence.org.au/about-us/
- https://www.cochrane.org/about-us/who-we-are/our-groups/evidence-synthesis-unit-australia
- https://www.monash.edu/mada/research/project/living-evidence-architecture
- https://www.monash.edu/mada/research/project/living-evidence-architecture/report

Why this is now the strongest direct falsification environment:
- active living-evidence production, not a historical project;
- an actual living-guideline workflow is being decomposed at task level now;
- AI-tool fit, risk and workflow burden are already explicit research objects;
- technology partners are explicitly in scope;
- the team is strong enough to tell us that EvidenceWatch is redundant, mis-scoped, or only useful as an upstream adapter;
- the question can be asked against their existing workflow rather than against a weak invented comparator.

Subsumption risk:
**VERY HIGH / DESIRABLE.** LEAPP-AI may already own or supersede the useful part of the problem. That makes it a stronger falsification target, not a worse one.

Disposition:
**CURRENT DIRECT STRONGER-OWNER / FALSIFICATION / INTEROPERABILITY CANDIDATE / NO CONTACT.**

## Candidate 3 — University of Bern evidence-synthesis ecosystem

Current public evidence:
- University of Bern runs an Evidence Synthesis and Systematic Review Service for researchers, clinicians and organizations.
- The service explicitly supports systematic searches, data management, screening tools, AI-tool selection and data extraction.
- Its site publicly invites collaboration through an institutional request route.
- ISPM's current `regenAIrate` project is a planned living systematic review of generative AI across the systematic-review workflow, with regularly updated living guidance and public data/analysis.
- Bern hosted a June 2026 lecture on pragmatic and responsible adoption of AI in systematic reviews.
- The earlier Bern living-review case is already our public workflow specimen for preprint→publication re-extraction.

Public owner surfaces:
- https://www.ub.unibe.ch/research/fachinformationen/portal_medicine_and_pharmacy/evidence_synthesis/index_eng.html
- https://www.ispm.unibe.ch/research/research_groups_and_themes/biostatistics_and_research_methodology/index_eng.html
- https://www.ispm.unibe.ch/continuing_education/the_bern_lectures_in_health_science/the_past_present_and_futures_of_pragmatic_and_responsible_adoption_of_ai_in_systematic_reviews/index_eng.html

Why this is a strong test:
- the organization already does exactly the kind of evidence-synthesis and AI-method evaluation where EvidenceWatch claims should be challenged;
- current living-review work exists;
- we already have a source-bound historical maintenance episode from this ecosystem.

Subsumption risk:
**HIGH.** Bern may already have stronger methods/tools or conclude the residual task is too small.

Disposition:
**DIRECT EMPIRICAL-FIT CANDIDATE / NO CONTACT.**

## Candidate 4 — MAGIC Evidence Ecosystem / living-guideline programs

Current public evidence:
- MAGICapp is a live platform for structured guideline authoring, updating and publication with audit trails and version control.
- MAGIC's current research program includes living guidelines and a digital trustworthy evidence ecosystem.
- A September 2026 case study describes the ADA / University of Pennsylvania living oral-health guideline program:
  - monthly evidence surveillance;
  - new studies assessed for meaningful impact;
  - expert panels reconvened only when evidence is likely to change certainty, benefit/harm balance or context;
  - incremental recommendation publishing;
  - live widgets update public guidance from a single maintained source.
- MAGIC explicitly seeks interoperability and efficient evidence flow across synthesis and guideline communities.

Public owner surfaces:
- https://www.magicevidence.org/
- https://www.magicevidence.org/magicapp/
- https://www.magicevidence.org/research-projects/
- https://www.magicevidence.org/featured%20guideline/2026/09/03/building-a-living-guideline-program-in-oral-health.html

Why this is a strong test:
- its operational question is very close to EvidenceWatch's desired routing behavior: what new evidence is material enough to reopen human work;
- its audit/versioning and linked evidence structures are likely stronger than our prototype.

Subsumption risk:
**VERY HIGH.** MAGIC may already implement the useful part of the problem; EvidenceWatch could become unnecessary or only an upstream adapter.

Disposition:
**STRONGER-OWNER / INTEROP TEST / NO CONTACT.**

## Current synthesis

The quarry does **not** support a claim that EvidenceWatch needs its own standalone user community.

It suggests the opposite:

```text
BEST PILOT
!=
FIND A WEAKER WORKFLOW THAT MAKES EVIDENCEWATCH LOOK GOOD

BEST PILOT
=
PUT THE RESIDUAL CLAIM IN FRONT OF A STRONG EXISTING LIVING-EVIDENCE OWNER
```

Current strongest candidate types:
1. **ALEC / LEAPP-AI** for direct task-level AI-in-living-guideline falsification and interoperability testing;
2. **EPPI Centre** for direct review-tool integration/subsumption testing;
3. **Bern** for systematic-review + AI-method evaluation;
4. **MAGIC** for downstream living-guideline materiality/routing.

This ordering is a work-routing heuristic, not a claim that any organization would participate or that EvidenceWatch is useful to them.

## What would count as a useful first conversation later

If Mark authorizes outreach at a later gate, the ask should **not** be "will you use our product?"

The bounded question is:

> In your current living-evidence workflow, do already-relied-on sources change outside the formal retraction/new-study paths often enough to create measurable human maintenance burden, and if so, would a shadow-mode comparison on historical/current episodes be worth testing?

Requested evidence before engineering:
- one actual current workflow;
- how source updates/corrections are noticed today;
- whether exact source lineage/currentness is already owned;
- approximate reviewer minutes;
- one or more historical episodes;
- acceptable miss/false-alert boundary.

If the answer is that existing systems already solve it adequately:

**STOP / OWNER FOUND.**
