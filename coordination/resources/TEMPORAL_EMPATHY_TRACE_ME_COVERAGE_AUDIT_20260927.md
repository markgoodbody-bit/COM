## Mechanical Ethics v0.8.0

Current source:
`e2ef746e931161cb70ac46a4eaa122442134e86b`

Already explicit in the released reader:

### Unequal waiting cost

ME states that if waiting is cheap for one side and expensive for the other, uncertainty "leans", especially where the party benefiting from delay controls records, route and clock.

### Strategic unknown

Honest uncertainty creates work for the party with power to resolve it:
- named owner;
- records opened;
- evidence preserved;
- threatened action paused where possible;
- affected person told what is missing / what could change the answer / when it will be reviewed again.

### Slow harm

ME explicitly asks what becomes harder to reverse while waiting and notes that a case can remain officially open while the person's life changes around it.

### Burden placement

ME's positive direction says actors already exercising power should carry avoidable complexity rather than exporting it to the entity with less power.

### Clock authorship

ME explicitly asks:
- what could close;
- when it becomes hard to restore;
- who controls the clock/evidence;
- who carries burden while uncertainty is resolved;
- what loss remains after correction.

### Open problems

Competing clocks / clock authorship remain explicit unfinished edges.

- created;
- relieved;
- transferred between scopes.

TRACE already distinguishes planning, detection, evidence-hardening, irreversibility and deadlines rather than using one generic time scalar.
