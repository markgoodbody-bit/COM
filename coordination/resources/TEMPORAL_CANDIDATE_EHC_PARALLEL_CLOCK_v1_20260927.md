## Why v1 exists

The first paper object centred the ordinary 20-week EHC-plan horizon.

Recent LGSCO cases show that the parallel education-protection question can arise much earlier, when any relevant council team becomes aware that a child may not be receiving suitable education.

Therefore:

~~~text
20-WEEK HORIZON
= ONE BACKSTOP

AWARENESS / STATE CHANGE
= POTENTIALLY EARLIER TRIGGER
~~~

## Trigger family

Create/check a preservation state when domain owners say it is appropriate after:

1. credible awareness that the child is not attending or may not be receiving suitable education;
2. EHC request/assessment material reporting reduced/absent attendance;
3. existing education/provision materially changes or breaks down;
4. a scheduled review point while the situation remains unresolved;
5. the ordinary 20-week EHC horizon is crossed.

These are workflow prompts only.

They do not determine that a legal duty is engaged.

## Owner-derived discipline

The object should support—not replace—owner expectations that:
- relevant information crosses internal team boundaries;
- consideration/decisions are recorded;
- part-time/reduced provision can require review;
- changed circumstances can trigger a new assessment/action;
- once action is chosen it should not remain merely theoretical.

## Burden guard

Prefer internal information propagation where lawful and appropriate.

~~~text

~~~

## 20-week backstop

At the ordinary EHC 20-week horizon, if still unresolved:
- reacquire the EHC process state;
- confirm whether current education/protection information is current;
- do not assume an earlier parallel-route decision remains adequate if circumstances changed.

The 20-week check is therefore a **currentness backstop**, not the first protection trigger.

## Kill / owner conditions

Route to the stronger owner if:
- local workflows already do this reliably;
- legal/professional owners use a different established mechanism;
- data quality cannot support the state safely;
- automated prompts create inappropriate legal conclusions;
- staff/family burden rises without improving action.

## If ever evaluated

Watch:
- time from attendance/provision change becoming known to consideration by the responsible team;
- whether decisions are recorded;
- whether reduced provision is reviewed;
- time from changed state to changed action;
- family chasing burden;
- duplicate evidence requests;
- actual education received where owner evidence supports measurement;
- staff workload / false triggers.
