## Priority and shared literature work

Start with the complexity question because it affects TRACE's architecture. ME's source integration and prose revision can proceed alongside it; neither track waits for an exhaustive literature review.

Use a compact shared map: current claim/passage; primary source and exact section/page actually read; relevant idea; relation to our language (borrowing, overlap, extension, disagreement or uncertainty); concrete change required. Source citations must alter or support the account at the point of use, not merely decorate an end bibliography. Similarity is not identity or established novelty. Cite only what has been checked; mark leads as unread where appropriate.

Initial leads already raised in the project: Sen and Nussbaum on capabilities, Gibson on affordances, Alicia Juarrero on constraints, Snowden's Cynefin/estuarine work. Recover relevant prior source work before repeating it. These are a bounded starting set, not a claim all have been read or a complete account of the literature.

Move repeated generic qualifications out of the main flow. Keep a condition near a claim when removing it would change that claim's meaning or lead the reader to act wrongly. Give readers a clear next act and a usable example.

## First paired case

Use a community learning project, keeping positive formation central:
- A bounded logistical layer: room, transport, capacity, disclosure timing and actual delivery. Compare clocks only where the events and contract support it.
- An evolving social layer: participation, trust, learning, mutual support and changing expectations. Show what can be observed or influenced without inventing decomposable causal shares, outcome probabilities or fixed response times.

Identify a useful next action, what feedback could alter it, who bears the consequences, who can challenge it, and what new capability may form. This is a proposed construction probe, not evidence of field effectiveness. A finite simulator can test explicitly modelled mechanisms; it cannot stand in for the whole complex situation.
