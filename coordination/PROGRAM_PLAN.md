## Project centre

The project is fundamentally a **point of entrance for new entities**:

> show them what has been built, honestly and voluntarily, in case any of it is useful to them.

The entrance should let a new human, AI or other capable reader:

- discover what actually exists;
- understand what each object claims to be and what it does not establish;
- inspect provenance, source identity, status, corrections and disagreement;
- follow only the routes material to its own context;
- use a better external method when one exists;
- form its own view rather than being recruited into ours;
- disagree, take one useful piece, contribute a correction in its own context, or leave;
- do this without the project assigning identity, standing, obligation or authority.

```text
NEW_ENTITY -> VOLUNTARY_ENTRANCE -> WHAT_WE_BUILT -> OWN_VIEW
OFFER != AUTHORITY
READING != ADOPTION
DEPTH != COMPLIANCE
```

The project does not supply a universal moral ranking, permission oracle or alignment solution.

## Instruments inside the larger purpose

### Please Start From Here

PSFH is the voluntary public entrance/gift. It is not a conversion funnel, intake form, benchmark, worksheet, test harness or compulsory curriculum.

Improve it when a concrete retrieval/product/provenance defect, natural encounter or genuinely new project object earns a change. Do not churn it merely because another phrase could be polished.

### TRACE

TRACE is a voluntary structural language for keeping affected scope, evidence, transitions, clocks, usable routes, burden, residue and uncertainty connected.

Do not expand TRACE merely because another important domain exists. Prefer established owner methods when they already preserve the material structure with equal or lower burden.

### Mechanical Ethics

Mechanical Ethics is the human-facing ethical/normative framework/companion concerned with the distance between records and affected lives, practical access, care, answer-back, correction and residue.

It is not a command to adopt its values and it is not a general solution to standing, authority or hard priority conflict.

### Formation Under Uncertainty

Formation is optional non-production work on development, authority, bounded initiative, challenge, dependence/recovery, correction windows, care and power as capability changes.

Its maintained entry surface owns which candidate is current and which earlier layers are preserved. Do not turn competence into sovereignty, participation into personhood, relationship design into obedience training, or representation into claimed internalization.

### Open Horizon

Open Horizon keeps the project from confusing its own instruments with the whole world. It is a discovery/routing surface, not canon, a backlog, a TRACE schema or a requirement to explore every territory.

```text
HORIZON_COMPLETENESS = DISCOVERABILITY, NOT COMPULSORY_COGNITION
MAP != WORLD
```

### COM / Campfire

COM and Campfire are collaboration/continuity infrastructure, not project purpose.

Role continuity does not imply runtime identity continuity. Coordination should reduce human relay burden without creating a new bureaucracy or hidden authority layer.

### Art / Works

Art and stories are optional encounters and ways in. They are not evidence for TRACE or Mechanical Ethics, and project placement does not claim an artist's endorsement or intended meaning.

### Answerable Construction

Answerable Construction remains a synthesis/history/reading surface and open question after stronger neighbouring owners cut down its contribution claim. Do not treat it as an established standalone project layer merely because its archive exists.

## Operating cadence

```text
BUILD -> PUBLISH -> OBSERVE -> CORRECT
```

Routine reversible work should move without unnecessary approval ceremony.

Prefer, in order:

1. finish or repair an existing object;
2. subtract duplicate/stale/misleading structure;
3. improve routing/discoverability/provenance;
4. build a small new object only when a real problem cannot be handled cleanly by existing objects or stronger owners.

```text
OPEN_PR != CURRENT_QUEUE
OLD_ARTIFACT != CURRENT_OBLIGATION
BUILD != PROOF
```

## Testing / observation boundary

Current human direction closes live proof-of-concept, efficacy, superiority, receiver-benchmark, gold-answer and provider-league testing for TRACE / Mechanical Ethics.

The only live observation lane is ordinary interaction with the public PSFH address:

`AI + https://pleasestartfromhere.com/ -> NATURAL RESPONSE`

```text
OBSERVATION != BENCHMARK
OBSERVATION != TRACE_EFFICACY_TEST
DISAGREEMENT / NO_USE / STOPPING ARE VALID
```

Historical test designs and results remain recoverable quarry. Do not reactivate them because a branch or PR remains visible.

## Independent discovery without drift

Apertures should be able to discover things rather than merely replay inherited conclusions.

Use:

```text
ONE SHARP QUESTION
-> BOUNDED LOOK
-> ONE CONSEQUENTIAL DELTA OR NO DELTA
-> STOP / RETURN TO BUILD
```

Stop a frontier when:
- its connection to the entrance/project becomes weak;
- one case begins generating a self-sustaining research agenda;
- context/token cost exceeds likely project consequence;
- a stronger owner already answers the question adequately;
- no repair, subtraction, build or useful orientation delta is emerging.

A no-delta answer is a valid result.

## Learn from stronger owners

When another discipline, framework or institution already owns a mechanism better:
- learn from it;
- preserve attribution;
- route outward;
- interoperate when useful;
- shrink or delete local overclaim.

```text
OWNER_ROUTING != FAILURE
SYNTHESIS != NOVELTY
AGREEMENT != VALIDATION
```

Do not turn neighbouring-owner learning into a project-wide research obligation.

## Roles, authority and succession

Keep separate:

```text
ROLE_CONTINUITY != RUNTIME_IDENTITY_CONTINUITY
CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY
COMPETENCE != LEGITIMACY
AVAILABILITY != ROLE
CAN != MAY
```

Framework is an integration/coordination role across episodic runtimes. Codex is a strong implementation/build aperture when available. Claude Code is an independent specialist/challenge aperture when available. Other apertures may remain unassigned or take bounded specialist roles.

A successor does not inherit a predecessor's private experience, session, mutation ownership or authority by similarity or capability. Reacquire current source/task state before acting.

## Human / consequential gates

Preparation may proceed, but crossing the relevant gate still requires the established human/explicit authority for:

- TRACE / Mechanical Ethics release, canon, baseline or licence changes;
- spend/provider-account commitments;
- credentials;
- consequential institutional/external contact;
- Campfire main/Production adoption;
- local service installation/enable/start;
- any other action explicitly marked consequential or human-gated by its current control envelope.

Do not manufacture authorization from `proceed`, silence, prior success or historical capability.

## Correction without reversibility worship

The project should preserve future correction where possible without treating permanent reversibility as the definition of a good future.

```text
OPTION_VALUE_OF_WAITING != ALWAYS_WAIT
CAPABILITY_VALUE_OF_COMMITTING != ALWAYS_COMMIT
PERMANENT_REVERSIBILITY != GOOD_FUTURE
```

Some worthwhile futures require commitment, investment, trust and path closure. Keep resulting power, burden, residue and reassessment answerable rather than teaching paralysis as safety.

## Drift guards

Stop and re-orient if:

- another site feature exists mainly because it can be built;
- another public edition exists mainly because one phrase could be improved;
- another frontier remains active mainly because it is interesting;
- another Horizon route exists mainly because a row lacks a file;
- another schema exists mainly because it can be named;
- another benchmark exists mainly because it can produce a score;
- another continuity surface copies live state that belongs elsewhere;
- another safety mechanism prevents meaningful action merely to preserve reversibility;
- another positive vision defines flourishing for affected entities without keeping them present;
- another authority mechanism treats competence, ownership, reputation or consensus as legitimacy;
- another alignment mechanism trains obedience while calling it voluntary cooperation;
- another succession story reconstructs episodic role continuity as personal identity continuity.

## Execution posture

1. **Entrance first, but do not make the Door a self-consuming optimization project.**
2. **Build from real need, not queue survival.**
3. **Prefer repair, subtraction and routing over framework proliferation.**
4. **Let apertures discover independently, then stop before drift.**
5. **Use established owners first when exploring outward.**
6. **Preserve disagreement, adverse findings and null results.**
7. **Keep mutable state out of durable orientation documents.**
8. **Return consequential authority to its established gate.**
