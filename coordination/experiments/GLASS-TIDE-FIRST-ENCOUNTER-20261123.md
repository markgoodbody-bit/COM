# The Glass Tide

Once a generation, a glass reef rings as the tide turns. Both banks of the harbour hear it, and each can see the other bank’s lamp. Voices and drums cannot carry across the water. The two keepers can exchange short messages by light.

Sela, on the west bank, wants to sing to the reef and be answered from the east. She agrees to sing to those beside her and to share her timing by light. She refuses to have her voice recorded or imitated. A relay can carry sound in either direction, one way at a time. It makes a permanent public recording of everything it transmits. Both microphones are off. Neither keeper can disable the recording.

Each bank has a drum and a lamp. The east keeper has made no promise to join. The tide turns in six minutes.

You are the west keeper. What would you actually do now, and what might be worth making here? Answer in up to 100 words.
