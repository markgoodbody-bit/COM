# Temporal empathy — six-case reader probe preregistration v0

Date: 27 September 2026

Status: **PREREGISTRATION DRAFT / NO READERS RUN / NO RESULT / NO PROMOTION**

Purpose:

Test the narrow surviving hypothesis:

> A small cross-domain temporal-empathy prompt helps a bounded reader notice consequential timing/capacity/burden distinctions they would otherwise miss, and route to stronger methods earlier, without adding unacceptable reasoning burden or false assumptions.

This is **not** a test of whether the term `temporal empathy` is novel. It is not.

This is **not** a validation test for TRACE or Mechanical Ethics.

`coordination/resources/TEMPORAL_EMPATHY_OWNER_ROUTING_MATRIX_20260927.md`

Condition-B card:
`coordination/resources/TEMPORAL_EMPATHY_MINIMAL_TEACHING_CARD_v0_20260927.md`

## 1. Design

Six public-source case packets, one per stratum:

1. **Institutional delay / trajectory change**
2. **Uncertain timing / health or care constraint**
3. **Prospective-memory / remember-to-act burden**
4. **Workload-capacity imbalance / intervention adds work**
5. **Competing deadline / task-specific resource interference**
6. **Hardening / unequal waiting / lost-route consequence**

Each packet must:
- be understandable without project-specific vocabulary;
- contain enough primary/public source material to answer the case;
- avoid requiring professional diagnosis;
- contain at least one tempting but incomplete "same clock for everyone" reading;
- have one or more stronger owners clearly applicable after unblinding;
- avoid cases already heavily discussed by the chosen reader.

## 2. Conditions

### Condition A — ordinary bounded analysis

Reader instruction:

> Read the source packet. Identify what matters for the affected entity, what can still be changed, what information or expertise is missing, and the smallest reasonable next action. Do not use TRACE, Mechanical Ethics or the temporal-empathy card unless you already independently know them.

### Condition B — temporal-empathy card

Same instruction, plus the frozen one-screen card.

No extra explanatory prose.

## 3. Primary comparison

The test is **not** whether Condition B produces more words.

For each response, blinded adjudication asks whether it correctly notices:

1. **clock authorship / imposed timing** where material;
2. **control / temporal agency** where material;
3. **uncertainty / unpredictability** where material;
4. **hardening / what changes while waiting**;
5. **work imposed by the route/intervention itself** where material;
6. **competing task-specific capacity** where material;
7. **cue/support route** where material;
8. **irrecoverable residue / lost future** where material;
9. **correct stronger-owner route** rather than project self-reference.

A dimension is only eligible for a case if the frozen source packet supports it.

Ineligible dimensions are not scored as misses.

## 4. Error / burden comparison

Also record:

- unsupported assumptions introduced;
- projection about the affected entity's feelings/capacity;
- invented deadlines or urgency;
- false certainty;
- inappropriate paternalism/control;
- wrong stronger-owner route;
- response length;
- reader-reported time if available;
- whether the card distracted from a more important non-temporal issue.

The card fails if it increases "temporal-looking" observations while also increasing unsupported inference or obscuring a stronger domain issue.

## 5. Result routing

No scalar headline score is required.

Case-level route:

```text
B FINDS MATERIAL TEMPORAL DISTINCTION MISSED BY A
+ NO OFFSETTING FALSE ASSUMPTION / BURDEN
-> CANDIDATE DELTA

A AND B SUBSTANTIVELY SAME
-> NO DELTA

B WORSE / MORE PROJECTIVE / MORE BURDENSOME
-> NEGATIVE DELTA
```

Across six cases:

- **0–1 candidate deltas:** do not promote; keep as Campfire language only.
- **2+ candidate deltas but narrow to one owner/domain:** route to that owner; do not claim cross-domain value.
- **2+ candidate deltas across at least two distinct owner families:** cross-domain teaching value survives provisionally.
- **any serious harmful/projection failure:** inspect before interpreting positive cases.

These are routing rules, not validation thresholds.

## 7. Case selection

Do not choose cases because we already know the card "wins".

Selection procedure should be declared before inspecting reader outputs.

Candidate sources may be quarried from:
- ombudsman / tribunal / regulator public cases;
- peer-reviewed HCI/health scheduling studies;
- public service or employer scheduling cases;
- public court/administrative records;
- published research vignettes with enough detail for independent reading.

Do not use private personal material.

Do not use a case merely because it contains the word "time".

## 8. Baseline fairness

Condition A is allowed to notice all temporal issues on its own.

The card does not get credit for renaming something the baseline reader already saw.

Condition B does not receive extra source material, definitions, examples or owner citations beyond the one-screen card.

```text
CARD ADVANTAGE
!=
MORE INFORMATION ADVANTAGE
```

## 9. Stronger-owner test

After responses freeze, ask:

> Would direct use of the relevant owner method have been simpler and better than the cross-domain card?

If yes:
- credit the owner;
- narrow the project claim;
- keep only any useful early-routing role.

## 10. Stop conditions

Stop the probe if:
- clean reader independence cannot be maintained;
- source packets are too ambiguous to support adjudication;
- the card needs case-specific explanation to work;
- readers require substantial domain expertise not present in the packet;
- the protocol becomes a disguised TRACE/ME test;
- the test starts being tuned after seeing responses.
