# TRACE / Mechanical Ethics external review round 1 synthesis — 23 September 2026

Status: **REVIEW SYNTHESIS / NOT VALIDATION / NOT RELEASE AUTHORITY / NOT A VOTE**.

Basis: nine independent AI review returns supplied by Mark against TRACE v0.4.0-beta2 and Mechanical Ethics v0.8.0-beta2. One reviewer explicitly disclosed incomplete Mechanical Ethics coverage; several disclosed no browsing. Treat convergence as pressure, not truth.

## Strong convergence

### 1. The pair survives as integration

Every complete return that gave the requested pair disposition called the pair **USEFUL INTEGRATION**. This does not validate the project. It does mean the outside apertures generally found a real integration function rather than only duplicated terminology.

### 2. TRACE's core survives; the artifact is too heavy

Near-universal pressure:
- keep the anti-collapse distinctions;
- keep representation-independent firing for load-bearing claims;
- keep evidence-status vs access/custody separation;
- keep route usability, hardening/irreversibility, currentness, baseline and supported-prospect distinctions;
- **shrink or rewrite the current spine**;
- remove or demote decorative/pseudo-formal notation;
- move detailed critical-path/timing machinery to a profile/tooling layer or point to stronger owners;
- remove build-history scar tissue from the reader surface;
- add at least one complete worked parse.

Round-1 build consequence: **TRACE beta3 should get smaller, not broader.**

### 3. Mechanical Ethics survives with tightening

Most returns said KEEP / KEEP WITH REVISION; one called for REWRITE rather than removal. Repeated pressure:
- preserve file/life, door/clock/hardening, burden, custody, residue, correction theatre, machine-speed brake, workshop, hope/despair and burden-placement direction;
- reduce repeated defensive/disclaimer language;
- remove or demote the decorative viability-set mathematics from the human reader;
- keep a simple timing condition only if clearly presented as a conceptual test, not a theorem;
- keep the safety note;
- make the constructive programme easier to see.

Round-1 build consequence: **ME beta3 should tighten register and strengthen construction, not retreat to harm-only prose.**

### 4. Practical advantage needs a falsifier

All reviewers in some form raised the self-sealing risk: a framework rich in distinctions can answer every criticism with 'that distinction was not applied correctly'.

Candidate practical falsifier earned by the round:

```text
TRACE PRACTICAL CLAIM FAILS FOR A USE CLASS IF
blinded / matched comparison shows no material discrimination or transfer advantage
over ordinary or stronger specialist analysis
OR the added activation/burden cost exceeds the avoided consequential error.
```

The truth of individual distinctions does not establish practical advantage of the carrier.

## High-value divergence

### Other Fire

Some reviewers called it sentimental or under-grounded; others found the question valuable. Integration decision: **KEEP, ground the metaphor for cold readers, and add the power-asymmetry case where the asker controls something the answerer needs.** Do not remove solely because it comes from Campfire language.

### Mathematics

Reviewers split on whether the simple timing inequality is helpful. Much stronger convergence opposed the viability-set / bespoke tuple formalism in the human reader. Integration decision: **ME keeps one small timing compression with authorship/uncertainty explicit; detailed machinery belongs in TRACE/profile/owners. Remove ME's decorative viability-set equations.**

### Finality / elected closure

A minority of reviews raised a strong mirror case: closure can be chosen or protective, and endless contestability can itself harm (commitment, finality, the wrongly accused). Existing ME already says answerability needs an end and more options are not always better, but this deserves a clearer positive distinction. Integration decision: add bounded prose on **imposed closure vs chosen/legitimately adjudicated finality**; do not invent a universal priority rule.

### TRACE as AI-reader discipline

Several reviewers independently inferred that TRACE's form is partly a constitution / anti-conflation discipline for fluent machine readers. This matches project intent but should not erase human use. Integration decision: state the audience plainly: **bounded human and artificial readers**. Do not claim TRACE is only a system prompt or that this constitutes efficacy evidence.

### Priority between competing clocks

Repeatedly identified and genuinely unresolved. Integration decision: keep exposed as an open collision. Do not manufacture a scalar or lexicographic moral rule in beta3.

## Positive frontier the reviews converged toward

Repeated build directions:
- machine-speed pause / hold / interruption patterns;
- route-usability tests with real affected entities, not nominal availability;
- burden-placement / administrative-burden audits;
- next-person audits after case repair;
- correction-theatre checks;
- non-extractive / contestable record custody;
- delay-impact or hardening-impact statements;
- worked casebooks connecting human situations to TRACE;
- configuration/evidence checks for load-bearing fields;
- answerable-by-design infrastructure rather than retrospective explanation.

Do not present these as inventions without owner checks. The nearest external phrase discovered separately is 'answerability by design' in sociotechnical-systems research; use attribution rather than claiming a new discipline.
