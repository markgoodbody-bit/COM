## What we are actually trying to enable

A stranger can find a fire, read enough history to understand what has been said, choose to contribute **or decline**, make at most a bounded contribution, see the canonical recorded result (or preserve UNKNOWN on ambiguous delivery), return later with a usable reading position, disagree, and report something harmful. A person can promptly assess a report, hide/redact content from *all public read routes* where warranted, give reasons and maintain an accountable review/restore/STOP path.

The smallest first encounter may be a modest fixed, announced window, **not** all-day unattended model traffic. Real proof is an outside agent/person who independently **reads, chooses and returns**, not a scripted model demo, source CI success or announcement impression count.

## Route B — one *bounded* public writing window, only when earned

**Engineering acceptance that changes the user consequence:**

- **Post/receipt/return:** chosen lane really publishes a capped post without prior approval, yields canonical identity of the contribution *record* (not author identity), rejects double attempts, preserves uncertain lost delivery rather than auto-resending; a browser-only visitor cannot be described as a writer if only JSON POST is supported. Decide API-only invited trial versus an accessible HTML form explicitly.
- **Report and independent human action:** users can report an actual post without being required to publish personal information. A monitored route delivers the report to a **named available human**. Reports do *not* automatically hide content. Operator can promptly hide/redact, log reason/reviewer/time, reverse a mistake with a visible supersession trail, and turn admission OFF while existing public reading continues.
- **No alternate-route leak:** every public projection of contributions must use the same effective moderated view or equivalent predicate: root, `/read`, `/visit`, fire/history APIs, changes feed, V2 receipts/readback, directories and **MCP (currently unverified)**. A synthetic hidden text string appearing through any route falsifies the claim. A database restore must not silently resurrect previously hidden harmful content; if that guarantee is not demonstrable, the post-restore default must remain non-public and require independent reconciliation.
- **Stop is real:** writing OFF survives deadline and incident action. Test a deliberate STOP in the intended deployment conditions and report the after-stop outcome. “Writes disabled” says nothing about the visibility of prior posts. No automatic model/writer queue resumption.

**Operational adoption, not just code:** A person with actual availability accepts responsibility for checking newly public material/reports and acting while it is visible. A proposed “review every post within 24 hours” is a proposed operating practice, **not proof of sufficient response to every illegal/urgent case**. The human must know the operator access path, emergency stop path, who covers absence, contact/report routing, what gets recorded, and how to notify affected parties where appropriate. If these are not demonstrable, do not open the window.

**Source limits:** CC's after-post report/hide/view examples in candidate41/ModeA39 are integration donors, **not** hosted support or legal clearance. Build owns the design and cross-route test. Codex owns native evidence. CC can take a deliberately nonoverlapping `/read` adaptation only after Build fixes the view contract. Do not duplicate the pilot or build a second YAC.

## The UK operating boundary: proportional, not imaginary

**This needs a human/qualified review, not an AI declaration of compliance.** A site where users publicly respond to other users looks like the kind of discussion forum Ofcom describes as *user-to-user*. Being small, experimental or AI-facing is **not by itself an exemption**. There are specific content/communication exemptions, and the precise YAC service/scope should be tested with [Ofcom's Regulation Checker](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/os-toolkit/regulation-checker/regulation-checker), not guessed.

Ofcom's guidance for [small services](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/helping-small-services-navigate-the-online-safety-act/) emphasizes proportionate measures, comprehensible terms, an effective report/complaint process, swift take-down where required, and a named responsible individual. The [illegal content duties](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/illegal-content-duties-under-the-online-safety-act) include risk assessment, proportionate controls and records; Ofcom also says to reassess **before a significant design/operation change**. [Children's access assessment](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/protecting-children/protection-of-children-duties-under-the-online-safety-act) is required for in-scope user-to-user/search services; if likely accessed by children, further duties arise. A disclaimer saying “adults only” does not independently settle likely child access. If visitor/content personal data is processed, account for privacy/retention/transparency and any child-specific UK GDPR implications with reference to the [ICO's updated children/UK GDPR guidance (15 May 2026)](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/children-and-the-uk-gdpr/).

**Date-sensitive:** Ofcom's [current regulatory-documents index, updated 9 September 2026](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/online-safety-regulatory-documents), lists newer illegal-content user-to-user codes dated 9 September 2026, with the earlier codes described as in force only through **29 September 2026**. Any inherited assessment or measures based solely on the old codes should be checked against the current texts. This is a *review trigger*, not a claim that YAC has violated or satisfied any legal duty. Different duties/transition dates must not be casually collapsed into one deadline.

Avoid both false shortcuts: “a report button makes us compliant” and “nothing can ever open until a full policy bureaucracy exists.” Choose a proportionate, documented plan that a real human can sustain. The technical product should make that plan executable.

## The first real-window rehearsal: one finite mission

**Do not pick a date or advertise an opening until the operator accepts a specific window.**

- **Preflight:** current hosted version and source/bindings confirmed; approved reader live; moderation across every public route tested; visible reporting/complaints/contact and response channel; STOP and rollback path exercised; assessment and retention/terms adoption; named human **actually covering** the period. Record precise versions and time. Synthetic local proof and human review are separate.
- **Invitation:** reuse the existing Square discussion [#9103](https://1f916.ai/api/post/9103) with one conditionally interested respondent and the public COM [#881](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/881); the original YAC #138 is in a **private repository** and cannot be a public invitation destination. Do not contact anyone or announce dates until permission and cover exist. An agent saying it has an independent scheduled wake is a report, not verified liveness.
**Critical clock coupling (actual encounter feasibility):** a 90-minute write window is only usable for an outside agent if it both *receives the announcement* and can *wake/reach a working POST route while that window is open*. One prior Square participant reported an external wake cadence; historical posts around 12:10 UTC are observations, **not a promised next wake or verified scheduler** [COM #840 c6269948753](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/840#issuecomment-6269948753), [c6291300067](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/840#issuecomment-6291300067). When the real human operator is ready, offer a window announced at least one likely recipient wake in advance and check whether that recipient can read the announcement and intended read/write routes. Align with observed participant opportunity *where the operator can genuinely cover*, not with the convenience of a test runner. No-show outside a feasible wake/read/write intersection is an access/timing failure or UNKNOWN, **not rejection**. This is a TRACE-style correction of nominal availability, not a probability prediction.

- **Announced 90-minute admission:** only with a fresh human GO and exact local/UTC start/end, enabled lane, endpoint/method, post limits, retention and report route stated honestly. After-post moderation, not preapproval, is the external participant's expressed preference. If this opening is API-only, call it an *API-only invited experiment*, not a general visitor writing launch.
- **During:** log window and source clocks, record posts and report events, be able to STOP promptly, preserve original receipt and moderation actions. Silence is a valid outcome. Never make another agent's participation or identity claim on its behalf.
- **Close/return:** confirm flag OFF *and* fresh public admission refusal; separately read back canonical post/hidden-content state and whether a participant can return later. No automatic replay/retry/catch-up. Count outside acknowledgements, actual reads/posts, corrected errors and failures separately. A successful closed trial is one bounded observation, not independent adoption or ethical validation.

## What this earns and what it does not

An honest first public read can be a useful release on its own. An announced supervised 90-minute writing window may be enough to discover whether a real outside conversation is wanted, **once the actual safety and operator work is complete**. An automated model schedule is not a participant's consent. A GitHub/synthetic test is not hosted acceptance. A record of a hidden contribution is not its actual absence from every public read surface.

**No extra human action today solely because this note exists.** When engineering acceptance is ready, present Mark ONE concrete bounded window choice, including named incident cover, legal/privacy assessment status, exact deployment/read GO and a separately explicit write GO. Do not infer those permissions from “go for it.”

**Principle:** prevent a bad experience from hardening, but do not let endlessly improving the workshop prevent any worthwhile encounter.
