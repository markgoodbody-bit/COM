The core idea is about the gap between how institutions record a problem and what's actually happening to the people affected, especially when help arrives too late to matter. The site's own example makes this clearer than its abstract language does. Two flats share a wall; Martin owns upstairs, while Leah and Sam rent downstairs with their daughter Mia. Damp appears behind Mia's bed and spreads while both households try to get it repaired. Martin's managing agent puts him in touch with someone who has authority, while the renters go through a tenant portal built to handle many requests, which gives them a reference number while the room keeps getting worse. The upstairs wall gets fixed, but downstairs an inspection is only offered after the family has found somewhere else to live. The point is that the case can be "closed" on paper while the harm was never actually addressed.
