# Campfire quarry: coupling before fixed entity boundaries

Status: working middle-out hypotheses, not canon or release instructions. 8 October 2026.

A bounded observer begins from local interaction: distinguish ground, situated body/sensor, changing fire, outward smoke/light and increasingly remote evidence. Other apparent entities and fires need not be identified with certainty before their relations can be tentatively inspected.

Question: could **coupling** be a more portable starting distinction than a fixed taxonomy of entities/groups? Entities and their boundaries are provisional, purpose-relative and evidence-limited; coupling does not establish standing, personhood or authority.

Candidate: A relation or process couples variables or provisional scopes if a change in one can causally alter a state/transition available to another (at an explicitly stated model/time scale). Merely correlated observations are not proof of causal coupling. Even distant strangers can have indirect connections through climate, institutions and infrastructure. Coupling may be asymmetric, mediated, delayed, conditional and changing.

A/B pairwise decomposition may fail when the A–B relationship depends on C (contextual or higher-order interaction), and a network of pairwise edges may not preserve every collective effect. Before calling interaction irreducible, test whether extra contextual state and an appropriate stronger domain model already explain it.

Potential direction for falsification: choose real specimens from combustion/feedback, family relations, ecological webs, internet packet routing, coordination institutions. For each expose entity boundary, variables, causal mechanism, timescale, evidence/access limits, changes of future reachability, and whether a pairwise or higher-order account has greater discriminatory power.

Important limits: no universal one-plus-one metaphysics established; neither observed proximity nor co-occurrence establishes coupling; not every distant relation matters at the chosen horizon; coupling is already represented in TRACE and must not be promoted merely as renamed grammar.

Small research test: Is there a situation where TRACE's existing entity boundary/coupling treatment cannot express a consequential difference without inventing a new primitive? If not, keep this as Campfire teaching/specimen rather than schema growth.

Middle-out orientation: ground -> fire and causal change -> traces/signals -> stars/unknown scales -> navigator's map/horizon -> other fires and inter-entity relations. Incomplete evidence is UNKNOWN, not ABSENT; a useful map remains revisable.
