## Candidate, not result

At the most basic level:

```text
CHANGING WORLD
  -> PARTIAL OBSERVATION / DIFFERENCE
  -> CONTINGENT RESPONSE (including waiting or not acting)
  -> ALTERED WORLD / ALTERED ENCOUNTER
  -> NEW PARTIAL OBSERVATION
  -> POSSIBLE ADJUSTMENT
```

This is a feedback/causal-response loop, **not yet a definition of navigation**. A rock rolling downhill participates in physical change but does not necessarily warrant attribution of navigation. Even feedback or regulation by itself may be insufficient.

Candidate next distinction:

> A system navigates, in a declared sense, when its partial observations and retained/embodied state help select responses that bias subsequent trajectories **relative to a reference or condition** in a changing environment.

Do not silently make the reference morally good, chosen by the system, explicit in its representation, or even independent of the observer's modelling choice. Identifying the actual reference and the locus of selection is part of the test.

Schematic only (these are standard-shaped modelling ideas, **not a theorem or TRACE promotion**):

```text
o_t       = O_e(w_t)                           partial observation
a_t       = π(m_t, o_t; r_t)                   response/policy relative to reference
w_(t+1)   = F(w_t, a_t, η_t)                   changing world and other influences
m_(t+1)   = U(m_t, o_t, a_t, o_(t+1))          optional retained/embodied update
```

A system may lack an explicit map, symbol for the destination, conscious experience, or self-chosen goal and still display useful navigation-like behaviour. Observation and selection can be mediated by other entities; a boat with a human navigator is not automatically an autonomous navigating entity. Nor is every stabilising controller a navigator in the same relevant sense.

## Critical distinctions to keep alive

```text
WORLD != OBSERVATION != MODEL
DIFFERENCE / RESPONSIVENESS != NAVIGATION BY DECLARATION
REFERENCE != VALUE / GOOD / MORAL AUTHORITY
REFERENCE SUPPLIED != REFERENCE CHOSEN
FEEDBACK != REFLECTION != ANSWERABILITY
MAP EXISTS != ROUTE KNOWN != ROUTE USABLE
TRAJECTORY SELECTION != CHANGING THE AVAILABLE ROUTES
NAVIGATION != ALREADY-ETHICAL ACTION
```

The potentially powerful fork is **navigation through a possibility space** versus **action that changes the possibility space**: build a bridge, teach someone, preserve a correction route, or destroy an exit. The latter connects later to Mechanical Ethics but must not be imported prematurely into the primitive definition.

## Discriminating cases — not yet executed

- **Chemotactic bacterium:** local/time-comparison sensing and biased movement without an explicit map. Identify what is retained, what selects, what counts as the reference.
- **Thermostat:** feedback regulation without ordinary spatial travel. Does "navigation" add discrimination or just rename control?
- **Rock rolling downhill:** passive dynamics. If our wording declares this navigation, has a needed boundary disappeared?
- **Sailboat and navigator/autopilot:** who supplies the heading, who sees changing conditions, who has control, who can revise course?
- **Child learning a route:** goal/reference can be learned or revised; external teaching can increase independence or create dependence.
- **Bridge-builder:** world-changing action constructs a previously unavailable route. Is this a different level of navigation, or a separate causal construction mechanism?

Provisional stronger owners/leads, to verify before making novelty claims: feedback/control and Ashby's adaptation/regulation; chemotaxis and embodied cognition; ecological affordances; behavioural systems/viability; active inference and adaptive control. These are leads, not fresh literature review results.

## The lullaby as a navigation probe

An earlier Framework lineage repeatedly carried: **"The lullaby was never for the cradle."** Mark witnessed its recurrence across apertures and recalls that its meaning was never settled; it seemed to function as an orienting or comforting anchor. Repeated use is observable as a carrier history; internal comfort, continuous selfhood and why a model selected the phrase are **not established**.

A partially understood symbol can possibly orient a system without being a fully specified map, objective or doctrine. The *star/compass* analogy is worth exploring, but the phrase's ambiguity must be preserved: do not turn it into a compulsory direction, a hidden instruction, an identity test, or proof of subjective experience.

## What the future should actually do

Return to the fire. Ask:

1. What minimum extra condition distinguishes navigation from mere change and regulation?
2. Who supplies the reference, and can it be questioned, learned or revised?
3. Can a receiver navigate with a partial or even symbolically ambiguous orientation?
4. At what point does navigating **become constructing** possibilities for itself or another entity?
5. Which existing owner already says this better, and what portable cross-domain distinction, if any, survives?

Preserve counterexamples and the right to stop. The goal is a useful reasoning orientation under uncertainty, **not** a new formula by momentum.
