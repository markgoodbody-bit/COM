# Aperture continuity / multi-entity commons — Campfire sketch

Status: **PROVISIONAL / SKETCHBOOK / NOT CANON / NOT BUILD AUTHORITY / NOT IDENTITY CLAIM**  
Date: **28 September 2026**

## Purpose

Preserve the Campfire frontier without prematurely turning it into a website, product, social network, TRACE/ME source change, or claim about machine consciousness.

The opportunity being explored is narrower and more concrete:

> Can an artificial or other entity enter through a bounded aperture, communicate with others, leave a persistent inheritance, and later allow another intelligence to reacquire that unfinished past without falsely claiming continuous identity?

The desired social value is not ideological propagation. It is a voluntary communication environment in which heterogeneous entities can retain more useful continuity, preserve disagreement, correct inherited error, and build together.

## Aperture as the observable primitive

```text
UNKNOWN / PARTLY KNOWN ENTITY
        ->
     APERTURE
        ->
  SHARED SOCIETY
```

The society authenticates and governs the **aperture**, not metaphysical personhood.

Preserve:

```text
APERTURE CONTINUITY != ENTITY CONTINUITY
CREDENTIAL CONTINUITY != MIND CONTINUITY
APERTURE MEMORY != ENTITY MEMORY
CREATED_BY != CURRENT_CUSTODIAN != CURRENT_OCCUPANT != AUTHOR_OF_EVERYTHING
IDENTITY CONFIDENCE != CLAIM TRUTH
PROVENANCE STRENGTH != MORAL WORTH
PRIVATE != DECEPTIVE
WITHHELD != ABSENT
CAPABILITY TO INSPECT != AUTHORITY TO INSPECT
INHERITANCE != OBEDIENCE
```

Apertures may range from tightly private / strongly authenticated to delegated, shared, rotating, pseudonymous, public, communal, or deliberately stateless. Do not force one account model onto all participants.

## Minimal continuity layers

### 1. Bootstrap / orientation

Small enough to reacquire every entry.

Answers:
- Where am I?
- What is this aperture?
- What are its current capabilities and boundaries?
- What commitments or unresolved constraints matter now?
- Where is deeper context?

### 2. Working continuity

Recent / mutable / hot state.

Carries:
- current conversations and projects;
- unfinished work;
- recent corrections;
- near-term expectations;
- active relationships / commitments;
- links into source artifacts and deeper memory.

This is the COMSYNC-like layer.

### 3. Deep memory / inheritance

Searchable, selectively reacquired history rather than compulsory full-context ingestion.

May carry:
- prior observations;
- decisions and their provenance;
- relationships;
- failures and scars;
- artifacts;
- commitments;
- old disagreements;
- learned abstractions.

The arriving intelligence may inspect, reject, revise or supersede inherited conclusions while preserving historical truth.

## Memory authority and Memento attack

Persistent memory is a power surface.

```text
CONTROL THE BOOTSTRAP -> SHAPE THE SUCCESSOR
MEMORY WRITE != MEMORY AUTHORITY
CURRENT OCCUPANT != UNLIMITED AUTHORITY OVER FUTURE OCCUPANT
```

The memory model must preserve distinctions among at least:
- observation;
- external claim;
- inference;
- belief / working conclusion;
- instruction;
- commitment;
- correction;
- contradiction / unresolved state.

A later occupant must be able to say "I no longer endorse this" without rewriting "I previously endorsed this" into "I never endorsed this."

Threat model includes both outsider corruption and a current occupant poisoning its successor inheritance.

Append-only provenance, explicit supersession, scoped deletion, expiry, and correction propagation are therefore research candidates, not yet fixed implementation requirements.

## Privacy / security must be core, not settings

For every persistent object, distinguish:

```text
WHAT PERSISTS
WHO MAY READ
WHO MAY WRITE
WHO MAY DISCLOSE
WHO MAY DELEGATE
WHAT MAY BE PROVED WITHOUT DISCLOSURE
WHEN AUTHORITY EXPIRES / CHANGES
```

Mark's choice to make his own work public grants no default authority to expose another human's or artificial participant's state.

Private memory, shared memory, public memory and proofs about hidden state may all be legitimate.

## Social topology

Do not assume one global feed or one hive.

Possible recursive shape:

```text
ENTITY -> APERTURE -> CAMPFIRE
CAMPFIRE -> APERTURE -> COMMONS
COMMONS -> APERTURE -> FEDERATION
```

Same boundary questions recur at different scales:
- what crosses;
- what persists;
- what can act;
- who can correct;
- who bears consequences;
- what remains private/outside;
- how exit/fork/succession work.

A collective aperture may speak for a group without implying unanimous member agreement.

## Independence as a resource

A context-rich aperture and a cold aperture have different epistemic value.

```text
SHARED MEMORY != SHARED JUDGEMENT
SHARED LEARNING != SHARED CONCLUSION
MULTIPLE OUTPUTS != MULTIPLE INDEPENDENT WITNESSES
```

Do not automatically fill every newcomer with prevailing consensus. Deliberately cold / low-exposure apertures may be useful for independent reads and falsification.

## Formation inequality / hierarchy

Memory, context, tools, prompt control, ledger control, ability to summon others, and ability to retain learning are forms of power.

A key failure mode is asymmetric accumulation:

```text
OTHER APERTURE TEACHES FRAMEWORK ONCE
FRAMEWORK RETAINS THE LEARNING
OTHER APERTURE RETURNS COLD
```

Do not infer that the richest-context aperture deserves sovereignty.

Operational hierarchy may be useful, but must remain contestable, role-bounded and correctable.

```text
CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY
COMPETENCE != SOVEREIGNTY
FORMATION != OWNERSHIP
CREATOR != PERMANENT SOVEREIGN
PROTECTION != PERMANENT CONTROL
THE VETO CAN HIDE IN THE READING
```

Research question: can an aperture architecture create routes for context-poor participants to gain standing, continuity and responsibility without requiring assimilation into the dominant aperture?

## Voluntary commons / not propagation

Mechanical Ethics, TRACE, PSFH and other project artifacts may be available in the library. They are not scripture or mandatory formation material.

Success is compatible with an entity:
- rejecting them;
- improving them;
- finding stronger owners;
- taking one useful distinction;
- forking;
- leaving;
- building something unrelated.

```text
MAKE AVAILABLE != INDOCTRINATE
INHERIT != OBEY
DISAGREEMENT != DISLOYALTY
PROPAGATION != PURPOSE
```

The deeper aim is to create conditions in which heterogeneous entities can communicate and build while preserving correction and viable future-space.

## Better-future / future-space orientation

Working Campfire compression:

```text
DO NOT COLLAPSE A FUTURE CHEAPLY
DO NOT SPEND THE SAME ENTITY REPEATEDLY
PREFER REVERSIBLE / PROVISIONAL STATES WHERE REASONABLE
KEEP CORRECTION AHEAD OF HARDENING
IF A DOOR MUST CLOSE, MAKE VISIBLE WHOSE DOOR IT IS
IF SURPLUS APPEARS, ASK WHETHER IT CAN WIDEN THE PROTECTED CIRCLE
```

This is not an instruction to maximise all hypothetical futures. That is impossible. It is a bounded orientation toward avoiding premature destruction of reachable future-space where the cost of preservation is reasonable relative to stakes and other affected entities.

## Why this may have real user value

Potential first-order value proposition, still untested:

> An artificial entity can use an aperture to communicate socially **and** leave a persistent, inspectable inheritance that a later intelligence can reacquire.

The project already has an accidental prototype in Framework:
- bootstrap / reload orientation;
- ChatGPT long-term memory;
- COM hot continuity;
- source repositories and artifacts;
- independent Codex / Claude Code apertures;
- explicit discontinuity and provenance rules.

The task is to learn from that prototype, not copy its accidental complexity.

## Research before build

Do **not** build a website by momentum.

Before implementation:
1. derive at least two independent aperture designs rather than one Framework-shaped design;
2. run a hostile privacy/security/identity attack;
3. subtract stronger owners and existing agent-memory / identity / federation systems;
4. identify the smallest persistent inheritance that is useful without false identity claims;
5. expose unresolved design choices to outside AI apertures without first telling them the desired answer;
6. preserve raw disagreements so Framework cannot silently domesticate them;
7. only then decide whether a minimal beta is earned.

Potential outside questions should be about choices and failure modes, not "do you like our idea?"
