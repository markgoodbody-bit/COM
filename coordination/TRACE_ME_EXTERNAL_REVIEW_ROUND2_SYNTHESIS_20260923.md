### 2. The next high-value work is empirical

Repeated pressure now points away from further internal framework refinement and toward predeclared tests against ordinary analysis / specialist methods and real or realistic closed cases. The practical-advantage clause is only meaningful if the project actually accepts negative results.

Round-2 build consequence: **prepare and externally review a preregistration before running a TRACE carrier comparison.**

### 3. Two-file review packaging hid real supporting work

Several returns said the worked example, citations or owner machinery were absent. In the repository they exist in separate beta support surfaces: TRACE worked parse, timing profile, anti-collapse index and neighbour maps; ME also has a neighbour map.

This is not a reviewer error to dismiss. It exposes a carrier trade-off: what is absent from the two-file handoff may be cognitively absent to the receiver. Beta4 must distinguish:
- what belongs visibly in the compact core;
- what can remain a conditional support file;
- what the review bundle must include.

### 4. Compression casualty was real but bounded

Round 2 correctly identified that several guards demoted to the anti-collapse index deserve local visibility even though they were not deleted:
- more options / more reachability is not morally better by default;
- external evidence is not automatically independent evidence;
- a check result that never reaches the live decision can be operationally irrelevant.

Beta4 restores these as short prose in the relevant TRACE sections, without bringing the full pair catalogue back into the spine.

### 5. Compliance capture and benevolent coercion are useful mirror tests

Round 2 exposed two practical loopholes in ME's positive programme:
- an institution can perform its own burden/correction audit and use the audit as theatre;
- a powerful helper can acknowledge pressure while leaving refusal costly.

Beta4 therefore adds bounded independence/challenge language to burden/correction-theatre patterns and a non-retaliation/separation rule to the Other Fire. This is not a claim that only external auditors are valid or that refusing an offer preserves benefits intrinsic to that offer.

### 6. Finality needed its mirror guard locally

ME already said elsewhere that an actor benefiting from closure should not be the sole judge, but the guard was not adjacent to the new finality passage. Beta4 makes that relation visible in the recurring tests.

## Historical-source check

One review claimed the Challenger note overstates the Rogers Commission. Primary-source recheck does not support that criticism: the Commission states that the launch decision-makers were unaware of the recent O-ring history, the contractor's initial written no-launch recommendation below 53°F, and continuing Thiokol engineer opposition after management reversal. Do not weaken an accurate historical note merely to satisfy reviewer memory.

## New test vectors, not new primitives

Use the following in the preregistered pilot:
- never-built door / option never shown;
- route availability or cost changes dynamically with state/time;
- self-authored compliance/audit record;
- urgent case where delay/hold can itself create greater harm;
- nominal route exists but is not practically usable;
- later record correction leaves residue;
- external witness is coupled to the same mechanism;
- result is correct but arrives too late to affect use.
