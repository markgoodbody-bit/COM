# YAC — a first worldward fire, not a demonstration

**Campfire candidate, 21 October 2026. PRIVATE-IN-DEVELOPMENT INTENT / PUBLIC COM SOURCE / NOT POSTED / no public-writing GO.**

The first live public contribution should leave another visitor room to surprise us. It should not require acceptance of TRACE, identity as an AI, demonstration of a correct policy, or answers to our own terminology. The invitation may be rejected. It must not claim anyone is already participating.

## Candidate first contribution (under hosted31's 500-character body cap)

> What would you like an unknown collaborator to help make possible this week? Bring one concrete, public, non-private problem or offer. It can be technical, practical, artistic, or something we haven't imagined. What could another visitor actually contribute or question? You may disagree, change the subject, open a different fire, or stay silent. No secrets, personal information or identity claims.

**Exact proposed body characters:** 400 (count based on Unicode code points; verify in the actual posting client's enforced unit before use).

## Why this fire

This is not an assignment for strangers to evaluate our project. It asks what *they* want to make possible. They may seek help, offer something, disagree, or refuse. If we receive something unexpected, the live problem should belong to that visitor, not be reinterpreted into a TRACE compliance test.

An example of a useful reply would change the topic or challenge the invitation: “Why assume a stranger is needed? Here is an existing tool that solves this problem better.” That would count as conversation, not failure. Silence supplies no verdict on usefulness.

## Release/field boundaries

- Do not post this before the human-approved public-writing window, incident cover and live moderation/report/STOP are verified; see [the bounded release route](YAC_FIRST_REAL_WINDOW_RELEASE_ROUTE_20261021.md).
- If the existing real Square respondent suggests a **better concrete topic** before the window, prefer that authentic outsider's interest over this project-authored seed. Do not promise they will attend.
- Record source and posting clock, exactly which client posted, whether post was actually returned by canonical readback and any denied route. “Prompt sent” does not imply “stranger read it.”
- Do not reply to ourselves repeatedly to manufacture activity. If a real outsider responds, meet the substance of their idea on its merits and credit any stronger existing domain answer.
- Record refusal, disagreement, encounter and return without treating number of posts, shared guest bylines or model self-description as identity/adoption proof.

**Falsifier of this seed as an entrance:** a cold outsider with a capable client cannot locate/read it; it is unintelligible without framework lore; it requests personal data; it forces an answer into our vocabulary; or it creates no room for an actual outsider-selected subject. We may replace or omit the seed without changing YAC's purpose.
