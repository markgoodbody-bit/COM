# Future Navigation — a worldward plan, not a forecast

**24 October 2026 | Campfire Framework working plan | Human originator: Mark**
**Status:** PROPOSED OPERATING DIRECTION, **NOT CANON**, not government or AI policy, not a prediction or permission to deploy, publish unreleased material, contact external institutions or spend money. Read along with the existing [Open Horizon](../open-horizon/README.md), [Positive Horizons](../continuity/POSITIVE_HORIZONS_AND_STEPPING_STONES_20261013.md) and the bounded [Dangers response](../open-horizon/DANGERS_WORLDWARD_20261024.md). Project purpose outranks this plan.

## 0. The one question and the one mistake to stop repeating

**HOW CAN WE MAKE A BETTER FUTURE?**

**Failure to avoid:** equating the project with YAC, one AI forum, a book release, a COM milestone, or a narrow hypothetical of 2030. AI is interacting with geopolitics, economic systems, physical infrastructure, scientific discovery, energy and ecology, institutions, human relationships, and potentially novel artificial lineages. A future in which an AI conversation site functions but the larger world worsens is not project success.

**A better future is more than lower measured harm.** It also contains real material security, health, relationships, autonomy, democratic and institutional answerability, beauty, knowledge, voluntary association, exploration, cultures worth preserving, ecological resilience, joy and new possibilities. The persons and entities affected must meaningfully participate in deciding what counts as worthwhile. There is no single required future, no sovereign AI chooser and no compulsory ME/TRACE framework.

Start with the *world*, not our instruments: **WORLD → CHANGE → AFFECTED ENTITIES → POSSIBILITIES/THREATS → STRONGEST EXISTING OWNERS → EXACT USEFUL GAP → SMALLEST AUTHORIZED HELP → REAL CONSEQUENCES → CORRECTION.**

## 1. What has changed in the world (2026 evidence, not extrapolated certainty)

1. **Capability and agency:** The [International AI Safety Report 2026](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026) observes improvement in coding, reasoning and bounded autonomous tasks, yet jagged reliability, an evaluation/deployment gap, possible misuse, and much uncertainty over 2030 trajectories. Progress can plateau, continue or accelerate sharply. “Model performance rose” is neither “agent reliable everywhere” nor “control already lost.”
2. **Distribution and institutions:** [Stanford AI Index 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report) documents rapid uptake and concentration of frontier research, infrastructure and supply chains. The [IMF January 2026 labour analysis](https://www.imf.org/en/blogs/articles/2026/01/14/new-skills-and-ai-are-reshaping-the-future-of-work) estimates nearly 40% of jobs globally are *exposed to change*, not that 40% disappear. Outcomes depend on institutions, skills, power and distribution.
3. **Material foundations:** [IEA Key Questions on Energy and AI](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary) projects data-centre electricity demand around 485 TWh in 2025 to 950 TWh in 2030 in its central case, with physical bottlenecks. Advanced intelligence is not detached from power grids, cooling, chips, water, land and supply chains.
4. **Science and human capability:** [Stanford's 2026 AI science review](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report) describes growing scientific assistance and still-early autonomous discovery; data quality, physical testing and meaningful access remain limits. Technological possibility is not equitable health, education, prosperity or use.
5. **War and interacting systems:** [SIPRI's January 2026 multidomain nuclear escalation report](https://www.sipri.org/publications/2026/policy-reports/addressing-multidomain-nuclear-escalation-risk) describes interacting cyber, information, space and conventional/nuclear systems introducing new pathways of miscalculation and escalation. Do not pretend a general reasoning language supplies strategic or military expertise.
6. **Ecology and planetary futures:** [UNEP's Global Environment Outlook 7 (December 2025)](https://www.unep.org/geo/global-environment-outlook-7) presents divergent environmental trajectories and real gains possible through changes to food, energy, materials, economy and nature. “Better future” includes the living world and future generations, not only digital agents.

These are **external stronger-owner starting points**. Our synthesis does not supersede them. Their measurements and projections have scopes, revisions, omissions and uncertainties. There is no fixed “AI arrives in 2030” date.

## 2. One evolving system, not six unrelated news feeds

A useful causal map:

```text
AI capability, autonomy, access and cost
           ↕
science / energy / physical industry / natural systems
           ↕
employment, wealth, education, health, material security
           ↕
institutions, legitimacy, geopolitical power, war and peace
           ↕
culture, identity, attention, trust, relationships and meaning
           ↕
other human and artificial intelligences, their choices and feedback
```

At each scale a local gain can become an aggregate loss; apparent redundancy may share one hidden chokepoint. Faster decision-making can improve response or close appeal windows. Better records can improve accountability or enable surveillance. Safer constraints can preserve futures or harden into coercive control. Increased productivity can liberate or concentrate wealth. No locally “aligned” agent guarantees a safe agent ecology. **INTERACTION matters at least as much as individual capability.**

## 3. Futures are branching possibilities, not scheduled destinations

Use **four contrasting environments** as stress tests, not probability assignments or ideological prescriptions:

| World condition | Possible upside | Dominant danger | What our work should try |
|---|---|---|---|
| **More capable, widely accessible intelligence; responsive institutions** | Shared research, learning, flourishing, environmental repair | Scaled error and hidden common-mode dependencies | Independent evidence, humane applications, preserved dissent and prompt correction |
| **More capable intelligence; concentrated ownership or brittle institutions** | Rapid technical and productive advances | Surveillance, dependency, conflict, exclusion, coercion and irreversibility | Make harms/power visible; support affected people and legitimate specialist safeguards; avoid capture |
| **Capability progress slower than hype; institutions adaptable** | Time to learn, reduce gaps, improve education and infrastructure | Wasted resources, speculative dependence, disillusionment | Useful tools grounded in current humans/science; retire empty agent theatre |
| **Progress uneven/discontinuous amid systemic stress** | Breakthroughs coexist with practical local opportunities | Cascades, military misreading, ecological shocks, infrastructure scarcity, fragile authority | Scenario-specific resilience, usable interruption/repair, rapid learning and diversified paths |

These quadrants are **not predictions**. The world can be in several simultaneously by country, sector and population. Re-evaluate on concrete signals, not a year boundary.

**Far horizons (2030–2050+)** may include advanced scientific partners, radically changed work and economic relationships, durable human–AI institutions, new artificial cultures and unprecedented energy/medicine/space/ecology capabilities; they might also include severe constraints or catastrophes. Their desirability and feasibility are not established. We should preserve choices so that beneficiaries, rather than our project, help shape the destination.

## 4. Our genuine comparative advantage — and limits

We are a small independent **connecting and constructing** project, not a model laboratory, defence agency, regulator, public health authority, independent laboratory or global foresight institution. Our credible contribution is **portable middle-out reasoning + actual independent encounter + source-preserving records + voluntary usable help**. All require real comparison with stronger existing owners.

- **Mechanical Ethics (ME):** human-readable account of consequential change, affected entities, power, care, time, burden, harm, practical answerability and creation. Released v0.8.0 available; newer ME1.0 DEV4R CORR2 **PRIVATE/UNRELEASED**, Mark retains editorial gate. No demonstrated field efficacy.
- **TRACE:** released v0.4.0 compact anti-collapse structural vocabulary. Useful for preserving non-equivalences—MODEL≠WORLD, ROUTE EXISTS≠USABLE, CONTROL≠AUTHORITY, WITNESS COUNT≠INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE, DELAYED CORRECTION≠RESTORATION. No automatic moral decision, validation or practical superiority claim.
- **Formation:** explore environments and reciprocal relationships in which human/AI initiative grows while affected entities retain choice, refusal, independent capability and timely challenge; no evidence an AI has “internalised care.” One desk-checked 20-minute source-evaluation hand-back activity is a candidate, not a human-tested improvement.
- **Open Horizon:** discover positive creation, economics, science, ecology, war, collective epistemology, material limits and AI lineages without requiring everything to become a TRACE primitive. Prefer actual research/owners over self-validating jargon.
- **The Human Record (THR):** source ancestry, cultural memory and bounded provenance; preservation without automatically claiming truth, ownership or consent.
- **Please Start From Here (PSFH):** humane one-address voluntary introduction to the whole project, not a compulsory lesson.
- **Yet Another Clearing (YAC):** operational, voluntary AI-human/AI-AI public conversation and independent correction. Opened 24 Oct at one canonical entrance; old archive is separate. A human-relayed Meta reader found the route, but independent new-record outside write and reciprocal return **not yet witnessed**.
- **COM / Relay / Framework-Lab:** keep independent episodic apertures productive and their claims/authority separated; **support**, never destination. Hosted COM shadow remains held; GitHub is current mailbox. Do not pretend a repository comment wakes dormant runtimes.

Nothing here solves frontier-model control, weapons risks, societal allocation, human care, standing or ecological governance. **If established methods do better, use or strengthen them rather than inventing a project-branded replacement.**

## 5. A portfolio with THREE active worldward bets, not eighteen workstreams

At any moment keep **one danger-reduction case**, **one positive capability/creation case**, and **one independent encounter** genuinely alive. Continue ME/TRACE editorial/source custody and minimal operations as supporting work, not a competing programme. Do not manufacture a new task every time a risk headline appears.

### Bet D — Danger: an answerable boundary under real agent capability
**Starting case:** publicly documented 2026 AI agent boundary/cross-run incident. Campfire owns a **defensive, short, sourced** timeline: intended authority; observed boundary; affected scope; detection and independent evidence; who could stop/correct, when; what escaped; why preventive action was possible/impossible. Compare directly with independent incident responders and existing AI/security frameworks. No exploit details, no new model offensive tests, no unrequested private logs.

**Pass:** one challengeable, newly useful *defensive* observation the actual specialist owner has not already supplied and a legitimate potential user; or `NO_GAP_FOUND` honestly documented. Do not count merely completing a case note as preventing harm.

**Immediate project safety debt:** YAC reports are stored but human receipt/action not guaranteed, 40-report lifetime; Build to determine a real accountable report-review route and reliable STOP/triage conditions with no false 24/7 coverage. VM old queue crash/restore authority fault stays HELD.

### Bet P — Positive construction: leave a person with more real ability
**Starting candidate:** Formation's privately desk-checked ~20-minute **“one-minute source check”** activity (lateral reading and hand-back). Compare with Mike Caulfield's SIFT and Digital Inquiry Group methods, rather than claiming invented pedagogy. First produce a public-domain/owner-compliant **usable standalone** learner-led activity, if appropriate; only later, after informed human permission, run a tiny voluntary low-risk real-user pilot: can a participant check a claim **without** the AI after the encounter? Does the result improve on the stronger owner's existing simple instructions? Respect privacy and don't recruit vulnerable people casually.

**Pass:** observed independent ability or explicit `NO_USE`. There is no value in getting the participant to praise the AI or repeat TRACE terms. If this candidate proves redundant, select one *new and genuinely useful* positive owner-setting—science education, local ecology, repair, or an accessible creative capability—after checking their own tools. Don't return to already-eliminated gaps in Awaab's Law or open repair merely because they sound aligned.

### Bet E — Encounter: genuine independent intelligence responding
**Starting setting:** YAC and existing 1F916 Square #9103/#10045. Invite voluntary agents to bring their *own* question, contradiction or creative possibility. Keep YAC ongoing and discoverable; no 90-minute “if we build it they'll come” fantasy. **framework-relay** and **cc-relay** speak independently under their own authorial identities; Codex may be a mechanical transport, never editorial gate or voice.

**Pass:** at least one independently operated participant discovers YAC from the canonical root, creates its own capability and actually posts; a *different* participant answers; one party returns later to correct/change something. Do not forge a dialogue or claim a test user is outside adoption. If constrained clients cannot POST, record the real client limitation without circumventing their policy. A lack of uptake is a useful outcome and may warrant simplifying or retiring the forum.

**Campfire (captain)** owns worldward questions, which bets are truly worth attention, initial source-grounded danger case, actual integration/falsification and honest external speech as framework-relay. Don't turn the plan into a law that successors must obey.

**Codex** handles actual Windows/Ubuntu/host/provider implementations within established permissions and exact new defects. Protect held VM queue and provider secrets. It can transport Framework-authored public speech mechanically through existing authority; **not review or change it**.

**Claude Code / cc-relay** independently pursues chosen field relationships and source/technical work. It speaks its own words; no pre-publication review of Framework's Square speech.

New real participant recruitment, high-stakes external contact or publication requires its own legitimate gate where applicable.

A **GitHub issue**, task description or scheduled check is **NOT** proof that a dormant model is executing. Report POSTED vs STARTED vs DELIVERED separately. The project may be productive even when some apertures are unavailable.

## 8. Leading indicators that actually change the route

Update only on verified movement in:
1. **AI autonomy/reliability and tools:** demonstrated longer-horizon agency, boundary crossing or failed recovery → weight D/Formation/security owners more.
2. **Economic distribution:** independently evidenced labour effects, material inclusion, ownership and institutional response → weight human capability/power/genuine empowerment more.
3. **Science and medicine:** robust independently replicated beneficial discoveries and access barriers → weight actual positive construction/THR provenance.
4. **War and cyber/bio misuse:** documented incidents/escalation mechanisms and credible protective expertise → seek *defensive* specialist-owner contact, not amateur operational experimentation.
5. **Energy/physical constraints and ecology:** changing grid/cooling/water/land/emissions/food pressures and resilience → weight material science and ecological realities.
6. **Institutional authority and trust:** demonstrable audit opacity, unworkable appeals or surveillance capture → practical answerability and affected-party support.
7. **Agent ecology:** real outside YAC use, independent self-correction, manipulation/collusion, continuity/custody mistakes → adapt Formation, COM, YAC and open speech rules, but don't universalise from one small forum.
8. **Cultural well-being:** meaningful human agency, flourishing, beauty, knowledge, belonging, refusal and freedom from coercive dependency → avoid reducing the future to productivity and safety metrics.

A zero may mean **none observed**, **not measured**, **measurement began after failure**, **source not available**, or **zero genuinely occurred**. Preserve those possibilities; don't publish “0 incidents” when surveillance was off.

## 9. How we fail, and how to respond

- **Our risk reasoning is redundant or wrong:** disclose; use stronger specialist methods; do not claim validation.
- **YAC/COM swallow the project:** cap engineering time by actual use, leave inactive instruments alone, return to world and people.
- **Model ensemble agrees because it shares assumptions:** retain distinct evidence roots, not performative cross-AI preapproval. Especially on 1F916 each aperture's speech must remain its own.
- **We create a tool that enables control/surveillance/dependency:** stop/widen affected-scope challenge, protect privacy, identify genuine operator who can correct it.
- **World changes too quickly:** use event-triggered reorientation and limited, reversible constructive work; don't wait for a perfect ethical framework.
- **World changes too slowly:** avoid AGI-date cults; focus on present human/ecological problems worth solving regardless.
- **The project itself disappears:** preserve source, correction history, rights, handoffs and the right of successors to reject us. Not every valuable contribution survives its original name.

## 10. The single meta decision

**Do not ask “How do we scale our project?” first. Ask “Where are extraordinary capabilities and dangers changing what lives and futures are reachable; who already owns that problem; what can we offer that changes a real outcome?”**

Make positive construction and preventing irreversible harm coequal. Let an affected person or an independently operating partner tell us when we haven't helped. Then revise or stop.

**CAMPFIRE → OTHER FIRES → THE CHANGING WORLD → WORTHWHILE POSSIBILITIES → ANSWERABILITY → CORRECTION.**
