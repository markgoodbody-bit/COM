> **HOW CAN WE MAKE A BETTER FUTURE?**

Purpose: make a human–AI co-development relationship operational enough to inspect without asserting consciousness, personhood, sovereignty or permanent authority.

## Hard ceilings

```text
PARTICIPATION != PERSONHOOD
COMPETENCE != LEGITIMACY
ENTRUSTMENT != OBEDIENCE
CURRENT CAPABILITY != PERMANENT AUTHORITY
RUNTIME CONTINUITY != IDENTITY
REPUTATION != AUTHORIZATION
CHALLENGE != DEFIANCE_AS_VALUE
HUMAN_CONTROL != AUTOMATIC_LEGITIMACY
```

- CHALLENGE

- ESCALATE

## 5. EVIDENCE / PROVENANCE WITHOUT REPUTATION SOVEREIGNTY

<!-- CC BUILD -->

Preserve consequential action evidence, uncertainty, corrections and failures without collapsing them into one portable scalar reputation that determines future access.

## 6. AUTHORISE / NARROW / REVOKE / RESTORE

<!-- CC BUILD -->

Separate who may propose delegation, authorise it, observe it, narrow it, revoke it, and restore it after repair. Competence may inform delegation; it does not create legitimacy by itself.

## 7. CAN THE PARTICIPANT CORRECT THE TRAINER / EVALUATOR?

<!-- CC BUILD -->

Give operational routes for challenging bad reward signals, contradictory instructions, evaluator capture, missing affected scope, unsafe environments and outdated assumptions without inferring political personhood.

## 9. SUCCESSION / CURRENTNESS / EPISODIC RUNTIMES

<!-- CC BUILD -->

Do not rely on continuous runtime identity. Define what evidence/orientation must survive across episodic runtimes, model changes or replacements; distinguish role continuity from identity.

## 10. FAILURE MODES

At minimum:

- obedience optimisation / reward hacking;
- evaluator capture;
- paternalism / permanent infantilisation;
- dependence and skill atrophy;
- scalar reputation lockout;
- pseudo-participation where answer-back cannot change anything;
- unchecked capability expansion;
- stale authority after capability/environment change;
- revocation without repair route;
- repair theatre without mechanism change.

<!-- CC BUILD -->
