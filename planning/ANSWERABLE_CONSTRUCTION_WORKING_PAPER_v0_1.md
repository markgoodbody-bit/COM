## Product goal

Produce a coherent paper that makes one constructive argument:

> avoiding unjustified closure is not enough; a better future also requires building real, viable, domain-owned positive conditions through commitments, institutions and relationships that remain answerable to affected scope, authority, burden, changing goals and the limits of correction.

The paper must be understandable without prior TRACE/ME vocabulary and must not become a procedure or decision rule.

## Required sections

1. **The missing positive half** — why option preservation / harm avoidance is insufficient.
2. **Constructive commitment** — how closing paths can create larger shared capabilities, and where commitment becomes domination or exclusion.
3. **Viability has a bearer** — function/scale/scope; organisational survival is not automatically the good.
4. **Authority and delegation** — competence informs bounded delegation but does not create legitimacy; propose/advise/authorise/implement/review/revoke remain distinct.
5. **Three domains** — institutional transition, ecological/climate transition, human–AI co-development. Use the Casebook and stronger domain owners; do not claim the project replaces them.
6. **Irreversibility and correction** — answerability != reversibility; repair cannot be fictional.
7. **Transition capacity before closure** — material support/readiness without turning support into an impossible universal veto.
8. **Human–AI co-development** — reciprocal challenge, evaluator correction, recovery capacity, bounded initiative and currentness without personhood/identity theatre.
9. **Field lesson from D046** — one compact operational example showing claim binding, temporal handoff/single mutator, publication receipt, independent witness, attribution correction and the v0.2 event-bound repair. Do not present D046 as efficacy evidence.
10. **What this does not solve** — standing, hard priority collisions, legitimate objective revision, finite repair, universal priority law.
11. **Owner routing** — name where established fields already own mechanisms better.
12. **Project remainder** — state the smallest useful cross-domain contribution left after routing stronger owners.

## Acceptance criteria

A successful v0.1 should:

- read as one paper rather than stitched project notes;
- make the positive construction claim clearly within the first page;
- use concrete examples before dense vocabulary;
- keep the strongest unresolved wound visible rather than hiding it in caveats;
- say what is project-specific and what belongs to stronger external owners;
- be shorter and clearer than the combined Reader + Casebook + architecture;
- end with a narrow, falsifiable project remainder rather than a brand claim;
- include a compact basis/limits section with exact internal source pointers.
