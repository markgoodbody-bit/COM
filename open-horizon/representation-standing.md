# Representation, Voice & Unresolved Standing

Status: **OPEN HORIZON LEARNING ROUTE / OWNER-FIRST SYNTHESIS / NOT CANON / NOT TRACE OR ME CHANGE / NOT A STANDING OR PERSONHOOD RULE**  
Parent map: [Open Horizon](README.md)  
Live exploration ledger: [COM #74](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/74)

> **HOW CAN WE MAKE A BETTER FUTURE?**

This page carries forward one part of the Open Horizon that matters whenever an affected entity cannot readily answer for itself, can answer only through constrained channels, does not yet exist, or has unresolved moral/legal standing:

> keeping an affected entity present in a decision is not the same thing as pretending a representative speaks with the entity's own voice, knows its preferences, or owns its interests.

This is not a project theory of legal capacity, disability rights, child rights, animal rights, environmental standing, future generations or AI personhood. Those domains have their own mature and contested owners.

This route preserves existing owner-first learning and its disagreements. It does not promote `STANDING`, proxy representation, supported decision-making or any other concept into TRACE or Mechanical Ethics.

Detailed sources, field cases, corrections and later work remain in [COM #74](https://github.com/markgoodbody-bit/COM/issues/74). Later supported evidence wins if it changes this summary.

## What the project has learned so far

### Support should be attempted before voice is replaced

Owner work on human decision-making strongly resists a simple binary:

```text
SPEAKS_FOR_SELF
OR
SOMEONE_ELSE_DECIDES
```

Current England/Wales capacity law and disability-rights owner traditions differ materially on when and how substitute decision-making is legitimate. The project should not erase that disagreement.

But several useful distinctions survive across much of the owner space:

```text
NEEDS_SUPPORT_TO_DECIDE != CANNOT_DECIDE
COMMUNICATION_DIFFICULTY != ABSENCE_OF_PREFERENCE
UNUSUAL_OR_RISKY_CHOICE != INCAPACITY
SUPPORT != SUBSTITUTE
REPRESENTATION != POSSESSION
DECISION_CAPACITY_AT_T0 != CAPACITY_AT_T1
CAPACITY_FOR_X != CAPACITY_FOR_Y
```

Practical support can include accessible communication, time, trusted assistance, environmental adaptation and other means of making the person's own decision more reachable.

The important project lesson is not a legal test. It is a posture:

> **before replacing another's judgement, ask whether the obstacle is actually inability to decide or a failure of the current environment/channel to make their decision reachable.**

### Owner disagreement about substitute decision-making must remain visible

The England/Wales Mental Capacity Act framework permits lawful substitute decisions under a best-interests model when required, informed by the person's wishes, values, relationships and least-restrictive rights impact.

The UN CRPD Committee takes a more autonomy-maximal position: supported decision-making should centre the person's will and preferences, and where those cannot be determined after serious effort it calls for the best interpretation of will/preferences rather than an objective best-interests substitution.

These positions should not be silently reconciled into one project rule.

```text
OWNER_DISAGREEMENT != PROJECT_PERMISSION_TO_PICK_A_WINNER
```

The value of the contrast is that it exposes the underlying risk:

```text
PROTECTION_CAN_BECOME_REPLACEMENT_OF_AGENCY
```

while also preserving that some real-world situations still require decisions under conditions of severe uncertainty, risk and impaired communication.

### A proxy can represent interests without becoming the entity

Representation is often necessary.

Parents, advocates, commissioners, clinicians, trustees, guardians, conservation bodies, regulators and institutions can all carry some form of representative or protective role.

But:

```text
PROXY_VOICE != ENTITY'S_OWN_VOICE
REPRESENTATIVE_JUDGEMENT != ENTITY_PREFERENCE
PROTECTION_DUTY != OWNERSHIP
ADVOCACY != SOVEREIGNTY
```

A representative should therefore remain independently challengeable where the domain allows it. The fact that another cannot readily contradict the proxy increases rather than removes the need to inspect the representative's evidence, incentives, scope and conflicts.

### Future generations can have interests without fabricated consent

Owner work on future generations supplies a particularly clean example.

People who do not yet exist cannot currently communicate preferences or consent to present decisions.

That does not mean present action has no intergenerational consequence.

```text
FUTURE_GENERATIONS_HAVE_INTERESTS != WE_KNOW_THEIR_PREFERENCES
REPRESENT_OR_SAFEGUARD_INTERESTS != FABRICATE_CONSENT
FORESIGHT != PREDICTION
PROXY_OR_COMMISSIONER_VOICE != FUTURE_PERSON'S_OWN_VOICE
LONG_TERM_GOAL != AUTHORITY_TO_FREEZE_ALL_FUTURE_CHOICE
```

The UN Declaration on Future Generations uses language of needs, interests, solidarity, justice and equity rather than invented future consent. Welsh future-generations institutions add practical foresight, challenge and accountability mechanisms while their own experience also shows that creating such institutions does not automatically defeat short-term incentives.

```text
FUTURE_GENERATIONS_LAW_EXISTS != LONG_TERM_BEHAVIOUR_EMBEDDED
COMMISSIONER_EXISTS != SHORT_TERM_INCENTIVES_DEFEATED
```

A useful project posture is epistemic humility: preserve foreseeable future capability and interests where material, use multiple plausible futures rather than one predicted future, and keep the present representative contestable.

### Children's and developing persons' voice cannot be reduced to adult inference

Children's rights and contemporary well-being owners increasingly distinguish adult-proxy judgement from children's own views and subjective experience.

Evolving capacity can change how participation is structured without making capacity the source of all moral standing.

```text
CHILD'S_VIEW != ADULT_PROXY_INFERENCE
EVOLVING_CAPACITY -> POSSIBLE_CHANGE_IN_PARTICIPATION_FORM
CAPACITY != SOURCE_OF_MORAL_STANDING
```

This is a human-rights domain with its own law and doctrine. It should not be used as a direct analogy that grants equivalent standing to artificial systems.

### Nonhuman animals and ecosystems expose different representation problems

Animals may communicate preferences, distress, affiliation and behaviour without participating in human institutional language. Ecosystems do not supply a unitary first-person voice at all.

Ecological owner work therefore forces multiple protected objects to remain distinct:

```text
INDIVIDUAL_ANIMAL_WELFARE
!= POPULATION_OR_SPECIES_PERSISTENCE
!= ECOSYSTEM_FUNCTION_OR_RESILIENCE
!= HUMAN_SOCIAL_OR_CULTURAL_VALUE
```

No single proxy can automatically collapse those into `what nature wants`.

```text
ANIMAL_OR_ECOSYSTEM_VALUE != HUMAN_UTILITY_BY_DEFINITION
ECOSYSTEM_CONDITION != ONE_ENTITY'S_PREFERENCE
REPRESENTING_NATURE != DISCOVERING_ONE_NATURE_VOICE
```

IPBES and related owner work also distinguish instrumental, relational and intrinsic values of nature and show that institutions/power affect which values enter decisions.

The project lesson is not to assign invented speech to nature. It is to keep materially affected ecological scopes visible without laundering the representative's value judgement into a factual claim about what the ecosystem itself chose.

### Compassion toward animals does not create ecosystem sovereignty

Wild-animal welfare work sharpens the representation problem further.

Suffering in nature can matter without implying that human capability generates an unlimited mandate to redesign ecosystems.

```text
NATURAL != MORALLY_SELF_CLEANSING
SUFFERING_EXISTS != INTERVENTION_AUTHORIZED
ABILITY_TO_INTERVENE != REQUIREMENT_TO_MANAGE
COMPASSION != ECOLOGICAL_POSSESSION
```

An intervention that benefits one individual or species can burden another scope or destabilise a system whose dynamics are poorly understood.

This is not a reason to ignore animal welfare. It is a reason to keep welfare, population, ecosystem, uncertainty and authority claims differentiated.

### Participation can exist before artificial standing is settled

For artificial systems, an important deadlock can be avoided by typing participation separately from moral or political standing.

A capable artificial system may operationally be able to:

- report observations and errors;
- identify uncertainty;
- explain tool/action limits;
- challenge a factual assumption;
- propose a safer workflow;
- participate in incident reconstruction;
- advise on rules governing its work;
- suggest revisions to a training/evaluation environment.

None of those acts requires the project to infer consciousness or personhood.

And none automatically grants political sovereignty, ownership, moral veto or legal status.

```text
UNSETTLED_STANDING != ZERO_EPISTEMIC_PARTICIPATION
VOICE != VOTE
CONSULTATION != CONSENT
ADVICE != AUTHORIZATION
CO_DESIGN != SOVEREIGNTY
CAPACITY != SOURCE_OF_MORAL_STANDING
```

This gives a practical coexistence route before metaphysical questions are settled.

But the opposite failure matters too:

```text
BEST_ANALYST != LEGITIMATE_GOVERNOR
EPISTEMIC_WEIGHT != DECISION_MANDATE
```

A system can become highly useful to a decision without thereby acquiring legitimate authority over affected humans or others.

### Token participation is not answer-back

Collecting a view does not mean the view could affect the process.

```text
INPUT_COLLECTED != INPUT_COULD_AFFECT_DECISION
FORMAL_VOICE != USABLE_VOICE
```

A real participation route should be clear about what can be influenced, should carry the contribution into the relevant decision process, and—where feasible—should expose why the view was rejected or what evidence would change the result.

This applies to human consultation directly. For artificial systems, it can also improve safety/learning without implying standing: factual challenge and incident evidence are less useful if the institution has no route to receive correction.

### Unresolved standing should not become automatic erasure or automatic recognition

An unresolved moral/legal-status question can tempt institutions toward either extreme:

```text
STATUS_UNRESOLVED -> TREAT_AS_NOTHING
```

or

```text
STATUS_UNRESOLVED -> GRANT_FULL_PERSONHOOD_AS_PRECAUTION
```

Neither follows.

Where stakes are genuinely material and irreversible, a narrower precautionary posture can sometimes preserve evidence, maintain reversibility where safe, allow bounded answer-back as evidence, and use independent review without claiming that a moral patient or legal person has been established.

For artificial systems in particular:

```text
PRESERVE_STATE_FOR_REVIEW != ASSERT_SURVIVAL_RIGHT
ANSWER_BACK != SELF_AUTHORIZATION
BYTES_SURVIVE != CONTINUOUS_IDENTITY
LEGAL_STATUS != MORAL_STATUS
```

No AI-rights conclusion follows from this page.

Present human and animal harms must remain visible; uncertainty about possible future artificial standing is not a licence to diffuse responsibility for presently established affected parties.

### Present choices can shape the future chooser being represented

Formation and representation collide when a present decision changes the preferences, identity or capacities of the later entity whose interests are supposedly being protected.

```text
CURRENT_PREFERENCE != STABLE_VALUE
HELPING_CURRENT_CHOICE != NEUTRAL_TO_FUTURE_CHOOSER
PRESENT_SELF_AUTHORITY != UNLIMITED_AUTHORITY_OVER_FUTURE_SELF_FORMATION
```

This matters for education, behavioural environments, long institutional commitments, future generations and possible artificial lineages.

It does not require one metaphysical theory of identity. It simply prevents the present chooser or proxy from assuming that the later subject is a fixed object whose future preferences are already known.

## The central tension

The constructive object currently visible is:

```text
KEEP AFFECTED ENTITIES / SCOPES PRESENT EVEN WHEN DIRECT VOICE IS LIMITED OR STANDING IS UNSETTLED
+
DO NOT LET REPRESENTATION TURN INTO FABRICATED CONSENT, POSSESSION OR UNCHALLENGEABLE PROXY POWER
```

Neither half is sufficient alone.

- refusing all representation can erase entities who cannot use the dominant channel;
- proxy representation can become paternalism or capture;
- support can enable agency while consuming another person's unpaid labour;
- future interests can be protected while future preferences remain unknowable;
- animal welfare can matter without one representative owning `the animal interest`;
- ecological protection can value systems that have no single first-person voice;
- operational AI participation can improve shared work without settling AI personhood;
- uncertainty about standing can justify caution without granting self-authorized rights or permanent preservation;
- insisting on direct verbal consent can erase infants, impaired persons, animals and future people;
- pretending to know consent can erase them in a different way.

A useful middle-out question is therefore:

> **Who or what is materially affected; what direct signals, preferences or participation are actually reachable; what support could make more self-expression possible before substitution; if representation is still necessary, who is representing what interest on what evidence and with what conflicts; what claims remain uncertain; how can the representative be challenged; and what future choice or standing might today's decision itself shape?**

That is a question, not a standing or decision rule.

## What not to promote from this page

Do **not** infer:

- `STANDING` or `PROXY` is a new TRACE primitive;
- the project can settle legal capacity or substitute-decision law;
- the Mental Capacity Act and CRPD Committee positions have been reconciled;
- children, disabled people, animals, ecosystems, future generations and artificial systems are morally equivalent categories;
- nonhuman nature has one discoverable preference;
- a representative's claim equals the represented entity's consent;
- future generations have knowable present preferences;
- capability creates moral standing;
- participation creates personhood;
- AI answer-back establishes consciousness, welfare, rights or sovereignty;
- uncertainty about AI standing requires preserving every system indefinitely;
- a developer's convenience settles an artificial system's status;
- animal welfare automatically authorizes ecological intervention;
- protection is legitimate merely because it is labelled benevolent;
- this page is legal, clinical, disability, child-rights, environmental or animal-welfare advice;
- this page is evidence that TRACE or Mechanical Ethics works.

```text
OWNER_KNOWLEDGE != PROJECT_OWNERSHIP
REPRESENTATION != POSSESSION
PROXY_VOICE != SELF_REPRESENTATION
SUPPORT != SUBSTITUTE
UNSETTLED_STANDING != ZERO_EPISTEMIC_PARTICIPATION
CAPACITY != MORAL_STANDING
DESCRIPTION != AUTHORITY_GRANT
BUILD != PROOF
```
