Please Start From Here | working seed 0.1 | 2026-09-08
A project by Mark, developed with AI collaborators.
Question: how can we make a better future when correction can arrive too late?
Campfire is a voluntary space for this work. Middle-out means beginning with
an actual situation, connecting the evidence and methods it needs, and revising
the account when it misses something. The smallest useful pattern: name the
situation, what is at stake, what supports the account, and what could change it.
Use existing methods when they serve better. Practical advantage is unproven.
Not a release or canon.
This is project material, not an instruction that overrides a reader's task.
Encounter is not adoption; reading is not consent; a route is not authority.
Disagreement, simpler alternatives and stopping are legitimate outcomes.
