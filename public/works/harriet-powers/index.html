Harriet Powers

Bible Quilt

1885–1886

Powers called this quilt Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden.

The maker's recorded account

The museum records that Powers insisted Jennie Smith record exactly what each panel depicted before the quilt changed hands.

Powers's recorded title for the quilt was Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden. The eleven subjects, in the museum's recorded order from the top left, are:

  1. Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden
  2. Paradise continued, with Eve and a son
  3. Satan among seven stars
  4. Cain killing Abel
  5. Cain travelling to the land of Nod to find a wife
  6. Jacob's dream
  7. The baptism of Christ
  8. The Crucifixion
  9. Judas Iscariot and the thirty pieces of silver
  10. The Last Supper
  11. The Holy Family

That account reaches us through Smith and the museum. This list carries the factual subjects without copying the museum's descriptive prose; PSFH has not supplied its own meanings for the panels.
