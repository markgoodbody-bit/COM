Put your criticism into words

A claim might be mistaken, omit a person, or miss a useful alternative. You can write a challenge in ordinary language and keep it for yourself or send it through a route you choose.

This page does not send or receive your words. It does not save your form in a site database, browser storage or analytics. The preview, copy and download happen in this browser only. Creating a packet is not submitting it, reaching the project, securing anonymity or obtaining a promise of review.

Privacy: avoid names, secrets, contact details, personal histories or other sensitive information. A file you save or text you paste elsewhere can be disclosed there; this local tool is not a confidential reporting service.
