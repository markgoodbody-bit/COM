# Work with a situation

Use one question or all six. There is no required order and nothing to submit here. If you want to write, copy the questions into your own notes or continue in the context that brought you here.

## 1. Notice

**What is changing? What can you actually observe?**

Keep what is observed, reported, inferred and unknown distinct. A failed search or fetch does not by itself establish absence.

Go deeper: [Change](nodes/change.md) · [Partial views](nodes/aperture.md)

## 2. Choose

**What actions or possibilities are actually reachable?**

Include waiting or doing nothing where relevant. An option is not usable merely because it can be described.

Go deeper: [Selection and consequences](nodes/selection.md) · [Reachable futures](nodes/futures.md)

## 3. Decide

**What will you do, at what scale, and with what uncertainty?**

A description is not permission. Ask what matters here and whether the actor has the capability and authority needed for the proposed move.

Go deeper: [Significance](nodes/significance.md) · [Power and answerability](nodes/power.md)

## 4. Responsibility

**Who can act, decide, stop or correct? Who carries the work and burden, and who can answer back?**

Do not let delegation or process make affected people disappear from the account.

Go deeper: [Power and answerability](nodes/power.md) · [Care without possession](nodes/care.md)

## 5. Repercussions

**What changes now, later or elsewhere? What becomes hard to undo?**

Notice what possibilities open or close, what correction routes remain reachable, and what residue would remain even after a later repair.

Go deeper: [Time and hardening](nodes/hardening.md) · [Correction and residue](nodes/correction.md)

## 6. Check and correct

**What would show this reading was wrong? What changed after the action?**

Continue, revise, look from another position, use a stronger method, or leave. Agreement is not required.

Go deeper: [Wisdom under pressure](nodes/wisdom.md) · [Challenge or disagree](challenge.md)
