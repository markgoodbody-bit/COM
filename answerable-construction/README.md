# Answerable Construction

Status: **WORKING PROJECT SYNTHESIS / CROSS-OWNER INTEGRATION LENS / NOT CANON / NOT A DECISION RULE / NOT TRACE OR ME**

Project question:

> **HOW CAN WE MAKE A BETTER FUTURE?**

Answerable Construction is the current project-level synthesis around a positive question that the earlier work did not fully answer:

> avoiding unjustified closure is not enough; a better future also requires building real positive conditions through commitments, institutions and relationships that do not hide affected scope, authority, burden, changing goals or the limits of correction.

This directory is a **front door to the existing work**, not another copy of it. The authoritative project objects remain at their source paths and commits.

It is also **not a claim of a novel positive-construction theory or a new traceability mechanism**. Fresh external-owner checks on 12 September 2026 cut that claim substantially: Dynamic Adaptive Policy Pathways already owns much of adaptive planning under deep uncertainty; Responsible Innovation owns anticipation/reflexivity/inclusion/responsiveness around innovation direction; the Capability Approach owns substantive opportunity, conversion conditions and plural ends; just-transition practice owns much of transition burden and social-dialogue structure; Value Sensitive Design/value-change work owns lifecycle value revision; systems engineering already owns lifecycle needs/requirements traceability, controlled change and impact analysis; distributed-responsibility / many-hands work already owns much of the accountability problem created by dispersed contribution.

The current reason to keep this layer is therefore narrower: a project-specific integration lens for noticing when a positive commitment crosses stronger-owner boundaries and material relations are being dropped in the handoff. Where established owners already preserve those relations adequately, use them and add nothing.

`SYNTHESIS != NOVEL_THEORY`
`TRACEABILITY != PROJECT_INVENTION`
`WHERE_OWNER_PRESERVES_RELATION_ADD_NOTHING`

## Current narrow claim

After routing substantive mechanisms back to stronger owners, the smallest project remainder currently defended is:

> **When a positive commitment crosses owner or lifecycle boundaries, keep explicit links to the relevant owner-specific dependencies, affected/represented scopes, authority, clocks, externalised burdens/residue, changing objectives and correction/reassessment limits long enough to notice when the handoff drops one of them. Do not replace the owner's method, and add nothing where existing practice already preserves the relation.**

This is an integration/attention claim, not a framework for choosing the future and not a new engineering traceability method.

It does **not**:
- define flourishing;
- rank genuinely colliding protected claims;
- establish moral standing;
- grant authority;
- replace domain methods;
- replace systems engineering requirements/configuration/traceability practice;
- replace responsible-innovation, value-sensitive-design or transition-justice practice;
- solve distributed responsibility;
- prove practical superiority;
- require permanent reversibility;
- establish conceptual novelty.
