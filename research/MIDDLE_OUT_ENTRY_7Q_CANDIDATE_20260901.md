This is not a universal ontology, ethics solver, domain method or requirement to use project vocabulary.

Use ordinary language.

## Q1 — WHAT IS ACTUALLY CHANGING?

What is the current situation/decision/relationship for this use?

- What is happening now rather than historically?
- What object/boundary are we treating as relevant?
- Is there one nearby boundary alternative that would materially change the action?

Do not solve metaphysical identity unless the case actually requires it.

## Q2 — WHAT CAN ACTUALLY HAPPEN HERE?

What can the relevant actors/entities actually do **now** under present conditions?

- Which stated options are only formal/theoretical?
- What capabilities, tools, access, support, infrastructure or relationships make an option practical?
- What capability could be developed in time to change the action field?

```text
FORMAL OPTION != PRACTICAL ACTION
CURRENT CAPABILITY != DEVELOPABLE CAPABILITY
```

## Q3 — WHO/WHAT IS AFFECTED, AND WHO CAN MAKE WHAT HAPPEN?

Identify only materially relevant scopes.

- Who/what bears benefit, harm, burden, dependence or irreversible loss?
- Who holds relevant evidence/knowledge?
- Who can sense/classify, decide/authorize, actuate, monitor or revoke?
- On what basis/mandate/responsibility?
- Can affected parties answer back in a way that matters before the decision hardens?
- If they cannot answer directly, what evidence/representation route exists and what does it **not** establish?

```text
POWER != MANDATE
ABILITY_TO_ACT != AUTHORITY_TO_OWN_THE_DECISION
FORMAL VOICE != USABLE ANSWER-BACK
```

Do not infer moral standing merely from causal affectedness.

## Q4 — WHAT DOES TIME DO TO THE CHOICE?

What clocks, lead times, path dependencies and lock-ins matter?

- What changes if we act now?
- What changes if we wait?
- What future capability/dependence/incentive/evidence environment does each action construct?
- If materially strategic actors can adapt to the rule/measure, what behavior does it incentivize or make gameable?
- Is the latest safe decision time earlier than visible failure because preparation takes time?

```text
DELAY CHANGES THE WORLD
FAILURE THRESHOLD != LATEST SAFE DECISION TIME
```

Use these only where time, lead-time or strategic adaptation is actually material.

## Q5 — WHAT OWNER / EVIDENCE DO WE NEED?

What remains unknown, disputed or outside the local method's competence?

- Which domain owner/framework/law/science knows this part better?
- What evidence is load-bearing for the action?
- What uncertainty must stay visible?
- What tempting inference is **not** supported?

```text
UNKNOWN != ABSENT
STRUCTURAL INTEGRATION != DOMAIN AUTHORITY
```

Better external structure should be credited and used, not renamed.

## Q6 — WHAT SHOULD HAPPEN NOW?

Give a bounded action/posture, not only a map.

State:
- what should be done, held, delegated, stopped, built, constrained, released or transformed now;
- which function/level owns each part where relevant;
- what harm/burden it imposes or moves;
- what capability/shared future it creates or preserves;
- why the commitment/constraint is proportionate rather than merely restrictive.

Important correction from the six-case run:

```text
MORE OPEN OPTIONS != MORE CAPABILITY
CONSTRAINT / COMMITMENT CAN ENABLE FUTURE ACTION
CAPABILITY CHANGE != AUTOMATIC ENTRUSTMENT EXPANSION
```

## Q7 — HOW DOES THIS REMAIN CORRECTABLE?

What would make the action tighten, relax, stop, transfer control, reopen, or transform?

- What should be monitored?
- What evidence would materially change the decision?
- Is the correction/appeal/reporting route practically usable before serious harm hardens?
- Is there enough people/time/money/expertise/tool access/transition capacity to execute correction?
- What loss would remain even after correction?

```text
ROUTE EXISTS != ROUTE USABLE IN TIME
CORRECTION != RESTORATION
```

## Stop rule

Do not keep expanding because more structure exists.

Stop opening the case when an additional distinction no longer changes:
- the warranted next action;
- a load-bearing evidence demand;
- an owner/authority handoff;
- a material affected scope/burden;
- a clock/lead-time condition;
- a correction/reversal route.

Then act proportionately.

## Type ceilings learned from the harness

- human volitional autonomy != agent operational autonomy;
- dependence != non-autonomy in human development;
- current AI operational continuity != intrinsic viability;
- artifact/successor continuity != continuous identity;
- collective capability != new individual;
- downward-accountability/external-recognition language is governance-typed, not universal relationship vocabulary;
- compensation/correction != restored life/place/opportunity;
- present capability != mandate/standing/sovereignty.
