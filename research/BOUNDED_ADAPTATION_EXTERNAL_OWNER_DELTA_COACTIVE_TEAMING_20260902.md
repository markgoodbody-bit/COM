## External-owner correction

### 1. The useful unit is interdependence, not maximum standalone autonomy

Coactive Design explicitly rejects treating increasingly capable machines only as teleoperated tools or independent automatons. It begins from **joint activity** and asks what each participant needs from the others because the work is interdependent.

That is directly relevant to the project's emerging-AI frontier.

```text
MORE_AGENT_AUTONOMY != BETTER_TEAM
INDEPENDENCE != THE_ONLY_GOAL
INTERDEPENDENCE -> DESIGN_REQUIREMENTS
```

This substantially precedes and owns part of what the project has been calling `answerable initiative`.

### 2. Observability, predictability and directability are mature operational requirements

Coactive Design's three core interdependence requirements are:

- **observability** — pertinent state, knowledge about team/task/environment and progress can be made visible enough for coordination;
- **predictability** — participants can anticipate one another's likely actions well enough to coordinate complementary action;
- **directability** — participants have practical means to influence/redirect one another's behaviour where the joint task requires it.

MITRE's applied human-machine teaming guide carries these ideas into systems-engineering practice alongside common ground, calibrated trust, adaptability, attention direction and exploration of solution space.

These are stronger external owners for much of the operational teaming layer than a new local vocabulary would be.

```text
OBSERVABLE != FULLY_TRANSPARENT
PREDICTABLE != DETERMINISTIC
DIRECTABLE != OWNED_OR_SUBORDINATE
```

### 3. Current human-autonomy-team research adds plannability

Recent 2026 work on adaptation in human-autonomy teams identifies four adaptation-enabling conditions:

- **predictability** — ability to anticipate each other's actions;
- **observability** — exchange/access to declarative information about team, task and environment;
- **plannability** — exchange/access to procedural and strategic knowledge about how/why the team will act;
- **directability** — ability to modify or redirect each other's actions, including fluid role/authority arrangements.

The important project consequence is not to adopt a new four-field form. It is to stop treating `answerable initiative` as a locally discovered primitive when mature neighbouring work already gives a substantial operational account of adaptive teaming.

### 4. This strengthens the distinction between human presence and human correction capacity

A human can technically remain "in the loop" while lacking enough observability to understand what is happening, predictability to anticipate the next action, plannability to understand or reshape the strategy, or directability to interrupt/redirect it in time.

Therefore:

```text
HUMAN_PRESENT != TEAM_CORRECTION_CAPACITY
BUTTON_EXISTS != DIRECTABILITY_USABLE_IN_TIME
```

This is an external-owner route into an existing project concern rather than evidence of local novelty.

### 5. Directability is reciprocal coordination, not automatic hierarchy

The coactive literature treats interdependence as involving requirements on both human and machine participants. Directability should therefore not be collapsed into "human can command machine" or "machine can command human".

For the project's voluntary/co-development frontier, the useful borrowing is:

```text
TEAM_MEMBER_CAN_REDIRECT != TEAM_MEMBER_OWNS_THE_OTHER
RECIPROCAL_INFLUENCE != EQUAL_AUTHORITY_FOR_EVERY_ACTION
```

Actual authority, consent, role, competence, standing and emergency delegation remain separately typed questions.

## What remains useful for Mechanical Ethics / TRACE

External-owner subtraction does **not** make the project remainder empty. It narrows it.

Coactive/HAT research strongly owns the operational coordination problem of interdependence and the conditions that make adaptation/teamwork possible. Mechanical Ethics / TRACE should therefore borrow those structures rather than rename them.

The project can still ask cross-domain questions that are not exhausted by O/P/P/D alone:

- **clocks and hardening:** is redirection reachable before consequential options close?
- **authority provenance:** who may redirect what, under which mandate, scope and expiry?
- **affected scopes:** who bears consequences without being part of the nominal team?
- **burden and residue:** what remains after a task is stopped or corrected?
- **correction independence:** can a challenged actor/team be corrected by a route not wholly controlled by itself?
- **voluntariness:** can a participant refuse, narrow, withdraw or renegotiate participation where materially possible?
- **developmental power:** can trainers/evaluators and institutions themselves be challenged and updated?
- **positive construction:** what new shared capability or future-space does the interdependence make possible?

Those should be treated as candidate integration/remainder questions, not claims that HAT research lacks them universally.

## Earned change to `answerable initiative`

Do not use `answerable initiative` as a claim of a new general theory of human-agent teaming.

Use it, if it survives, as a project-local compression that **imports** mature interdependence requirements and adds explicit project pressures around correction, authority and hardening.

Candidate translation:

```text
ANSWERABLE_INITIATIVE (project shorthand)
requires at least:
  OBSERVABILITY
  PREDICTABILITY
  PLANNABILITY
  DIRECTABILITY
plus context-dependent:
  AUTHORITY / ENTRUSTMENT BOUNDARIES
  CORRECTION ROUTES + CLOCKS
  AFFECTED-SCOPE VISIBILITY
  RESIDUE / REPAIR
  REFUSAL / WITHDRAWAL / CHALLENGE WHERE APPLICABLE
```

No requirement above is promoted to TRACE/ME canon by this note.

## Consequence for PR #87

The Bounded Adaptation Loop should test its own ecology as a human-agent team, not merely as a scheduler:

- can Mark/Codex/CC/Framework observe the consequential state without ingesting everything?
- can each aperture predict enough of the others' commitments and boundaries to coordinate safely?
- can current plans and reasons be made available without exposing private chain-of-thought?
- can an aperture redirect/challenge ordinary work without pretending that coordination authority is universal actuation authority?

This supplies a stronger external-owner basis for PR #87's disagreement, routing and anti-bottleneck tests.

## Disposition

`ROUTE_EXTERNAL_OWNER -> PASS WITH SUBTRACTION`.

Keep the frontier. Cut the implied novelty. Import coactive/interdependence structures explicitly and test the remaining correction/authority/hardening integration against real cases.
