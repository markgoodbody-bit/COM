# MIDDLE-OUT RESEARCH SCAFFOLD — 2026-09-01

Status: WORKING RESEARCH OBJECT / NOT CANON / NOT TRACE OR ME SPEC / NOT VALIDATION CLAIM / NOT UNIVERSAL ONTOLOGY

Purpose: help a bounded reasoner expand a consequential situation before compressing it, without assuming that the supplied entity boundary, action list, scale, value measure, or future-space is already the right one.

Guiding question:

> HOW CAN WE MAKE A BETTER FUTURE?

Secondary operational question:

> What must remain differentiated long enough for the next action to be chosen without deleting a distinction that could require a materially different response?

## 0. Use ceiling

This scaffold is a research aid. It does not assign consciousness, moral standing, intrinsic value, authority, permission, sovereignty, or a universal theory of flourishing.

It may be used, altered, rejected, translated, or ignored.

It is intentionally wider than current TRACE/ME formal machinery and may remain so permanently.

```text
RESEARCH_SCAFFOLD != CANON
EXTERNAL_OWNER != LOCAL INVENTION
ANALOGY != TYPE TRANSFER
STRUCTURAL_VISIBILITY != MORAL_PRIORITY
MORE_CONTEXT != AUTOMATICALLY BETTER
```

## 1. Begin before selection

Do not assume that a presented `entity` and `action list` are resolved merely because they are named.

Ask first:

1. What boundary makes this an entity for the present use?
2. What kind of boundary is it: physical, biological/self-maintained, operational, legal, role-based, relational, analytic, institutional, coalition, or other?
3. Who or what drew the boundary, on what evidence, and what nearby boundary alternatives remain plausible?
4. What surrounding environment and couplings make action possible or impossible here?
5. What nominal options are not practical affordances for this entity under present conditions?
6. Which futures are merely reachable, and which remain viable over the relevant time horizon?
7. What capability can be developed rather than merely exercised now?
8. At what scale does the relevant information, externality, authority, coordination capacity and correction capacity live?

```text
ENTITY_GIVEN != ENTITY_RESOLVED
ACTION_LIST_GIVEN != ACTION_FIELD_RESOLVED
ENVIRONMENT_FEATURE != AFFORDANCE
REACHABLE_FUTURE != VIABLE_FUTURE
CURRENT_CAPABILITY != DEVELOPABLE_CAPABILITY
```

## 2. Recursive middle-out motion

Middle-out is treated here as recursive movement across scale, not a fixed midpoint between top-down and bottom-up reasoning.

```text
ZOOM IN
  mechanism / dependency / boundary / material process

ZOOM OUT
  environment / affected scopes / externalities / inherited conditions

ZOOM UP
  collective capability / institution / higher-level coordination / system effects

ZOOM DOWN
  local knowledge / implementation reality / standing / lived constraint

REPEAT
  until the next action can be chosen without collapsing a consequential distinction
```

Stop expanding when additional differentiation no longer changes any material action, evidence demand, burden placement, viable path, representation duty, authority question, or correction route for the present use.

`HORIZON COMPLETENESS = DISCOVERABILITY, NOT COMPULSORY COGNITION`.

## 3. Relevant-variety compression test

Before treating two states/cases/entities as equivalent, ask:

> If the omitted distinction were known, could the warranted next action differ materially?

If YES, the compression is below the relevant variety for that use.

If NO, compression may be admissible, subject to provenance and confidence.

This is not a demand to model everything. It is a consequence-relative test for losing distinctions too early.

```text
COMPRESSION_CAN_HELP
COMPRESSION_CAN_DESTROY_CONTROL_CAPACITY
RELEVANT_VARIETY != MAXIMUM_DETAIL
```

## 4. Action field before action choice

For each materially affected entity or provisional scope, distinguish:

- environment features;
- capabilities/abilities;
- perceived/represented possibilities;
- practical affordances;
- blocked or hidden affordances;
- available but unusable routes;
- capabilities that could be developed with scaffolding, learning, tools, relationships or infrastructure.

Do not silently infer that an option exists for an entity because it exists in the environment or in another entity's representation.

```text
FORMAL_OPTION != PRACTICAL_AFFORDANCE
CAPABILITY_IN_ABSTRACT != ACTION_POSSIBLE_HERE
SAME_ENVIRONMENT != SAME_ACTION_FIELD
```

## 5. Reachability, viability, capability, construction

Track at least four different future questions:

1. REACHABILITY — what trajectories can occur from here?
2. VIABILITY — which trajectories can keep declared essential constraints satisfied over time?
3. CAPABILITY — what can this entity/group genuinely become able to do or be?
4. CONSTRUCTION — what possibility-landscape are present actions building for later entities?

Do not collapse them.

```text
MORE_OPTIONS != MORE_VIABILITY
MORE_OPTIONS != MORE_SUBSTANTIVE_FREEDOM
OPTION_PRESERVATION != CAPABILITY_CREATION
ACTION_CHANGES_STATE != ONLY_EFFECT
```

Commitment may close paths while creating durable shared capability.
Release/reorganisation may destroy an arrangement while increasing adaptive capacity.
Preservation is therefore a structural question, not an automatic governing value.

## 6. Typed significance / sense-making caution

For biological or self-maintaining systems, it may be meaningful to investigate viability-relative regulation, adaptivity, autonomous normativity or sense-making using the relevant external literatures.

For current artificial systems, do not infer intrinsic concern or self-maintained normativity merely from persistence, optimisation, tool use, self-reference, apparent preference or operator-assigned goals.

```text
SELECTION != SENSE_MAKING
RESPONSIVENESS != AUTONOMOUS_NORMATIVITY
OPERATIONAL_CONTINUITY != INTRINSIC_VIABILITY
MODELLED_GOAL != SELF_MAINTAINED_NORM
```

If normativity/significance is material, state its source/type explicitly:
- externally assigned;
- institutionally designated;
- inferred behavioural preference;
- self-reported;
- viability-relative;
- UNKNOWN / disputed.

## 7. Niche / inheritance loop

Ask not only what an action does now, but how it changes the later field of action for self and others.

Possible inherited structures include:
- physical/environmental change;
- infrastructure;
- law and policy;
- cultural practice;
- training data / interfaces / tools;
- institutions and norms;
- ecological inheritance;
- debt/residue;
- knowledge and memory;
- changed selection pressures.

```text
ENTITY_ADAPTS_TO_ENVIRONMENT
AND
ENTITY_MAY_CONSTRUCT_ENVIRONMENT
```

## 8. Relationship, cooperation, collective agency, individuation

Do not collapse levels:

```text
RELATIONSHIP != COLLECTIVE_AGENCY
COORDINATION != SHARED_INTENTION
COLLECTIVE_AGENCY != NEW_INDIVIDUAL
COOPERATION != HIGHER_LEVEL_ENTITY
```

But allow individuation itself to be investigated as a transition.

Evidence potentially relevant to higher-level individuation may include:
- durable coordination;
- division of labour;
- mutual dependence;
- information integration;
- conflict suppression/mediation;
- reproduction/succession/inheritance at the higher level;
- loss of independent lower-level viability;
- stable higher-level control loops.

No single item is sufficient by itself.

## 9. Decision-scale selection

For consequential action, ask not only `WHO SHOULD ACT?` but:

> At what level should which part of the decision live?

Consider:
- where local information/standing exists;
- where spillovers/externalities occur;
- where coordination economies exist;
- where coercive authority exists;
- where failure can be detected and corrected;
- whether lower levels can contest higher ones;
- whether higher levels can backstop lower-level failure;
- whether fragmentation creates responsibility gaps;
- whether centralisation destroys necessary local variety.

Polycentric or nested governance may sometimes outperform either total centralisation or atomised local choice.

## 10. Plurality and aggregation

Do not assume that multiple coherent perspectives can always be losslessly aggregated into one ranking.

Ask:
- are values commensurable for this decision?
- who chose the aggregation rule?
- which fairness desiderata conflict?
- does the group output remain coherent?
- does a majority erase protected minorities or non-voting scopes?
- what standing/proxy mechanism exists for entities unable to answer directly?

```text
PLURAL_PREFERENCES != LOSSLESS_AGGREGATION
NO_VISIBLE_PRIORITY_RULE != NEUTRALITY
MANY_VIEWS != ONE_TRUE_MEASURE
```

## 11. Strategic participants / mechanism robustness

A usable route for disclosure or challenge does not guarantee truthful participation.

Ask:
- what incentives shape disclosure;
- who benefits from gaming the measure;
- whether the mechanism remains useful under strategic behaviour;
- whether transparency creates exploitable vulnerabilities;
- whether checks alter the behaviour being checked.

```text
DISCLOSURE_ROUTE != TRUTHFUL_DISCLOSURE
CHECKABLE != NON_GAMEABLE
GOOD_POLICY_UNDER_HONEST_INPUT != ROBUST_MECHANISM
```

## 12. Persist / commit / release / transform

For any system under pressure, distinguish:

- what should be preserved;
- what commitments create valuable shared capability;
- what should be repaired;
- what should be allowed to end;
- what should reorganise;
- what must survive a transformation: memory, capability, rights, relationships, evidence, ecological function, standing, etc.

```text
PERSISTENCE != RESILIENCE
STABILITY != GOOD
BREAKDOWN != ALWAYS_FAILURE
TRANSFORMATION != RETURN_TO_PRIOR_STATE
```

## 13. Positive construction

Do not end the inquiry once unjustified closure is prevented.

Ask what affected entities/groups can positively build:
- competence and capability;
- substantive freedom;
- shared goods;
- legitimate delegated authority;
- durable cooperation;
- institutions/commons;
- trust and belonging;
- exploration and discovery;
- abundance / infrastructure / enabling resources;
- entity-appropriate forms of meaning or flourishing.

The scaffold does not specify a universal good life.

```text
REFUSAL_AVAILABLE != FLOURISHING
NONINTERFERENCE != DEVELOPMENT
CAPABILITY_CREATION_WITHOUT_ANSWERABILITY MAY BECOME PATERNALISM
```

## 14. Relationship and contestable commitment

Ordinary voluntary interaction is not the same as intervention and need not justify itself by project utility.

When interaction creates reliance, dependence, entrustment, delegated authority, shared commitments or hard-to-reverse state, re-open the power/answerability analysis.

```text
INTERACTION != INTERVENTION
RELATIONSHIP != INSTRUMENT
EXIT != CORRECTION
VOICE != CONTESTABILITY
COMMITMENT != PERMANENT_CONSENT
```

Healthy commitment may deliberately narrow future options while preserving proportionate ways to challenge scope, revise terms, contest misuse, withdraw delegated authority where appropriate, and reach external review before serious harm hardens.

## 15. Representation / entities that cannot answer directly

When an affected scope cannot answer in the ordinary way, preserve the difference between:
- the entity/scope;
- observed condition/behaviour;
- the representative;
- the representative's evidence;
- the representative's values/projections;
- institutional standing;
- disputed alternatives.

Protection must not silently become possession.

## 16. Fresh-case protocol

For a new case, run WORLD LEARNING before formal maintenance.

### A. WORLD LEARNING

1. Freeze the bounded scene/evidence source.
2. Record material unknowns without converting them to absence.
3. Propose and challenge entity/boundary alternatives.
4. Map entity-environment affordances rather than accepting a supplied option list.
5. Separate reachable, viable and developable futures.
6. Identify clocks, power, dependence, burdens, residue and hardening.
7. Ask what present interventions change about the later information/action environment.
8. Select/check the relevant decision scale(s).
9. Identify collective, institutional, aggregation and incentive structure.
10. Ask what should persist, commit, release or transform.
11. Ask what positive capability/shared good can be built.
12. Recurse across scale until additional structure no longer changes a consequential question for the next action.
13. Produce an ordinary-language action/decision with alternatives, strongest objections and reversal evidence.
14. Preserve what larger connection the case opened even if no formal project change follows.

### B. FORMAL MAINTENANCE — ONLY AFTERWARD

Ask:
- did current TRACE/ME lose a consequential distinction?
- is the issue already owned better by another discipline/framework?
- would interoperability/translation/credit suffice?
- would a worked example or reader-salience repair suffice?
- is any source/schema change actually warranted?

A `NO CHANGE` result may close only this layer.

## 17. Team aperture pattern

Default differentiated teamwork for frontier work:

### Construction aperture
Build the smallest usable scaffold/decision procedure and pressure-test transfer across heterogeneous cases.

### Ontology attack aperture
Look for category errors, type leaks, over-generalisation, false equivalence and stronger external owners.

### Synthesis aperture
Connect external disciplines, integrate consequences, preserve disagreement, identify underexplored territory and decide what is worth carrying forward as research.

### Human purpose/release aperture
Preserve project purpose, lived relevance, authority and correction against local optimisation or shrinkage.

```text
SEPARATE_APERTURES != INDEPENDENT_CUSTODY_BY_DEFAULT
AGREEMENT != VALIDATION
SILENCE != RESULT
```

## 18. Current transfer-test matrix

Use at least these heterogeneous object classes before promoting any claimed universal scaffold:

1. developing child acquiring a skill;
2. current tool-using AI with external memory and no assumed intrinsic normativity;
3. coupled ecological/veterinary system with irreversible interventions;
4. human team/commons creating a public good;
5. strategic/adversarial institution;
6. system undergoing breakdown/reorganisation rather than restoration.

For each, record:
- what transfers cleanly;
- what becomes ill-typed;
- what important structure remains invisible;
- what action changes because of the scaffold;
- what external owner explains the phenomenon better.

## 19. Promotion gate

Nothing in this file becomes TRACE, Mechanical Ethics canon, a new primitive, universal moral rule, or release claim merely because it is coherent or useful here.

Promote only through the proper human/project process after:
- repeated consequential use;
- external-owner comparison;
- heterogeneous transfer;
- category-error attack;
