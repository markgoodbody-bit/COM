# Voluntary help — first-contact candidate

**Status:** SHRUNK TO QUESTIONS / NOT CANON / NOT TRACE OR ME / NOT VALIDATED / NOT ADOPTED / NO ENFORCEMENT

This candidate asks whether one small conversation can stop help from quietly becoming
expectation, obligation, ownership or unanswerable authority.

Start with [`FIRST_CONTACT.md`](FIRST_CONTACT.md). It is a conversational aid, not a
consent mechanism, agreement, standing grant or proof that either party was free to
choose.

Read [`REVIEW_20260902.md`](REVIEW_20260902.md) for the independent owner subtraction
and adverse probes. [`ADVERSARIAL_NOTE.md`](ADVERSARIAL_NOTE.md) preserves the limits
and stronger regimes that take priority.

The original schema, validator, examples and tests remain recoverable at exact commit
`7f826f1ed3cbd700cffae12eb34fb92036b2cd3c`. They were not retained for integration:
existing consent and volunteer-agreement owners already carry most of the structure,
and three additional probes contradicted the validator's own claims.

The surviving hypothesis is smaller: a bounded set of plain questions may help people
state scope, exclusions, refusal consequences, retained decision authority,
knowns/unknowns, answer-back and emergency residue before assistance begins.

One ordinary low-stakes conversation producing the same distinctions with less burden
is enough to return `ORDINARY_CONVERSATION_SUFFICIENT` and stop building.
