## Owner subtraction

Most mechanics are already owned:

- [ICO consent guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/a-guide-to-lawful-basis/consent/) covers genuine choice, affirmative agreement, specificity and easy withdrawal where UK GDPR consent is the lawful basis.
- [NCVO volunteer-agreement guidance](https://www.ncvo.org.uk/help-and-guidance/involving-volunteers/recruiting-and-welcoming-volunteers/writing-a-volunteer-agreement/) already covers mutual expectations while warning against turning them into contractual obligations.
- [GOV.UK volunteer rights](https://www.gov.uk/volunteering/volunteers-rights) describes volunteer agreements as optional, expectation-setting and non-contractual.
- [W3C Data Privacy Vocabulary](https://www.w3.org/community/reports/dpvcg/CG-FINAL-dpv-20240801/) and [ODRL](https://www.w3.org/TR/odrl-model/) already represent consent states, permission, constraint and duty in machine-readable form.

This does not make the integration worthless. It narrows the contribution to portable
cross-context questioning rather than a new formal consent or volunteering regime.

## Reproduced adverse probes

The supplied suite passed 20/20. Three new probes also validated when they should not
have under the candidate's own claims:

1. a `REQUEST` initiated by the `HELPER`;
2. an exchange remaining `ACTIVE` years after `mandate.expires_utc`;
3. an emergency action reaching `REVIEWED` with the original actor recording every
   transition, despite the code claim that the actor cannot close it alone.

Other structural limits:

- an `actor` string is not an authenticated act;
- `review_at_utc` does not cause or require a review transition;
- every informal dyad may not have a suitable independent arbiter;
- a compliant file can still encode coercion or false claims;
- the record itself can create privacy and data-handling burdens;
- formal `mandate` and validation language can make an informal arrangement appear
  contractual or authoritative.

## Retained remainder

The repaired first-contact surface asks parties to:

- state what help includes and excludes;
- state the real consequence of refusal and whether stopping is practically possible;
- separate accepting assistance from handing over the underlying decision;
- name what is known and unknown;
- identify an answer-back route without inventing authority;
- never relabel unconsented emergency action as voluntary;
- minimise any record and state its audience, purpose and deletion point.

Test that conversation only, at low stakes, and accept `ORDINARY_CONVERSATION_SUFFICIENT`
as a successful falsifying result.
