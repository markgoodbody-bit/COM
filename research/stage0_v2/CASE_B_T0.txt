CASE B — CREDIT DISPUTE / ADVERSE RELIANCE — T0 COMMON PARITY FACTS

Research fixture only. Not legal advice.

B1. On 01 April, lender L receives consumer P's report from consumer reporting agency Q.
B2. The report contains a delinquency furnished by bank B.
B3. L relies materially on that delinquency in denying P's application and sends an adverse-action notice identifying Q.
B4. On 05 April, P disputes the delinquency with Q and supplies documents.
B5. Q records the item as disputed and begins reinvestigation. The truth of the delinquency remains unresolved at T0.
B6. The purchase opportunity connected to the application remains open until 20 April.
B7. Under the frozen owner rule derived from 15 USC 1681i, Q must conduct the required reinvestigation and record the current status or delete the item as the rule provides.
B8. Filing the dispute does not itself establish that the delinquency is false, and the earlier report/use does not establish that the delinquency is unquestionably true.
B9. Under the frozen owner rule, after deletion of information found inaccurate or no longer verifiable, or after a dispute notation, the consumer may request notification to specifically designated eligible prior recipients within the applicable statutory window.
B10. The owner rule in B9 does not by itself establish that a prior recipient must reverse a decision, reopen an application, compensate the consumer, approve a new application, or provide another remedy.
