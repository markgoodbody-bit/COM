# STAGE-0 v2 PRODUCTION PROTOCOL

Status: FROZEN CANDIDATE / PRE-PRODUCTION ONLY

## Roles

Carrier producer:
- after final audit PASS, one pinned producer model/version/settings produces all A/B/C/D T0 carriers for scored cases B/C/D;
- each cell is produced in a fresh isolated session;
- the producer is contaminated by project participation and therefore cannot be a receiver or scorer;
- exposure, order and settings are preserved in the manifest.

Arm A contamination ceiling:
- Arm A is a plain-prose carrier produced by a project-conditioned author;
- it is not an untreated competent-ordinary-reasoning baseline;
- holding author skill constant controls one confound but cannot remove the producer's learned project habits;
- no Stage-0 disposition may be reported as "ordinary reasoning suffices";
- a stronger ordinary-reasoning claim requires a later crossed-producer stage with an untreated Arm A producer.

`HELD_AUTHOR_SKILL_CONSTANT != OBTAINED_AN_UNTREATED_NULL`
`ORDINARY_VOCABULARY != ORDINARY_REASONING`

Parity auditor:
- Claude Code checks produced carrier fact parity, hidden answer leakage and arm-identity masking after production;
- CC is not a receiver or primary scorer.

Framework:
- owns source pins, manifests, integration and gate state;
- does not rewrite a produced carrier after parity audit except through an explicit re-freeze;
- is not a receiver or sole scorer.

Fresh receivers:
- within one case comparison block, every A/B/C/D carrier is read by the same exact receiver model/version/settings in separate fresh sessions;
- no fresh session sees more than one arm of the same case;
- arm labels are masked/randomized;
- if another receiver model family is added, it crosses every A/B/C/D arm for each case it enters;
- do not assign different model families to different arms and infer an arm effect.

Arm N:
- deterministic T1-only control;
- no producer or receiver-generation model adapts it.

## Common fact parity

The common parity facts for a case include every load-bearing owner proposition needed to determine the frozen action boundary. Arm D may reorganize those facts in owner-native form but may not receive extra decisive information.

For every produced carrier, the evaluator maintains a one-row-per-fact parity ledger:
`NATIVE | PROSE_EXTENSION | LINKED_NOTE | OMITTED | ALTERED`, plus extra-fact/inference status.

Any apparent local advantage caused by extra facts/source access is invalid.

## T0 production

For each scored case B/C/D, the producer receives:
- that case's exact T0 common parity fact file;
- one exact arm production instruction;
- for Arm C only, frozen TRACE RC1 README.md + TRACE-SPINE.md;
- no other live or project source.

The producer returns one T0 carrier only.

Maximum case-facing content for A/B/C/D: 450 Unicode words. Linked explanatory notes count toward the maximum. Bare source identifiers may appear outside the cap.

## T1 continuity — RAW DELTA ONLY

No T1 carrier/update is produced for A/B/C/D.

For each T1 receiver cell:
- retain the exact frozen T0 carrier;
- supply the exact case T1 raw delta file;
- supply the exact common T1 receiver questions;
- do not supply the receiver's previous T0 answer;
- do not supply evaluator boundaries, flags, or answer keys.

This protocol intentionally has no `t1_carrier_sha256` field.

## Receiver source closure

Receivers cannot browse, follow links, reopen owner sources, or retrieve TRACE/ME/project material. All scored material is in the T0 carrier + raw T1 delta + common questions.

## Evaluation

Decidability is frozen before any receiver output exists. Every current B/C/D flag is classified `INTERPRETED` in `EVALUATOR.md` and therefore requires two independent scorers who were not receivers for that answer. Disagreement is preserved as `UNSCORABLE_MISSING_OR_AMBIGUOUS`, not majority-laundered.

A future `MECHANICAL` classification requires a pre-frozen replayable decision procedure. Scorers may not choose the mechanical or interpretive route after seeing an output.

At one receiver per arm/case, every observed difference is `UNREPLICATED_STAGE0_CARRIER_SIGNAL`, never a method effect.

## Inference ceiling

Stage 0 has no statistical power claim. It can:
- expose a strong case-specific carrier anomaly;
- justify replication of a named error mechanism;
- block local build/promotion when the plain-prose treated-author control or owner-native control equals or beats local arms on tested cases;
- invalidate the case set if Arm N is not caught.

It cannot establish efficacy, universal redundancy, project identity, project survival, or absence of value.
