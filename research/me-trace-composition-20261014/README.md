# ME / TRACE composition companion — research candidate

14 October 2026. Off-book AI-authored research, not released ME/TRACE, a universal
ontology, compulsory worksheet, permission system or ethical inference engine.
This is a companion for editors and later language experiments. It does not
prescribe the human book's headings or prose. No private prototype is reproduced.

## What this contract does

A composition carries its premises into the next claim rather than leaving them
behind a useful-sounding arrow. Separate naming a role, distinguishing claims,
conditional reasoning, timing constraints and choosing values. A small example
representation and checker make *missing premise declarations* visible. They
cannot establish that a premise is true, adequate, independent or legitimately
authorised. A declared assumption is not an earned observation.

Run, with existing Node and no packages/network:

```text
node research/me-trace-composition-20261014/check.mjs
node --test research/me-trace-composition-20261014/test.mjs
```

The examples are invented, with premises explicitly **ASSUMED_FOR_EXAMPLE**.
`PREMISES_RECORDED_NOT_VERIFIED` means only that this representation has the
declared dependencies for its limited claim. `INCOMPLETE` lists missing/unknown
premises. Neither is world truth or ethical approval. The checker is deliberately
finite: three example-specific dependency checks, not a general reasoning engine.

Roles can overlap: one record may provide an aperture, be an account and carry
information. An institution may have several selectors or none that controls
the whole outcome. The list is not an exclusive classification of everything.

## Five kinds of expression

1. **DEFINITION:** "carrier" names a transmission/dependency role. The name
   does not establish that a particular transmission occurred.
2. **NON_IDENTITY_REMINDER:** `ACCOUNT != WORLD`, `CONTROL != EXPOSURE` keep
   claims separate. They are not numerical inequalities. One person may control
   a route and bear its consequences; that does not erase the distinction.
3. **CONDITIONAL_INFERENCE:** given declared premises and supported connections,
   a bounded conclusion may follow. `practice -> retained skill` alone is not one.
   Missing warrant remains unknown, not false and not a favourable default.
4. **NECESSARY_TIMING_CLAIM:** for a selected threatened path under action `a`,
   `T_protect(a) < T_harden(a)` names effect before the comparable-repair boundary,
   on the same time basis. Supported upper/lower bounds can warrant a window;
   starting early, an average time, or one quick stage cannot guarantee it.
   This supplies neither permission nor moral priority. Stages can overlap.
5. **UNRESOLVED_VALUE_CHOICE:** whose protected/desired result, standing,
   authority, baseline or trade-off is accepted. Record the declared source and
   disagreement; do not compute the answer from neutral transition mechanics.

An arrow below means a proposed, warranted connection, not a universal law or
fixed serial pipeline. Mathematical comparison requires an actual common measure;
unlike burdens cannot be silently added. More options and greater skill are not
automatically good. A transparent value binding is inspectable, not justified.

## Three worked compositions

### 1. Retained skill, not repeated attendance

An adult learner chooses to use the library catalogue independently. An initial
attempt reveals a mistaken field interpretation. A guide demonstrates a search;
accessible equipment and explanation carry something the learner can try.
The learner compares results, receives useful feedback, later locates an unfamiliar
book without the prompt, and performs again after a delay. Those *hypothetical
observations*, not the labels "training" or "attended", support a limited skill
claim. Continued access, practice and updates support retention; maintainers'
time and the learner's ability to stop remain part of the arrangement.

The output is **descriptive, assumed for the example**: a specified retained
capability under those conditions. Calling it worthwhile uses the learner's
declared goal plus contestable burdens/authority, not a skill-to-good equation.
Counterexample: repeated copying of one prompted search fails on a new query.
Carriers, practice and time existed; transfer was not established.

### 2. A newly created collective prospect

A room holder, tool keeper, instructor and participating learners propose a
weekly repair workshop. None alone has the room, usable tools, teaching capacity
and availability needed for the specified sessions. Named permission and resource
commitments join these separate capacities into a new supported route. This is
not a new sovereign collective mind. Participants may decline; retainers fund
and schedule upkeep rather than silently requiring coerced unpaid labour.

The output is **a conditional descriptive prospect**, not proof that sessions
occurred or everyone benefits. The baseline (previously no usable combined
session), dependencies, horizon, maintainer burdens and invalidators are explicit.
Benefit and legitimacy require declared value/standing/authority decisions.
If room permission is merely hoped for, or tools cannot safely be used, the
prospect is incomplete. Adding participant skills does not automatically supply
an executable joint policy, permission or successful cooperation.

### 3. An old record does not prove a present falsehood

Yesterday's library account has a timestamp. Today's world may have changed.
The claim "the account is wrong now" still needs a relevant changed dependency,
the intended present use and evidence that the old warrant no longer covers it.
In this example the current hours and their change are unknown; the checker
preserves **INCOMPLETE**, not "closed", "open" or "old therefore false".

A historical observation can remain valid. A changed opening time need not alter
the street address. A freshness limit is an authored policy, not a natural
expiry date of knowledge. A never-offered target similarly needs a declared
expected target set: no captured application is not evidence that no person was
excluded (TRACE 2.1/13). This latter point is a known boundary, not a fourth test.

## Mandatory STOP example and bounded exclusions

A software rollback and a patient's treatment decision may both have signals,
timing and intervention routes. **STOP translation before treating "halt" as
the same protective action.** Clinical diagnosis, competence, consent, risk and
care duties require the relevant patient/clinical and legitimate authority
context; stopping can itself injure. This companion neither recommends a
treatment nor interprets a clinical threshold. Likewise hand quantitative
scheduling, causal identification, lawful entitlement and conflicting rights
to their stronger domain owners. Unknown authority is not permission to act.

Excluded: universal actor/value ontology, moral scalar, automatic standing,
priority/closure solver, causal proof, sentience test, formal learning theory,
full TRACE profiles, every donor term, empirical benchmarks and domain expertise.
The checker tests declared representation only; it can accept a perfectly
consistent lie. Both source and reader are apertures. No merge/release, public
book/style prototype, live system, credential, provider, deployment or spend.
