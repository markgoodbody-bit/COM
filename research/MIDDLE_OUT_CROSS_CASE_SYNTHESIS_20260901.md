## A. What transferred across all six?

Only the following structures survived without requiring one domain's ontology to be smuggled into another.

### 1. Current object/boundary must remain provisional and use-relative

Across cases it mattered to distinguish:
- child vs child-parent dyad;
- runtime vs model/tool/environment;
- cattle/herd/farm/wildlife/pathogen/programme;
- co-owners vs steward body;
- firm vs internal classifiers/employees/regulator;
- current settlement vs community/host/downstream system.

Portable form:

> What exactly is the current object/relationship/system for this decision, and what nearby boundary alternative could change the action?

Do not promote `entity boundary` into metaphysical individuation by default.

### 2. Formal options are not practical action possibilities

Every case required environment/capability conditions:
- bicycle/support/surface;
- agent tools/permissions;
- vaccine/surveillance/delivery capacity;
- dues/time/monitoring arrangements;
- safe reporting/audit capability;
- housing/finance/utilities for retreat.

Portable form:

`FORMAL OPTION != PRACTICAL ACTION FIELD`

The word `affordance` is useful in some domains but should not be compulsory vocabulary.

### 3. Capability is distinct from authority/entrustment and from current action

Capability repeatedly mattered, but no monotonic moral rule survived.

- child capability growth usually supports fading teaching support;
- AI capability growth can justify more initiative **or** tighter containment;
- institutional capability enables collective maintenance;
- transition capability can make future paths genuinely reachable.

Portable form:

`CAPABILITY CHANGE -> REASSESS ACTION / SUPPORT / ENTRUSTMENT / CONTROL`

Direction is domain/risk/evidence dependent.

### 4. Constraints and commitments can create capability

This survived strongly:
- explicit no-surprise support can enable child exploration;
- sandbox/tool boundaries can enable useful AI initiative;
- dues/reserve/stewardship can create a maintainable commons;
- reporting/audit/protection capacity can enable regulatory correction;
- vaccination/surveillance investment enables policy transition;
- relocation infrastructure enables retreat before hazard lock-in.

Portable form:

`CONSTRAINT / COMMITMENT CAN ENABLE FUTURE ACTION`

This rejects `MORE OPEN OPTIONS = BETTER` without creating a universal pro-commitment rule.

### 5. Action changes the future action field

Across all six, intervention was not an exogenous one-shot input.

Action can create:
- skill;
- trust/reliance expectations;
- tool/permitted-action ecology;
- institutional rules/reserves;
- strategic gaming incentives;
- disease evidence/participation changes;
- development/relocation lock-in.

Portable question:

> What future capabilities, dependencies, incentives, evidence conditions or lock-ins does this action construct?

### 6. Power/authority is functional and relational, not one scalar

No single authority formula transferred intact.

What survived:
- who can sense/classify/interpret;
- who can authorize;
- who can actuate;
- who can monitor/assure;
- who can revoke/recover;
- what basis/mandate/responsibility supports each function;
- can affected parties answer back directly or through appropriate representation/evidence?

The governance-specific distinction `de facto power / downward accountability / external recognition` was useful in institutions but failed as universal child-development language.

Portable form:

> Who can make which consequential part happen, on what basis, and who/what can correct or contest that use of power?

### 7. Formal answer-back/correction routes are insufficient without usable capacity before the clock closes

This survived with different substrates:
- child must be able to say stop/not yet without pressure making it meaningless;
- human approver must retain attention/correction capacity rather than click through prompts;
- animals require proxy/evidence representation;
- low-income/time-poor commons members need practical participation routes;
- whistleblowers need safe pre-hardening reporting/protection;
- coastal residents need transition capability before relocation becomes emergency displacement.

Portable form:

`ROUTE EXISTS != ROUTE USABLE IN TIME`

### 8. Time/lead time/currentness are action variables

All cases changed when capability, evidence, risk or relationship changed.

Particularly strong:

`FAILURE THRESHOLD != LATEST SAFE DECISION TIME`

but this is only consequential where long lead times exist.

Portable question:

> What changes with time, and what must be built/rechecked before waiting makes a different action unavoidable?

### 9. Domain owners remain indispensable

The generic scaffold did not replace:
- developmental psychology/motor learning;
- agent security/mixed initiative;
- veterinary epidemiology/wildlife ecology;
- commons/institutional design;
- regulatory/mechanism/whistleblower expertise;
- coastal adaptation/engineering/relocation justice.

Portable rule:

`STRUCTURAL INTEGRATION != DOMAIN EFFECT ESTIMATION / AUTHORITY`

### 10. Concrete action + reversal evidence is essential

Every useful case ended with:
- a current action/posture;
- explicit uncertainty;
- conditions that would tighten, relax, reverse or transform it.

A map without an action was consistently insufficient.

## B. What is domain-typed and should NOT be default portable vocabulary?

### Demote from default entry

- **VIABILITY** — useful for explicit biological/control/institutional constraints; overkill or misleading in simple learning/AI cases.
- **SENSE-MAKING / AUTONOMOUS NORMATIVITY** — biological/enactive owner territory; not established for current AI.
- **AUTONOMY** — human volitional autonomy and agent operational autonomy are different objects.
- **FLOURISHING** — important human/domain question, too value-loaded as generic next-action field.
- **DOWNWARD ACCOUNTABILITY / EXTERNAL RECOGNITION** — useful governance terms, not universal asymmetric-relationship vocabulary.
- **PARTICIPATION BURDEN** — useful in institutions; developmental/AI cases need thinner `usable answer-back under asymmetry` language.
- **COLLECTIVE AGENCY / INDIVIDUATION** — ask only where collective-boundary status actually changes the decision.
- **EXIT** — often not equivalent to correction in home/community/relationship cases.
- **PERSIST / COMMIT / RELEASE / TRANSFORM** as a mandatory four-way classification — useful when systems are under transition, unnecessary in many ordinary cases.

Keep these in the research horizon with explicit type checks, not in the default entry form.

## C. What was missing or under-specified?

Only two additions/revisions recurred strongly enough to justify a research-level repair.

### 1. Control/entrustment recurrence through changing capability

The scaffold had decision scale and authority, but not a sufficiently clear temporal question:

> As capability, risk, dependence, evidence or environment changes, should the allocation of initiative/support/authority also change?

Important ceiling:

`CAPABILITY CHANGE != AUTOMATIC AUTHORITY EXPANSION`

This is not one moral rule. It is a recurrence trigger.

### 2. Transition capability before latest safe decision time

The scaffold distinguished developable capability, but cross-case pressure shows a stronger operational question:

> Is the future path actually constructible before the time at which choosing/building it becomes too late?

Examples:
- vaccination field force/cattle vaccine;
- roof reserve;
- regulatory audit/protection capacity;
- inland housing/utilities;
- skill/scaffolding time.

This is already substantially externally owned by transition/adaptation/resilience literatures.

## D. What should be deleted or demoted from the research scaffold?

The six-case run shows that the full 14-item return contract is too heavy for default use.

Demote the following from mandatory steps to optional modules triggered by case need:
- viability type check;
- individuation evidence;
- social-choice/aggregation analysis;
- strategic-mechanism analysis;
- persist/commit/release/transform taxonomy;
- explicit flourishing inventory;
- proxy-standing analysis where no non-answering scope is material.

Keep discoverability, not compulsory cognition.

`HORIZON COMPLETENESS != DEFAULT FORM LENGTH`

## E. What should stay research-only?

Most frontier terms:
- higher-level individuation;
- autonomous normativity/sense-making;
- general viability language;
- universal collective capability ontology;
- theories of flourishing;
- universal authority topology beyond the thin functional questions;
- metaphysical identity through transformation;
- AI moral standing/personhood/sovereignty.

These remain legitimate research questions but should not load the ordinary entry surface.

## F. What, if anything, deserves a later formal-maintenance question?

No TRACE/ME source change is earned by this run.

What **is** earned is a new research compression candidate: a much smaller middle-out entry form built only from the structures that survived all six cases.

The candidate should ask about:
1. current object/change;
2. practical action field and developable capability;
3. affected scopes + power/knowledge/mandate/answer-back;
4. time/lead-time/lock-in;
5. external owners/evidence/unknowns;
6. current action/commitment and future capability construction;
7. correction/reversal conditions.

That candidate should be tested as a usable first-contact surface before any formal integration.

## Cross-case verdict

The broad research scaffold was useful as a **quarry / breakage instrument**, not as the likely user-facing object.

Strong external-owner correction was the dominant outcome.

Case-level action advantage over competent domain reasoning was:
- child: integration/type correction, not owner replacement;
- current AI: strong type falsification, little local deployment novelty;
- bovine TB: no decision delta;
- commons: mostly owner-owned, modest cross-domain integration;
- adversarial regulation: owner-owned, strong cross-domain transfer;
- coastal transformation: owner-owned, strong cross-domain transfer.

Therefore:

`SCAFFOLD AS RESEARCH QUARRY: USEFUL`
`SCAFFOLD AS 14-FIELD DEFAULT METHOD: TOO HEAVY`
`EXTERNAL OWNER ROUTING: CENTRAL`
`SMALLER PORTABLE ENTRY: EARNED AS RESEARCH CANDIDATE`
`TRACE/ME MUTATION: NOT EARNED`

The correct next build is compression by deletion, not another frontier layer.
