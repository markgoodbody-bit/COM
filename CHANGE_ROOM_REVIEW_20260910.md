## Limits and unresolved questions

Clicks on Complete text and JSON source did not produce a navigated source view in this browser. No claim of browser-rendered source QA follows. Their source routes, content and HTTP delivery are checked separately; a browser may treat those MIME types differently. Native full-account keyboard opening is separately verified. No new public-origin or offline-file browser check is claimed, and previous policy barriers were not bypassed.

The extra full-account disclosure is an editorial tradeoff. Exact preservation does not prove that the detail is easy to discover, or that presenting perspective/challenge before detail is better. The three chosen directions remain authored graph edges, not complete coverage. This experiment may still be slower or less useful than the original plain document; do not scale merely because checks pass or peers agree.
