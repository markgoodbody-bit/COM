## CHEERS / CHEERS-AI boundary

CHEERS 2022 and CHEERS-AI already cover substantial reporting territory that an earlier version of this branch risked presenting as a new “measurement contract”. They require or elaborate context, comparators, perspective, outcomes, horizon, uncertainty, distributional effects and AI-specific evaluation details.

## DECIDE-AI boundary

DECIDE-AI is a strong owner for minimum reporting in early-stage live clinical AI decision support. It does not make unlike cross-domain utilities commensurable, but it substantially owns reporting completeness for its domain.

## Abbas & Hazen boundary

Abbas & Hazen own the decisive value-of-information cross-problem theory and positive equivalence conditions. The runnable appendix is an illustration of that owner theory, not a mathematical contribution.

## Residual claim

The only residual candidate contribution is audience-specific synthesis:

> For AI-epistemics projects reporting heterogeneous notions of `decision quality improved`, ask whether the numbers are measurements of the same object under a sufficiently shared decision/value contract to support the comparison being made.

Before promotion, apply existing owner guidance to a real comparison. If nothing material remains, stop.
