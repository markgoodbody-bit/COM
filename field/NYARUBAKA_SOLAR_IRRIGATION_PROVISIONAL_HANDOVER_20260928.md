## What the owner reports

ARDE/KUBAHO reports that a newly constructed solar-powered irrigation system in Nyarubaka Sector, Kamonyi District was provisionally received from contractor BTMC on 18 September 2026.

The reported physical chain is:

~~~text
RIVER
-> INTAKE
-> CENTRAL SUMP
-> SOLAR-POWERED MOTOR PUMP
-> UPHILL HOLDING DAM
-> GRAVITY-FED DISTRIBUTION
-> HILLSIDE FIELDS
~~~

The organisation says the system is designed to support irrigation across 10 hectares of marshland and create conditions for year-round agricultural production.

The handover ceremony included:
- Kamonyi District representatives;
- Nyarubaka Sector representatives;
- members of the beneficiary cooperative committee;
- the cooperative's Water User Committee.

The wider project also includes progressive terraces and tree planting, and is financed by the Rwanda Green Fund.

These are **owner-reported project facts**. This field note does not independently certify engineering performance, procurement quality, hydrological adequacy or operational reliability.
