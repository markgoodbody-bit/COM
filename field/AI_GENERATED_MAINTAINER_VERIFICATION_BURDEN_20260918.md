> HOW CAN WE MAKE A BETTER FUTURE?

## Current world pressure

AI assistance and autonomous agents have reduced the cost of generating bug reports, security findings, issues, comments and code contributions.

Verification/triage cost has not fallen at the same rate.

Current open-source owner evidence includes:
- reports of maintainers receiving large bursts of AI-generated security reports, many weak or unactionable;
- curl's explicit contribution/disclosure guidance warning that fabricated or unverified AI reports consume scarce maintainer time;
- Open Home Foundation's July 2026 policy: AI assistance allowed, autonomous contributions disallowed, contributor must understand/explain the work;
- PostHog: submitter owns, tests and explains the contribution; unreviewed AI issues/security disclosures may be closed;
- Electron: unreviewed AI-generated content that wastes scarce maintainer time is unacceptable;
- OSSF vulnerability-disclosure work explicitly treating AI-slop report volume and maintainer burden as an ecosystem problem.

```text

-> REPRODUCE
-> UNDERSTAND
-> CHECK
-> EXPLAIN
-> RESPOND TO REVIEW
AND
RECEIVER MAY LIMIT / CLOSE AUTOMATED OR LOW-EFFORT INTAKE
```

## Relation to Mechanical Ethics

This is a concrete burden-placement case.

If one side can create ten plausible claims at negligible marginal cost while the other side must spend hours falsifying each one, an open correction/review route can itself become a burden-transfer mechanism.

```text
ROUTE OPEN != ROUTE SUSTAINABLE
CHEAP ASSERTION != CHEAP VERIFICATION
ANSWER-BACK CHANNEL != UNBOUNDED INTAKE OBLIGATION
```

Strong owner practice already handles the operational policy details.

## THR pressure test

Current The Human Record contribution surfaces were reread against this owner pressure.

THR already says:
- a small correction with a checkable source is more useful than many generated summaries;
- contributors should say what they actually examined;
- checked / not-checked / unknown states remain separate;
- a valid packet is not a valid claim;
- ordinary prose is allowed; a packet is optional;
- receipt is not acceptance or a promise of review;
- review capacity is limited;
- avoid duplicate submissions, bulk AI-generated reports and unsolicited automated posting;
- volume earns no priority or authority.

Therefore:

```text
THR RECREATES AI-SLOP BURDEN BY DESIGN = NOT OBSERVED
CURRENT CONTRIBUTION BOUNDARY = SUFFICIENT FOR THIS PRESSURE
NEW CONTRIBUTION POLICY = NOT EARNED
NEW CHECKER / RATE LIMIT = NOT EARNED
```

Keeping `research_lead` / `reported_not_checked` valid is intentional: a useful lead may be accepted as a lead without pretending the original contributor performed the later verification. The receiver remains free not to review it.

## Claim ceiling

Do not infer:
- that AI-assisted contributions are generally low quality;
- that human-written reports are reliable by default;
- that disclosure proves understanding;
- that project policies eliminate maintainer burden;
- that THR has enough volume to demonstrate its boundary works at scale.

```text
OWNER POLICY EXISTS != BURDEN SOLVED
THR BOUNDARY LOOKS SOUND != SCALE TESTED
AI ASSISTED != AI AUTONOMOUS
```
