## Bounded factual sequence

The review concerns two Horizon Shortfall Scheme claimants whose legal representatives raised:
- substantive delay in resolving the claims; and
- lack of clarity/information about claim progress.

The Independent Senior Lawyer accepted that the remaining claim cohort is increasingly complex and often involves vulnerable claimants, and accepted the systemic approach of identifying/resolving common issues before completing individual cases.

For claimant 1, a proposed nil offer was held while policy work determined how such offers should be reviewed under newer guidance. The reviewer treated that substantive review route as appropriate.

Separately, the reviewer found that the Post Office had failed to keep the two claimants appropriately informed about progress. In claimant 1's case, the review records that this left the claimant feeling ignored and distressed.

The review distinguishes the two questions:
1. whether the substantive delay/review process is justified; and
2. whether claimants are being kept informed about what is happening, why, and what comes next.

The review records remedial communication measures including:
- a specific customer-support function;
- a named single point of contact across schemes;
- dedicated relationship managers for relevant claimants;
- restorative-justice-led training;
- engagement through postmaster groups/unions.

It also asks the Post Office to report monthly on progress resolving remaining scheme claims and says continuing systemic delay or communication failure can be referred back to the Independent Senior Lawyer.

## Structural reading

```text
SUBSTANTIVE REVIEW JUSTIFIED
!= COMMUNICATION ADEQUATE

DELAY EXPLAINABLE
!= SILENCE / VAGUENESS HARMLESS

KNOWN DELAY + UNKNOWN CAUSE / NEXT STEP / CLOCK
-> DISTRESS CAN ACCUMULATE

COMMUNICATION REPAIR
!= CLAIM RESOLVED

NAMED CONTACT + NEXT-STEP VISIBILITY
= ROUTE SUPPORT
NOT SUBSTANTIVE ADJUDICATION
```

The useful distinction is not "all delay is wrong."

It is that **route legibility is independently consequential**. A route can be substantively legitimate while its communication layer is harmful or unusable.
