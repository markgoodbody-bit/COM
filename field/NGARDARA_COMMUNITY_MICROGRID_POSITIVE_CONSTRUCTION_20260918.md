## Bounded observed/projected structure

Current public project material says the Ngardara project in Borroloola aims to construct a utility-scale solar+battery microgrid owned through the First Nations Ngardara Co-operative / project vehicle and connected to the existing remote diesel network.

ARENA records:
- project start 23 March 2026;
- at least 2.1 MW solar PV;
- 1.8 MW / 6.6 MWh battery;
- existing electricity supply heavily dependent on diesel;
- intended household bill relief through prepaid-meter credits;
- intended training, capability building and employment;
- intended community participation in development and ongoing operations/management;
- intended emissions, resilience and energy-security benefits.

Original Power describes Ngardara Co-operative as majority owner and frames the project around lower energy costs, reduced diesel dependence, jobs and community control.

Important status ceiling:

```text
FUNDED / UNDER DEVELOPMENT != COMPLETED
INTENDED BENEFIT != REALISED BENEFIT
PROJECT OWNER CLAIM != INDEPENDENT OUTCOME EVALUATION
```

Do not convert project aims into observed outcomes before they occur.
