## Established owner facts

The independent review found persistent systemic problems in earnings-related Carer's Allowance overpayments.

Material findings include:
- from 2019–2024, one in five claimants combining caring with paid work had an earnings-related overpayment detected;
- DWP examined only approximately half of HMRC earnings alerts in that period, so alerts could remain unprocessed while overpayments accumulated;
- flawed/unclear operational guidance around irregularly fluctuating earnings contributed to overpayments;
- the review concluded DWP had failed to demonstrate the focus needed to resolve persistent injustices;
- carers described unexpected large repayment demands and restrictions on opportunity to combine caring and paid work.

The government accepted or partially accepted 38 of 40 recommendations.

## Reassessment exercise

The reassessment exercise began **13 April 2026**.

Official current programme scale:

```text
cases expected to be reviewed: >200,000
cases potentially reduced / cancelled / refunded: ~25,000
funding: £75m across 2026/27–2028/29
```

In April 2026 the Minister told Parliament that the department hoped to complete the exercise in two years, with three years allowed/funded to ensure it could be completed properly.

The exercise covers potentially affected overpayments between **10 April 2015 and 2 September 2025**.

For most cases DWP says it already has the information needed and will contact people if more information is required.
