## Why this case

Spent nuclear fuel disposal creates obligations over timescales longer than any present organisation, workforce, software stack, media format or human lifetime.

Finland's ONKALO / Posiva programme is a current material implementation case, not a thought experiment.

## Current status

Posiva's encapsulation/final-disposal facility at Olkiluoto is intended for spent fuel from the Olkiluoto and Loviisa nuclear plants.

Current public status in 2026:
- STUK published a positive safety assessment in August 2026 as a major input to the Government's operating-licence decision;
- full-scale non-fuel installation/testing continues;
- Posiva aims for operational readiness at the end of 2026, but final disposal cannot begin until licensing, commissioning, final tests and regulator approvals are complete.

## Physical and institutional chain

Posiva's public model combines:
- deep geological containment;
- engineered and natural barriers;
- staged construction over the operating life;
- safety-case evidence;
- repeated regulator review;
- organisational capability/safety culture;
- dedicated nuclear-waste funding;
- state assumption of follow-up responsibility after final closure.

Posiva explicitly frames current final disposal as the present generation taking responsibility for its own spent fuel rather than simply passing the decision forward.

## Knowledge/record succession owner

IAEA safety guidance already treats organisational/knowledge succession as part of radioactive-waste safety.

It says, in substance:
- when responsibility transfers to another organisation, relevant records/information should transfer too;
- the receiving organisation must be able to read the information;
- future generations may need contextual material explaining legal/regulatory context, decision rationale, language/terminology, scientific understanding and measurement methods;
- media/equipment/systems needed to preserve availability must be considered;
- reasons for long-term safety decisions should remain traceable for present and future generations.
