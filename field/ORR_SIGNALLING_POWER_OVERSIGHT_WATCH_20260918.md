# ORR signalling-power oversight gap — implementation watch — 18 September 2026

Status: **REAL FIELD WITNESS / OWNER IDENTIFIED / IMPLEMENTATION WATCH / NO NEW PROJECT FRAMEWORK**

## Owner finding

Office of Rail and Road annual assessment 2025–26 identifies a growing risk from signalling supply power failures.

Recent incidents at Stoke, Stone and Kidsgrove led ORR to identify a systemic oversight gap between Network Rail's electrical & power and signalling disciplines, particularly around resilient back-up power.

ORR states that this gap does not appear to be fully addressed through Network Rail's internal incident reviews, increasing recurrence risk.

ORR asked Network Rail's Technical Authority to provide assurance that all regions have effective arrangements in place, with assurance expected by September 2026.
