## What the owner decision says

The complainant said:
- a Stage 3 statutory children’s complaints review panel had recommended correction of inaccurate information held about him;
- the Council had not carried out that recommendation;
- he believed the inaccurate information damaged his reputation and relationship with his children.

The Ombudsman records that, during its enquiries, the Council said it had reviewed the accuracy of the information and identified corrective action where required. Because the Council had then considered the issue and taken steps to address inaccuracies it identified, the Ombudsman concluded that further investigation of that aspect was unlikely to achieve a worthwhile outcome.

Separately, the Ombudsman upheld fault for a very long delay in the statutory complaints procedure and the Council agreed an apology and £500 symbolic payment for distress caused by that delay.

## What is observed

This is evidence that an owner-native correction recommendation can fail to propagate promptly into implementation.
