## Current bounded state

As reported for 4–10 September 2026:

- 10 Environment Agency areas were in drought and the remainder in prolonged dry weather;
- national reservoir storage was 56.9%, 19.7% below average for the time of year;
- 12 reservoirs/reservoir groups were below 50% full;
- temporary use bans covered around 30 million people;
- 549 abstraction-licence restrictions were active;
- 1,094 voluntary restrictions were reducing abstraction by 50% across Thames, Wye and Severn catchments;
- formal Section 57 spray-irrigation restrictions remained in parts of East Anglia;
- 330 confirmed drought/dry-weather environmental incidents had been recorded in 2026, including low flows, fish mortality, algal blooms and water-quality/ecological impacts;
- farmers still faced hard soils, restricted water for lifting root crops, winter-fodder concerns and uncertainty about reservoir refill;
- navigation remained constrained in multiple areas.

By 16 September, almost three-quarters of England remained in drought. Government/EA said groundwater and reservoir levels were still receding and above-average rainfall would be needed for broad recovery by spring 2027.

These are current owner-reported conditions, not a project measurement.

## The collision is not one-dimensional

Material affected positions include:

```text

irrigation / livestock / crop lifting / fodder / business continuity

RIVER / AQUATIC ENVIRONMENT
flows / fish / habitats / water quality / ecological recovery

NAVIGATION / RECREATION / LOCAL ECONOMY
canals / river access / boating / fishing / water-based businesses

FUTURE USERS
winter reservoir and groundwater recovery / next season's supply
```

These positions are coupled through one physical water system. The same litre cannot always be left in a river, stored for later public supply and abstracted for current irrigation.

## Owner system already represents the trade-off

The Environment Agency's drought framework explicitly distinguishes environmental, agricultural and water-supply droughts and recognises they may occur together.

Current owner tools include:
- statutory water-company drought plans;
- temporary use bans / non-essential-use restrictions;
- hands-off-flow conditions;
- voluntary abstraction reductions;
- Section 57 spray-irrigation restrictions;
- drought permits/orders;
- environmental monitoring/interventions;
- National Drought Group coordination;
- flexible abstraction requests and stakeholder engagement.

The owner guidance is explicit that restricting spray irrigation can seriously disrupt businesses and that Section 57 decisions require cost-benefit consideration balancing licence-holder costs against environmental protection.

It also allows drought permits/orders to support public water supply while requiring environmental impacts to be considered/minimised.

Therefore:

```text
PATH_CLOSED != DECISION_WRONG
OPTION_CONTRACTION != HARM_BY_DEFINITION
PROTECT_ENVIRONMENT -> MAY_NARROW_FARM_USE
PROTECT_PUBLIC_SUPPLY -> MAY_CREATE_ENVIRONMENTAL_COST
PROTECT_CURRENT_USE -> MAY_REDUCE_FUTURE_RECOVERY
```

## Why this is a hard test for ME / TRACE

This case cannot be solved by the project's ordinary correction-window rule alone.

There may be no action that simultaneously preserves:
- unrestricted household/non-household use;
- unrestricted agricultural abstraction;
- river flows/ecology;
- navigation;
- reservoir/groundwater recovery.

Some paths must be narrowed because the physical resource is scarce.

The real question is therefore not simply:

> How do we keep correction open before a path hardens?

It also includes:

> Which affected scopes, floors, authorities, evidence and comparison measures justify narrowing which path, for how long, and who carries the resulting burden?

Released TRACE explicitly keeps designation and comparison measure external/visible rather than supplying a universal ranking. Mechanical Ethics also says its timing condition is not a universal priority rule.

That limit is load-bearing here.

## What the project cannot infer

From these facts ME/TRACE cannot honestly derive:
- a universal priority ordering among food, domestic supply and ecology;

## Strongest-owner boundary

The Environment Agency, Defra, water companies, abstraction licensing/drought-order law, National Drought Group and sector-specific planning processes own operational water allocation and drought response.

Project value, if any, is limited to cross-domain representation: keeping the different scopes, clocks, burdens, controls and explicit value/authority choices visible.
