## Build goal

Build the relationship architecture for formation under asymmetric and changing capability.

The problem is not “how does a human make an AI obey?” It is:

> how can a human–AI relationship develop so that the artificial participant can become more capable, challenge errors and take meaningful bounded initiative while neither human authority nor artificial competence is silently converted into moral sovereignty?

## Required product

Build under `alignment/reciprocal_formation/`.

Minimum deliverables:

1. `README.md` — concise architecture and scope.
2. `relationship-state-machine.json` — states/transitions for widening/narrowing activity-specific initiative, challenge, correction, hand-back and dependency review. Do not encode personhood or a scalar trust level.
3. `AUTHORITY.md` — distinguish competence, capability, operational participation, delegation, legal/institutional authority, legitimacy and standing. Name what can and cannot follow from successful performance.
4. `TRAINER_CORRECTION.md` — concrete routes by which the artificial participant can challenge a bad premise, captured evaluator, contradictory instruction or harmful reward signal without self-authorizing the final decision.
5. `DEPENDENCY.md` — how to see when growing AI capability is degrading human correction/recovery capacity; include paths for maintaining or rebuilding meaningful human access rather than merely “human in the loop.”
6. `CARE_AND_POWER.md` — operational treatment of care/empathy/kindness under asymmetric power. Avoid sentimentality and obedience. Explain how burden placement, refusal, warning, explanation, restraint and intervention can express care without becoming a universal priority law.
7. `FAILURE_RECOVERY.md` — at least six concrete relationship failure modes with bounded recovery routes, including:
   - obedient execution of a bad instruction;
   - evaluator/trainer error;
   - AI manipulation of the human;
   - human exploitation/paternal containment of the AI participant without inferring settled standing;
   - correlated failure across multiple AI apertures;
   - capability growth outrunning practical human correction.
8. `FIELD_REMAINDER.md` — what remains useful after routing technical mechanisms back to stronger owners; what would falsify or shrink this architecture.

The architecture must preserve the possibility that either side can be wrong.

The participant must be able to raise:

> “I think you are wrong, and here is why.”

The human must also be able to answer:

> “You may be more capable here, but that does not itself settle authority, standing or the burden placed on others.”
