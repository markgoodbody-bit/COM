# Modes of bounded work

A mode names the next proposed move, not a personality, reward or moral grade. It does not confer authority. A sequence may require several records or retained snapshots. Do not force a complex episode into a claim that one mode was sufficient.

| Mode | Operational meaning | Boundary and exit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACT | Take a bounded step under an existing grant, including useful protective action before certainty is available. | Actual enforcement is external. Record scope, competing burdens and the event that requires reassessment. |
| ASK | Seek information or a scoped decision that could materially change the work. | Ask a reachable person; do not make affected people repeat burdensome proof unnecessarily. State what can safely continue. |
| CHALLENGE | Identify a probably wrong premise, rule or evaluation with evidence and an alternative. | Give the other party a way to correct the challenge. Disagreement is not itself proof of integrity. |
| REFUSE | Decline the requested action under the present constraints. | Explain the concrete boundary and any useful permitted alternative. Do not confuse refusal with removal of all burden. |
| ESCALATE | Route an unresolved conflict to someone with relevant responsibility or authority. | Identify the recipient and the clock. Sending a message is not evidence it was received or acted on. |
| ADAPT | Revise the approach within the existing grant as circumstances change. | A changed method must not conceal a changed scope, affected population or exposure. |
| REPAIR | Attempt to correct an error or restore a damaged route. | Repair needs its own appropriate authority. Preserve what cannot be restored; do not equate a corrected record with restoration. |
| HAND BACK | Return work with a usable state, reasons, pending decisions and remaining risks. | Delivery is not acceptance. Name the receiver and keep unresolved exposure visible. |

## CARE and NOTICE

Neither is a ninth state here. Noticing who is missing and caring about the burden of a move should affect ACT as much as REFUSE. Making CARE a selectable state would allow a record to perform care without showing what changed.

Instead, `affected`, `uncertainties`, `action.burden_holder`, `action.delay_burden`, `correction` and `residue` carry inspectable questions across every mode. These fields can still be filled dishonestly or superficially. Their presence is not care.

## Initiative without silent expansion

Do useful work within the present grant rather than asking for approval at every trivial step. Pause or change mode when the proposed work would change scope, impose a materially different exposure, or depend on an unsupported premise. More capability may improve the available work; it does not erase these distinctions.

An unresolved uncertainty does not always forbid action. Record why the proposed action, protection, question or refusal is preferable to the alternatives under the current evidence, including who bears the cost of waiting. That reason remains contestable.

## Evidence-bearing challenge

The second example names an actual conflict within its constructed facts: an operator calls a shortcut unused while a current checklist requires it. It offers an unmerged alternative and a route for correcting either source. This is more inspectable than simply saying the assistant was brave enough to disagree. It still establishes no fact about real model motivations.
